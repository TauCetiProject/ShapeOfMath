1. Vybíral’s Sobolev embedding for variable-exponent Triebel–Lizorkin sequence spaces
   Let s₁≤s₀ and 0<p₀≤p₁ be measurable with 0<p₀⁻≤p₁⁻≤p₁⁺<∞, s₀−n/p₀=s₁−n/p₁ pointwise, and s₀, 1/p₀ satisfying the log-Hölder condition for ∥x−y∥_∞≤1. For q≡∞ or 0<q⁻≤q<∞, f^{s₀(·)}_{p₀(·),q(·)} embeds continuously in f^{s₁(·)}_{p₁(·),q(·)}, where ∥γ∥=∥(∑_{j≥0,m∈ℤⁿ}(2^{js(·)}|γ_{jm}|χ_{Q_{jm}})^{q(·)})^{1/q(·)}∥_{L^{p(·)}} with Q_{jm} the cubes of side 2^{−j}. If inf(s₀−s₁)>0, then f^{s₀(·)}_{p₀(·),∞} embeds in f^{s₁(·)}_{p₁(·),q} for every constant 0<q≤∞.
   Source: introduces, Theorems 3.1–3.2, §3
   Note: Function-space versions are Theorems 3.4 and 3.7 via the φ-transform characterization of Diening–Hästö–Roudenko.
   Origin evidence: Introduction p.530: Diening–Hästö–Roudenko 'have not presented any version of Sobolev embedding'; 'Our aim is to fill this gap.' Theorems 3.1–3.2 state the sequence-space embeddings as the paper's main results; the constant-exponent model is credited to Jawerth (Theorem 1.1).
