1. Positive-semidefinite nullspace obstruction for Gaussian symmetric measurements
   LetA(X)=(tr(A_iX))_(i=1)^m with independentA_i∼GOE(n) in Frobenius normalization andd=n(n+1)/2. For0<ε<1 andm≤(1−ε)d/2, with probability at least1−4exp(−ε²d/32), kerA contains a nonzero positive semidefinite realmatrixΔ. Consequently every noiseless positive-semidefinite feasibility set through anyX≥0 contains the unboundedrayX+tΔ,t≥0.
