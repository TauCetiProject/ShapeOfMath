LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Eisenstein spectral decomposition of automorphic L²
   The automorphic L²-space of a reductive number-field group decomposes as the orthogonal sum over cuspidal parabolic data of direct integrals of normalized parabolically induced unitary representations; Eisenstein series realize the corresponding unitary maps and residual subspaces.
   Source: explains, §VI.2, Theorems VI.2.1–VI.2.2 and proofs
   Note: Complete reworking of Langlands’ proof (LNM 544); original attribution is to Langlands.

2. Pseudo-Eisenstein series
   For a cuspidal datum (M,π) of a reductive group over a global field and a Paley–Wiener section φ of the induced family, the pseudo-Eisenstein series is θ_φ(g)=∑_{γ∈P(k)∖G(k)}φ̂(γg), the Eisenstein series E(φ(λ),λ)(g) integrated over a vertical plane Re λ=λ₀ far inside the positive chamber. It is rapidly decreasing on G(k)∖G(A).
   Source: explains, II.1.10
   Note: Definition and basic properties.

3. Meromorphic continuation and functional equations of Eisenstein series
   For cuspidal automorphic data on a reductive group over a global field, the Eisenstein series E(φ,λ) and the standard intertwining operators M(w,λ) continue meromorphically to all λ, are holomorphic on the unitary axis, and satisfy E(M(w,λ)φ,wλ)=E(φ,λ) and M(w′,wλ)M(w,λ)=M(w′w,λ); M(w,λ) is unitary on the unitary axis (Langlands; Moeglin–Waldspurger Ch. IV).
   Source: explains, Ch. IV, IV.1.8–IV.1.10
   Note: Proof attributed by the authors to Selberg via Jacquet; result due to Langlands.
