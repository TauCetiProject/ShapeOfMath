- Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer leading-term conjecture over a number field: For E/K of Mordell–Weil rank r, the BSD conjecture asserts analytic continuation and analytic rank r, finiteness of Sha(E/K), and lim_(s→1)L(E/K,s)/(s−1)^r=P(E/K)Reg(E/K)|Sha(E/K)|/|E(K)_tors|². Here P is the adelically normalized BSD period and Reg is the relative height-pairing determinant on a torsion-free basis, with torsion kept separate.
- Cauchy’s theorem on an element of prime order: If a prime p divides the order of a finite group G, then G contains an element of order p.
- Chebotarev density of Frobenius conjugacy classes: For a finite Galois extension L/K of number fields, each conjugacy class C⊂Gal(L/K) occurs as the Frobenius class of unramified prime ideals of K with Dirichlet density |C|/|Gal(L/K)|. In particular every finite-quotient conjugacy class occurs at infinitely many primes outside any finite excluded set.
- Cohomology formulas for a finite cyclic group: For G=⟨g⟩ of order n acting on M, put N=1+g+⋯+g^(n−1). Then H¹(G,M)=ker N/(g−1)M and H²(G,M)=M^G/NM. The formulas extend periodically to positive odd and even degrees.
- Conjugation action on a matrix space: For a subgroup G≤GL_n(k) over a field k, the matrix space M_n(k) is a linear representation of G by g·A=gAg⁻¹. The trace-zero subspace is invariant under this action.
- Cup-product period generator for cyclic group cohomology: For a finite cyclic group C of order n, choose an injective character χ:C→ℚ/ℤ and let δχ∈H²(C,ℤ) be its connecting class for0→ℤ→ℚ→ℚ/ℤ→0. Cup product with δχ induces an isomorphism Ĥ⁰(C,M)→H²(C,M) for every C-module M, natural in coefficient and group automorphisms together with the chosen character.
- Cyclic cohomology criterion for elliptic local-global divisibility: Let E be an elliptic curve over a number field F, m>1 and G=Gal(F(E[m])/F). If the cyclic restriction kernel H¹_loc(G,E[m]) is zero, every P∈E(F) divisible by m in E(F_v) at all but finitely many finite places is divisible by m in E(F).
- Cyclotomic determinant of an elliptic torsion representation: For an elliptic curve E over a characteristic-zero field F and a prime p, detρ_(E,p)=ω_p as characters ofGal(Fsep/F) into𝔽_p×.
- Cyclotomic expression for the quadratic character of √5: Over any characteristic-zero field F, the quadratic character κ_5:σ↦σ(√5)/√5, viewed in 𝔽₅×, equals the square ω₅² of the mod5 cyclotomic character.
- Cyclotomic unramified Kummer cohomology at a regular prime: Let p be odd, K=ℚ(ζ_p), Γ=Gal(K/ℚ), and S the infinite places and the unique prime above p. If p∤h_K, then, for the natural Γ-action, H¹_S(K,μ_p)≅𝔽_p(1)⊕⊕_(i=0)^((p−3)/2)𝔽_p(2i), where Γ acts on 𝔽_p(k) by its cyclotomic character to the kth power.
- Dirichlet’s unit theorem: For a number field L with r₁ real places and r₂ pairs of complex places, O_L× is the product of its finite roots-of-unity group and a free abelian group of rank r₁+r₂−1.
- Division polynomials of an elliptic curve: Fix a characteristic-zero Weierstrass equation y²+a₁xy+a₃y=x³+a₂x²+a₄x+a₆. Its division polynomials have ψ₀=0; for odd n≥1, ψ_n=n∏_(P∈(E[n]∖{O})/{±1})(x−x(P)); for even n≥2, ψ_n=(n/2)(2y+a₁x+a₃)∏_(P∈(E[n]∖E[2])/{±1})(x−x(P)). The products give polynomials over the base field; their zeros encode the indicated torsion points in these fixed coordinates.
- Dvornicich–Zannier counterexample to local-global divisibility by four: There exist an elliptic curve E/ℚ and an infinite-order P∈E(ℚ) which lies in4E(ℚ_v) at every finite place except possibly2, but does not lie in4E(ℚ).
- Faltings’s finiteness theorem for rational points: If C is a smooth projective geometrically connected curve of genus at least two over a number field K, then C(K) is finite.
- Finite generation of the S-unit group: For a number field K and finite S containing the infinite places, the S-unit group is isomorphic to μ(K)×ℤʳ for a finite integer r, where μ(K) is the finite group of roots of unity in K.
- Frobenius traces on elliptic torsion at a good prime: Let E/ℚ have good reduction at a rational prime ℓ and write a_ℓ=ℓ+1−#Ẽ(𝔽_ℓ). For any prime p≠ℓ and r≥1, the characteristic polynomial of arithmetic Frobenius at ℓ acting on E[p^r] is T²−a_ℓT+ℓ modulo p^r. The corresponding polynomial on T_pE is T²−a_ℓT+ℓ.
- Full finite congruence images for a non-CM rational p-isogeny at p≥7: Let E/ℚ be non-CM, p≥7 prime, and suppose E has a ℚ-rational isogeny of degreep. For every i≥2, the image of Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ) on E[pⁱ] is the full inverse image of its image on E[p] in GL₂(ℤ/pⁱℤ).
- Genus of a prime-level modular curve X₀(p): For a prime p>3, the genus of X₀(p) is n−1 if p=12n+1, n if p=12n+5 or p=12n+7, and n+1 if p=12n+11. In particular X₀(11) and X₀(17) have genus one, and for p>17 with p≡2 mod3 it has genus at least two.
- Geometric automorphism group of an elliptic curve in characteristic zero: For an elliptic curve E in characteristic zero, its origin-preserving automorphism group over an algebraic closure is cyclic of order2 if j(E)∉{0,1728}, order4 if j(E)=1728, and order6 if j(E)=0. In a short Weierstrass model it consists of (x,y)↦(u²x,u³y) with u⁴A=A and u⁶B=B.
- Greenberg’s 5-adic image index for a rational 5-isogeny: Let E/ℚ be a non-CM elliptic curve with a ℚ-rational isogeny of degree5, and let I=[GL₂(ℤ₅):imρ_(E,5∞)]. Then v₅(I)=0 if no curve in the ℚ-isogeny class of E has two distinct ℚ-rational cyclic subgroups of order5, and v₅(I)=1 otherwise.
- Group of equivariant homomorphisms: For abelian G-modules M,N, Hom_G(M,N) is the abelian group of additive maps f:M→N satisfying f(gm)=g f(m), with pointwise addition. If M,N are 𝔽_p-vector spaces and the G-actions are linear, every additive map is 𝔽_p-linear, so Hom_G(M,N) is an 𝔽_p-vector space.
- Hochschild–Serre spectral sequence in group cohomology: For a normal subgroup N of G and a G-module M, there is a first-quadrant spectral sequence E₂^(a,b)=Hᵃ(G/N,Hᵇ(N,M)) converging to H^(a+b)(G,M). The quotient action is induced on cochains by(g·c)(n₁,…,n_b)=g·c(g⁻¹n₁g,…,g⁻¹n_bg).
- Inflation–restriction sequence in first group cohomology: For a normal subgroup N of a group Γ acting on an abelian group M, the sequence0→H¹(Γ/N,M^N)→H¹(Γ,M)→H¹(N,M)^(Γ/N) is exact. The last action is quotient conjugation. For continuous cohomology, Γ is profinite, N is closed normal, and M is a discrete continuous Γ-module.
- Injective restriction to a subgroup of index prime to the coefficient characteristic: Let p be prime, G finite and M a G-module killed by p. If H≤G has index coprime to p, then Res:Hⁿ(G,M)→Hⁿ(H,M) is injective for all n>0. In particular this applies to a p-Sylow subgroup.
- Integers and ramification in a prime-power cyclotomic field: Let p be prime, r≥1 and ζ a primitive p^rth root of unity. For K=ℚ(ζ), [K:ℚ]=p^(r−1)(p−1), O_K=ℤ[ζ], and λ=1−ζ generates the unique prime above p, with (p)=(λ)^(p^(r−1)(p−1)) and O_K/(λ)≅𝔽_p. Every prime other than p is unramified in K.
- Kolyvagin bound from a Heegner point and surjective torsion image: Let E/ℚ be geometrically non-CM and let K be an imaginary quadratic field distinct from ℚ(i) and ℚ(√−3), satisfying the Heegner hypothesis for E. Let y_K∈E(K) be a nontorsion Heegner point with finite index I_K=[E(K):ℤy_K]. If p≥5 and ρ_(E,p):G_ℚ→GL₂(𝔽_p) is surjective, then Sha(E/K)[p^∞] is finite and v_p(#Sha(E/K)[p^∞])≤2v_p(I_K).
- Lagrange’s theorem for a finite group: For a subgroup H of a finite group G, |G|=[G:H]|H|. In particular |H| divides |G|.
- Lifting a full congruence image from p² to all finite levels: Let p be odd, n≥1 and H≤GL_n(ℤ_p). Write H_i for its image modulo pⁱ. If H₂ is the entire inverse image of H₁ under reduction modulo p, then H_i is the entire inverse image of H₁ for every i≥2. No closedness assumption is needed for this finite-level assertion.
- Linear disjointness of rational Galois extensions with disjoint ramification: Let L/ℚ and M/ℚ be finite Galois extensions in a common algebraic closure. If no finite rational prime ramifies in both extensions, then L∩M=ℚ, L and M are linearly disjoint over ℚ, and restriction identifies Gal(LM/M) with Gal(L/ℚ).
- Local-global divisibility by a prime on a rational elliptic curve: For every elliptic curve E/ℚ and prime p, a rational point P∈E(ℚ) which belongs to pE(ℚ_ℓ) for all but finitely many rational primes ℓ already belongs to pE(ℚ).
- Maschke splitting in characteristic prime to the group order: If a finite group G acts linearly on a finite-dimensional vector space V over k and|G|is nonzero in k, then every G-invariant subspace W has a G-invariant direct complement.
- Mazur’s theorem on rational elliptic torsion: For an elliptic curve E/ℚ, its torsion subgroup E(ℚ)_tors is isomorphic either to ℤ/nℤ with 1≤n≤10 or n=12, or to ℤ/2ℤ×ℤ/(2m)ℤ with 1≤m≤4. Each of these fifteen groups occurs.
- Mazur’s theorem on rational isogenies of prime degree: If an elliptic curve over ℚ admits an isogeny over ℚ of prime degree p, then p∈{2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,37,43,67,163}. Conversely each listed prime occurs for some elliptic curve over ℚ.
- Multiplicative Kummer isomorphism in Galois cohomology: For a number field K and an integer n≥1, the connecting map gives K×/(K×)^n≅H¹(G_K,μ_n), where μ_n is the group of nth roots of unity in a separable closure. The class of a maps to the cocycle σ↦σ(α)/α for any α with α^n=a.
- Néron–Ogg–Shafarevich criterion: For an abelian variety A over a nonarchimedean local field K and a prime ℓ different from the residue characteristic, A has good reduction if and only if inertia acts trivially on T_ℓA. If this holds for one such ℓ it holds for every such ℓ.
- Order of a general linear group over a finite field: For a finite field𝔽_q, |GL_n(𝔽_q)|=∏_(j=0)^(n−1)(qⁿ−qʲ). In particular the order of GL₂(𝔽_p) is p(p−1)²(p+1), whose p-part is p.
- Prime-field GL₂ subgroup dichotomy at the coefficient prime: If G≤GL₂(𝔽_p), p prime, and p divides|G|, then either G contains SL₂(𝔽_p), or G stabilizes a line and is conjugate into the upper triangular subgroup. The field is specifically the prime field.
- Prime-to-residue-characteristic divisibility under elliptic good reduction: Let K be a characteristic-zero nonarchimedean local field with residue field k of characteristic ℓ, and let E/K have good reduction, and let n≥1 be coprime to ℓ. - Rational cyclic subgroup of an elliptic curve: For an elliptic curve E over a characteristic-zero field F, a rational cyclic subgroup of order n is a cyclic subgroup C≤E[n](F̄) of order n stable under Gal(F̄/F). - S-unit Kummer exact sequence: Let K be a number field, p an odd prime, and S a finite set of places containing the infinite places and every place above p. - Successive congruence kernel for general linear groups: For a prime p, n≥1 and i≥2, the kernel of GL_n(ℤ/pⁱℤ)→GL_n(ℤ/p^(i−1)ℤ) is isomorphic to the additive group M_n(𝔽_p) by I+p^(i−1)A↦A modp. Conjugation corresponds to conjugation by the reduced matrix in GL_n(𝔽_p).
- Surjectivity of the cyclotomic character under disjointness: For a number field F with F∩ℚ(ζ_p)=ℚ, the mod-p cyclotomic character ω_p of F is surjective onto𝔽_p×.
- Sylow p-subgroup of a finite group: If p^a is the largest power of a prime p dividing the order of a finite group G, a Sylow p-subgroup is a subgroup of order p^a. Its index is prime to p.
- Unramified elliptic division fields at good primes: Let E be an elliptic curve over a number field K, p a prime and r≥1. At a finite place v of residue characteristic different from p where E has good reduction, K(E[p^r])/K is unramified.

- Vanishing of finite-group cohomology when the group order is invertible: For a finite group G and a G-module M on which multiplication by |G| is invertible, Hⁿ(G,M)=0 for every n>0.
- Weil pairing on elliptic-curve torsion: For an elliptic curve E in characteristic zero, the Weil pairing e_n:E[n]×E[n]→μ_n is a nondegenerate alternating bilinear pairing, equivariant under Galois. Consequently the determinant of ρ_(E,n) is the cyclotomic character modulo n.

LOCATED CONCEPTS (94)

1. Galois representation on elliptic-curve torsion
   For an elliptic curve E/F in characteristic zero, the natural action on E[n] gives ρ_(E,n):Gal(F̄/F)→Aut(E[n])≅GL₂(ℤ/nℤ) after choosing a basis; its kernel fixes the division field F(E[n]).
   Source: relies_on, §§1–2
   Note: The finite image on torsion defines all G_i.

2. Isogeny of elliptic curves in characteristic zero
   An isogeny φ:E→E′ over F is a nonconstant algebraic group homomorphism of elliptic curves preserving O. Its degree equals the order of its finite geometric kernel in characteristic zero. A cyclic isogeny has a cyclic kernel.
   Source: relies_on, §§1–3,6
   Note: Galois-stable torsion lines correspond to rational cyclic isogenies.

3. Dual isogeny of an elliptic-curve isogeny
   For an isogeny φ:E→E′ of degree n, the dual isogeny φ̂:E′→E satisfies φ̂φ=[n] on E and φφ̂=[n] on E′.
   Source: relies_on, §§2,4–6
   Note: The character on the dual kernel controls H².

4. Quadratic twist of an elliptic curve
   For E:y²=x³+Ax+B over a characteristic-zero field F and D∈F×/(F×)², the quadratic twist E^D is y²=x³+D²Ax+D³B. It becomes isomorphic to E over F(√D), with Galois descent changed by the associated quadratic character.
   Source: relies_on, Theorems 1–2; Lemmas 10,23
   Note: Exceptional image characters are expressed by twisting.

5. Cohomology of a group with abelian coefficients
   For a G-module M, Hⁿ(G,M)=ker(d:Cⁿ→C^(n+1))/im(d:C^(n−1)→Cⁿ), where Cⁿ consists of maps Gⁿ→M and (df)(g₁,…,g_(n+1))=g₁f(g₂,…)+∑_(i=1)^n(−1)^if(…,g_ig_(i+1),…)+(−1)^(n+1)f(g₁,…,g_n). For a profinite G and a discrete M, use continuous cochains.
   Source: relies_on, §§2–8
   Note: H¹,H², connecting maps and cohomological exactness.

6. Inflation–restriction sequence in first group cohomology
   For a normal subgroup N of a group Γ acting on an abelian group M, the sequence0→H¹(Γ/N,M^N)→H¹(Γ,M)→H¹(N,M)^(Γ/N) is exact. The last action is quotient conjugation. For continuous cohomology, Γ is profinite, N is closed normal, and M is a discrete continuous Γ-module.
   Source: relies_on, §2 equation (1); §§3–8
   Note: Restricts to congruence kernels and cyclic p-subgroups.

7. Long exact sequence in cohomology of a short exact module sequence
   A short exact sequence of modules for a group induces the connecting maps and exact cohomology sequence beginning0→A^Γ→B^Γ→C^Γ→H¹(Γ,A)→H¹(Γ,B)→H¹(Γ,C)→H²(Γ,A)→⋯.
   Source: relies_on, §2 equation (2); §§6,8
   Note: Passes between p-torsion and p-power torsion.

8. Cohomology formulas for a finite cyclic group
   For G=⟨g⟩ of order n acting on M, put N=1+g+⋯+g^(n−1). Then H¹(G,M)=ker N/(g−1)M and H²(G,M)=M^G/NM. The formulas extend periodically to positive odd and even degrees.
   Source: relies_on, Lemmas 6,7,13
   Note: Explicit degree-one and degree-two calculations.

9. Vanishing of finite-group cohomology when the group order is invertible
   For a finite group G and a G-module M on which multiplication by |G| is invertible, Hⁿ(G,M)=0 for every n>0.
   Source: relies_on, Lemmas 3,6,8,13
   Note: Eliminates coprime-order terms.

10. Weil pairing on elliptic-curve torsion
   For an elliptic curve E in characteristic zero, the Weil pairing e_n:E[n]×E[n]→μ_n is a nondegenerate alternating bilinear pairing, equivariant under Galois. Consequently the determinant of ρ_(E,n) is the cyclotomic character modulo n.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma 4
   Note: Surjectivity of the determinant under F∩ℚ(μ_p)=ℚ.

11. Complex multiplication of an elliptic curve
   An elliptic curve E in characteristic zero has complex multiplication if End_(F̄)(E) strictly contains ℤ; the extra endomorphisms form an order in an imaginary quadratic field.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma 23
   Note: Conductor-49 exceptional image calculation.

12. Cyclic restriction kernel in first group cohomology
   For a finite group G and a G-module M, H¹_loc(G,M)=ker(H¹(G,M)→∏_(C≤G cyclic)H¹(C,M)). It records cohomology classes whose restrictions to every cyclic subgroup vanish.
   Source: relies_on, §8 equation (7)
   Note: Connects finite image cohomology with divisibility.

13. Local-global divisibility problem for elliptic-curve points
   For an elliptic curve E over a number field F and an integer m>1, the local-global divisibility question asks whether a point P∈E(F) divisible by m in E(F_v) for all but finitely many finite places v must already be divisible by m in E(F).
   Source: relies_on, §8
   Note: Application formulated by Dvornicich–Zannier [9].

In every exceptional case the cohomology group has p elements.

ADDITIONAL PREREQUISITES (333)

- Abelian category [definition]: An additive category with kernels and cokernels in which every monomorphism is a kernel and every epimorphism is a cokernel.
- Abelian group [definition]: A group whose operation is commutative.
- Abelian group with a group action [definition]: A G-module is an abelian group M equipped with an action of G by abelian-group automorphisms. A morphism is a group homomorphism commuting with the G-actions.
- Abelian scheme [definition]: An abelian scheme over S is a proper smooth group scheme over S whose geometric fibers are connected. Each geometric fiber is an abelian variety.
- Abelian variety [definition]: An abelian variety over a field k is a smooth proper geometrically connected commutative group scheme over k. Geometric connectedness means connectedness after base change to an algebraic closure; its group law and identity are defined over k.
- Absolute Galois group [definition]: The group Gal(F^sep/F), with its Krull topology, where F^sep is a separable closure.
- Absolute norm of a fractional ideal [definition]: For a nonzero integral ideal I⊆O_K, N(I)=|O_K/I|. Extend this multiplicatively to nonzero fractional ideals by N(I/J)=N(I)/N(J); for a principal ideal (a), this equals |N_(K/ℚ)(a)|.
- Absolute value on a field [definition]: A map |·|:K→R_{≥0} that vanishes exactly at zero, is multiplicative and satisfies |x+y|≤|x|+|y|. It is nonarchimedean if the stronger bound |x+y|≤max(|x|,|y|) holds.
- Additive category [definition]: A category with abelian-group morphism sets, composition additive in each variable, a zero object and finite biproducts.
- Additive group of rational numbers modulo integers [definition]: The group ℚ/ℤ is the quotient of the additive rational group by its integer subgroup; each element is a rational residue class and has finite order. In group cohomology it is given the stated group action, often trivial.
- Adele ring of a number field [definition]: The restricted product A_F=∏′_v F_v of all completions of a number field,where almost every finite component lies in the valuation ring O_v.
- Affine scheme [construction]: The affine scheme Spec R is the prime spectrum of R with the structure sheaf characterized on standard opens by O(D(f))=R_f and the localization restriction maps. Its stalk at 𝔭 is R_𝔭. An affine scheme is any locally ringed space isomorphic to one of these.
- Algebraic closure of a field [definition]: An algebraic extension of a field that is itself algebraically closed. This specifies an object, without including the existence or uniqueness-up-to-isomorphism theorem.
- Algebraic element of a field extension [definition]: An element α of L/F is algebraic over F if a nonzero polynomial in F[x] vanishes at α.
- Algebraically closed field [definition]: A field in which every nonconstant polynomial has a root.
- Alternating group of a finite set [definition]: For an n-element set, the alternating group A_n is the subgroup of its symmetric group consisting of the permutations expressible as a product of an even number of transpositions. The parity of such an expression is independent of the expression.
- Arithmetic modular curve X₀(N) [construction]: Over ℚ, Y₀(N) is the coarse moduli curve of elliptic curves with a cyclic subgroup scheme of order N; X₀(N) is its smooth projective compactification. On complex points τ corresponds to ℂ/(ℤ+τℤ) with subgroup generated by1/N, giving Γ₀(N)\ℍ. The arithmetic curve and its moduli convention are distinguished from the bare complex quotient.
- Arithmetic modular curve with µ_N level structure [construction]: For N≥4 over ℚ, Y₁^µ(N) represents isomorphism classes of elliptic-curve families with a closed group-scheme embedding µ_N↪E[N]; X₁^µ(N) is its smooth projective compactification. Its complex point τ corresponds to E_τ=ℂ/(ℤ+τℤ), with ζ_N↦1/N. This fixes the arithmetic model and complex identification: its cusp∞ is ℚ-rational and its local parameter is q=exp(2πiτ).
- Artin and Swan conductor of a local ℓ-adic representation [construction]: For a local ℓ-adic representation V, its Swan exponent is Sw(V)=∫₀^∞codim_F V^(G_K^u)du, where G_K^u are the positive upper ramification groups. The Artin conductor exponent is a(V)=codim_F V^I_K+Sw(V); its conductor ideal is 𝔭_K^(a(V)). These exponents are nonnegative integers in this setting.
- Associative algebra over a field [definition]: A vector space with an associative bilinear multiplication and a multiplicative identity.
- Automorphism of a group [definition]: A bijective group homomorphism from a group to itself; automorphisms form a group under composition.
- BSD period of an elliptic curve over a number field [construction]: For E/K choose a nonzero invariant differential ω and additive Haar measures dx_v whose product on𝔄_K gives vol(𝔄_K/K)=1. Almost all finite factors are1; the product formula makes the result independent of ω, and the adelic normalization fixes its discriminant factor.
- Base change and fibers of a scheme [construction]: For X→S and S′→S, the base change is X_(S′)=X×_S S′ with its projection to S′. The fiber at s∈S is X_s=X×_S Spec κ(s), where κ(s)=O_(S,s)/𝔪_s is the residue field of the local ring. A geometric fiber extends this residue field to an algebraic closure.
- Basis of a free module [definition]: A family (e_i) in a module over a commutative ring such that every element is a unique finite linear combination of the e_i with coefficients in that ring. A module with such a family is free.
- Basis of a vector space [definition]: A linearly independent family of vectors whose linear span is the whole vector space.
- Bijection between sets [definition]: A function for which each element of the codomain is the image of exactly one element of the domain.
- Bilinear map [definition]: A map of two vector-space variables that is linear in each variable separately.
- Binary operation on a set [definition]: A function S×S→S.

- Dirichlet density of a set of prime ideals [definition]: For a set T of nonzero prime ideals in a number field K, its Dirichlet density is lim_(s→1+) (∑_(p∈T)N(p)^(−s))/log(1/(s−1)), when this limit exists. Changing finitely many primes does not change this density.
- Dirichlet series [definition]: A Dirichlet series is ∑_(n≥1)a_n n^(−s), with complex coefficients and n^(−s)=exp(−s log n), considered on a right half-plane where it converges.
- Discrete module for a topological group [definition]: A discrete module for a topological group G is an abelian G-module M with the discrete topology and continuous action G×M→M. Equivalently each element of M has an open stabilizer in G.
- Discrete topological space [definition]: A topological space is discrete if every subset is open.
- Discrete valuation on a field [definition]: A surjective map v:K×→Z satisfying v(xy)=v(x)+v(y) and v(x+y)≥min(v(x),v(y)), extended by v(0)=+∞.
- Disjoint sets [definition]: Two sets are disjoint when they have no common element; a family is pairwise disjoint when every two distinct members are disjoint.
- Divisibility of integers [definition]: An integer a divides b if b=ac for some integer c.
- Division field of an elliptic curve [definition]: The n-division field F(E[n]) is the extension of F generated by the affine coordinates of all n-torsion points of an elliptic curve E/F. It is finite Galois, and its Galois group acts faithfully on E[n].
- Eigenvalue of a linear endomorphism [definition]: A scalar λ for which a linear endomorphism T has a nonzero vector v with Tv=λv.
- Element integral over a commutative ring [definition]: For a ring inclusion R⊂A, an element a∈A is integral over R if it satisfies a monic polynomial with coefficients in R.
- Elliptic curve over a characteristic-zero field [definition]: Over a characteristic-zero field F, an elliptic curve can be presented as a nonsingular projective cubic y²z=x³+Axz²+Bz³ with A,B∈F and 4A³+27B²≠0, with distinguished point O=[0:1:0] and the chord-and-tangent abelian group law. Curves are identified by F-isomorphisms preserving O.
- Elliptic curve over a finite field [definition]: An elliptic curve over a finite field k is a one-dimensional abelian variety over k, with its identity as distinguished point. Its k-rational points form the finite abelian group E(k).
- Epimorphism in a category [definition]: A morphism f:X→Y of a category is an epimorphism if it is right cancellable: a∘f=b∘f implies a=b for every object W and all morphisms a,b:Y→W.
- Equivalence class [definition]: For an equivalence relation ~ on X, [x]={y∈X:y~x}; the quotient set X/~ consists of these classes.
- Equivalence relation [definition]: A reflexive, symmetric and transitive binary relation.
- Euclidean space ℝᵈ [definition]: For d≥1, ℝᵈ consists of d-tuples of real numbers with distance dist(x,y)=(∑ᵢ(xᵢ−yᵢ)²)¹ᐟ².
- Exact sequence of modules [definition]: A sequence of homomorphisms of modules over one commutative ring is exact at a module if the image of the incoming map equals the kernel of the outgoing map; it is exact if this holds at every term.
- Extended nonnegative real numbers [definition]: The nonnegative real numbers together with a greatest element ∞. Sums involving ∞ equal ∞, and 0·∞ is taken to be 0 when integrating simple functions.
- Factorial [definition]: For n≥1, n!=1·2···n, and 0!=1.
- Family of elliptic curves [definition]: An elliptic curve over a scheme S is an abelian scheme E→S whose geometric fibers have dimension one. In particular it has a specified identity section, and its group law is part of the family; pullback gives a family over every S′→S.
- Fiber product of schemes [construction]: For X→S and Y→S, X×_S Y is the scheme with projection morphisms whose composites to S agree, universal for pairs of morphisms T→X,T→Y over S. For affine schemes Spec A,Spec B over Spec R it is Spec(A⊗_R B).
- Field [definition]: A commutative ring in which 1≠0 and every nonzero element is invertible.
- Field embedding [definition]: An injective homomorphism of fields preserving the multiplicative identity.
- Field extension [definition]: An inclusion of one field into another.
- Field of p-adic numbers [definition]: For a prime p, ℚ_p is the completion of ℚ for the p-adic absolute value |x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}.
- Finite Galois field extension [definition]: A finite Galois extension L/k is a field extension of finite vector-space dimension which is both normal and separable. Its automorphisms fixing k form the finite Galois group Gal(L/k).
- Finite biproduct in a category [definition]: An object with injections and projections making it simultaneously the product and coproduct of a finite family of objects, with the usual compatible identity and zero composites.
- Finite field [definition]: A field whose underlying set is finite.
- Finite set [definition]: A set X is finite if it is bijective with {1,…,n} for some natural number n≥0; its cardinality is n.
- Finite unramified extension of a nonarchimedean local field [definition]: A finite extension of a complete discretely valued field whose ramification index is one and whose residue-field extension is separable.
- Finite-dimensional vector space [definition]: A vector space having a finite basis.
- Finitely generated commutative algebra [definition]: An R-algebra A generated as an algebra by finitely many elements; equivalently a quotient of R[X₁,…,X_n] for some finite n.
- Finitely presented commutative algebra [definition]: An R-algebra isomorphic to R[X₁,…,X_n]/(f₁,…,f_m) for finite n,m.
- First group cohomology with abelian coefficients [definition]: For a G-module M, H¹(G,M) is the abelian group of cocycles f(gh)=f(g)+g·f(h), modulo coboundaries f(g)=g·m−m. For profinite G and discrete M, the cocycles are continuous.
- First-quadrant cohomological spectral sequence [definition]: A first-quadrant cohomological spectral sequence in an abelian category consists of objects E_r^(a,b) for r≥r₀ and integers a,b, zero unless a,b≥0, with differentials d_r:E_r^(a,b)→E_r^(a+r,b−r+1), d_r∘d_r=0, and isomorphisms E_(r+1)≅ker d_r/im d_r. For fixed (a,b) the terms stabilize to E_∞^(a,b). Convergence to objects H^n means each H^n has a finite descending filtration by subobjects whose graded pieces are E_∞^(a,n−a). Modules over a ring are the basic example.
- Fixed vector subspace of a group representation [definition]: For a representation π on V and a subgroup H, the subspace V^H={v:π(h)v=v for every h∈H}. This means pointwise fixed vectors, not merely an invariant subspace.
- Formal power series over a commutative ring [definition]: An expression ∑a_α X^α with coefficients in a commutative ring and multi-indices α in N^n, added coefficientwise and multiplied by the finite Cauchy sums defining each coefficient, without any assertion of analytic convergence.
- Fraction field of an integral domain [definition]: For an integral domain R, its fraction field Frac(R) consists of fractions a/b with a∈R,b a nonzero element of R, identified by cross multiplication. It is a field containing R and every injective homomorphism from R into a field extends uniquely to a homomorphism from Frac(R).
- Fractional ideal of a number field [definition]: A nonzero fractional ideal of a number field K is a finitely generated O_K-submodule I⊆K with aI⊆O_K for some nonzero a∈O_K. Products and inverses give the group of nonzero fractional ideals.
- Free abelian group [definition]: An abelian group with a family of elements such that every group element is a unique finite integer linear combination of them; the number of basis elements is its rank.
- Frobenius element at a prime of a Galois extension [definition]: The element of D_w/I_w acting on the residue extension by x↦x^(#k_v). At an unramified prime it gives a conjugacy class in Gal(L/F), independently of the chosen w above v.
- Frobenius endomorphism in characteristic p [definition]: The field endomorphism x↦x^p of a field of characteristic p; over F_q the q-power iterate x↦x^q is the corresponding q-Frobenius.
- Full lattice in Euclidean space [definition]: A subgroup Λ⊂ℝⁿ generated overℤ by a real vector-space basis ofℝⁿ.
- Function between sets [definition]: An assignment taking each element of a specified domain set to exactly one element of a specified codomain set.
- Functor [definition]: A map of categories preserving objects, morphisms, identities and composition.
- Galois field extension [definition]: An algebraic field extension that is both normal and separable.
- Galois group of a finite Galois extension [definition]: The group of field automorphisms of the extension that fix the base field pointwise.
- Galois group of a prime cyclotomic field [theorem]: For a prime p and primitive pth root ζ_p, Gal(ℚ(ζ_p)/ℚ) is isomorphic to𝔽_p× by σ↦a when σ(ζ_p)=ζ_p^a.
- Galois trace of a point on an elliptic curve [definition]: For a finite Galois extensionL/K and an elliptic curveE/K, the point trace is Tr_(L/K)(P)=∑_(σ∈Gal(L/K))σP∈E(K) forP∈E(L), with addition inE. It is a group homomorphism and is distinct from the field trace of point coordinates.
- General linear group over a commutative ring [definition]: The group GL_n(R) of square matrices over a commutative ring R having a two-sided matrix inverse, with matrix multiplication.
- Genus of a smooth projective curve [definition]: For a smooth projective geometrically connected curve C over a field k, its genus is g(C)=dim_k H⁰(C,Ω¹_(C/k)), the dimension of the space of global regular differential one-forms. This is the geometric genus convention.
- Good reduction of an abelian variety [definition]: An abelian variety over the fraction field K of a discrete valuation ring R has good reduction if it extends to an abelian scheme over R. Equivalently its Néron model is proper over R; for elliptic curves this agrees with smooth reduction of a minimal Weierstrass model.
- Group [definition]: A set with an associative binary operation, an identity element, and a two-sided inverse for each element.
- Group action on a set [definition]: A map G×X→X, written (g,x)↦gx, satisfying ex=x and (gh)x=g(hx).
- Group homomorphism [definition]: A map f:G→H satisfying f(xy)=f(x)f(y) for all x,y.
- Group scheme [definition]: A group scheme G over S is an S-scheme with multiplication G×_S G→G, an identity section S→G and inverse G→G satisfying the group axioms as identities of S-morphisms. It need not be affine, connected or proper.
- Group scheme of roots of unity [construction]: Over a ring R, µ_N is the group scheme Spec R[t]/(t^N−1), with multiplication induced by t↦t⊗t, identity t↦1 and inverse t↦t^(N−1). Its points over an R-algebra are the units whose Nth power is1.
- Haar measure on a locally compact group [definition]: A nonzero Radon measure on a locally compact group, i.e. a Borel measure finite on compact sets, outer regular on Borel sets and inner regular on open sets, invariant under left translation: μ(gA)=μ(A). A right Haar measure is defined analogously with right translations.
- Hasse–Weil L-function of an elliptic curve over a number field [definition]: For E/K, its Hasse–Weil L-function is ∏_𝔭 P_𝔭((N𝔭)^(−s))^(−1). At good reduction, P_𝔭(T)=1−a_𝔭T+(N𝔭)T² with a_𝔭=N𝔭+1−|E(k_𝔭)|. At split multiplicative, nonsplit multiplicative and additive reduction the factors P_𝔭 are 1−T, 1+T and 1 respectively. The product converges for Re s>3/2.
- Hausdorff topological space [definition]: A topological space in which any two distinct points have disjoint open neighbourhoods.
- Heegner CM point on X₀(N) [definition]: LetK⊂ℂ be imaginary quadratic with ringO_K and let𝔫⊂O_K satisfyO_K/𝔫≅ℤ/Nℤ. The cyclicN-isogenyℂ/O_K→ℂ/𝔫⁻¹ defines a Heegner CM pointx onX₀(N)(ℂ); equivalently both endpoint elliptic curves have complex multiplication byO_K. The ideal and embedding are part of the chosen construction.
- Hilbert class field of a number field [definition]: The Hilbert class fieldH of a number fieldK is its maximal abelian extension unramified at every finite prime and at infinity: every real place remains real. For an imaginary quadraticK the infinite-place condition is vacuous.
- Holomorphic function of one complex variable [definition]: A function on an open subset of ℂ is holomorphic if its complex derivative exists at every point of that subset.
- Holomorphic function of several complex variables [definition]: A complex function on an open subset of C^n that is locally representable by a convergent power series in the n complex coordinates. Vector-valued holomorphy for a finite-dimensional target means holomorphy of its coordinates in any basis.
- Homeomorphism [definition]: A bijection between topological spaces that is continuous and has a continuous inverse.
- Homomorphism of commutative rings [definition]: A map between commutative rings preserving addition, multiplication and the multiplicative identity.
- Homomorphism of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive map f:M→N of modules over the same commutative ring satisfying f(rm)=rf(m).
- Ideal class group of a number field [definition]: For a number field K, its ideal class group Cl(K) is the group of nonzero fractional ideals of O_K modulo principal fractional ideals (a), a∈K×. Its cardinality h_K is called the class number.
- Ideal of a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup I of a commutative ring R such that ri belongs to I for every r in R and i in I.
- Identity component and component group of a Néron special fiber [definition]: For a Néron model 𝒜 over a discrete valuation ring with residue field k, the geometric identity component 𝒜⁰_(k̄) is the connected component containing the identity in its geometric special fiber. The geometric component group Φ(k̄)=𝒜_k(k̄)/𝒜⁰_k(k̄) is the finite group of connected components, with its natural Galois action. The open subgroup 𝒜⁰ has the whole generic fiber and only the identity component of the special fiber.
- Imaginary quadratic number field [definition]: An imaginary quadratic number field is a degree-two extension ofℚ with no embedding intoℝ, equivalently K=ℚ(√D) for a negative square-free integerD. A chosen embeddingK⊂ℂ fixes its complex realization.
- Index of a subgroup [definition]: For a subgroup H≤G, the index [G:H] is the cardinality of the set of left cosets gH. Left and right cosets have the same cardinality by inversion.
- Indicator function of a subset [definition]: For A⊆Ω, the real function 1_A is 1 on A and 0 on its complement.
- Inertia group at a prime of a Galois extension [definition]: The subgroup I_w of the decomposition group consisting of automorphisms acting trivially on the residue field at w.
- Integer [definition]: An integer is an element of the ordered ring ℤ, obtained by adjoining additive inverses to the natural numbers.
- Integral domain [definition]: An integral domain is a nonzero commutative ring R in which ab=0 implies a=0 or b=0.
- Intersection of a family of sets [definition]: Inside a specified ambient set, the elements belonging to every member of the family.
- Invariant differential of an elliptic curve [definition]: A nonzero invariant differential on an elliptic curve is a regular one-form unchanged by translations. For a generalized Weierstrass equation y²+a₁xy+a₃y=x³+a₂x²+a₄x+a₆, it is a scalar multiple of dx/(2y+a₁x+a₃); in short form it is a multiple of dx/(2y).
- Invariant submodule of a group action [definition]: If a group G acts by R-linear automorphisms on an R-module M, its invariant submodule is M^G={m∈M:g·m=m for every g∈G}.
- Inverse limit of groups [definition]: For groups G_n and homomorphisms f_n:G_(n+1)→G_n, the inverse limit is the subgroup of ∏G_n consisting of tuples (x_n) satisfying f_n(x_(n+1))=x_n for every n; for topological groups it carries the subspace topology of the product.
- Irreducible polynomial [definition]: A nonzero nonunit polynomial over a specified coefficient ring that cannot be written as a product of two nonunits. Over a field, both factors would have smaller positive degree.
- Irreducible topological space [definition]: A topological space is irreducible if it is nonempty and cannot be written as a union of two proper closed subsets. Equivalently any two nonempty open subsets intersect.
- Isomorphism in a category [definition]: A morphism f:X→Y having an inverse g:Y→X, so gf=id_X and fg=id_Y.
- Kernel of a module homomorphism [definition]: For an R-module homomorphism f:M→N, its kernel is the submodule {x∈M:f(x)=0}.
- Kernel of a morphism in a category with a zero object [definition]: For a morphism f:X→Y in a category with a zero object, a kernel of f is a morphism k:K→X with f∘k=0 such that every g:W→X with f∘g=0 factors as g=k∘h for a unique h:W→K.
- Krull dimension of a topological space [definition]: The Krull dimension of a topological space is the supremum of the lengths r of chains Z₀⊊Z₁⊊⋯⊊Z_r of nonempty irreducible closed subsets. The dimension of a scheme is the dimension of its underlying space.
- Krull topology on an infinite Galois group [definition]: On Gal(L/F), the subgroup fixing each finite intermediate extension E/F is an open neighbourhood of the identity; their cosets form a basis. For an absolute Galois group take L=F^sep.
- Kähler differentials of an algebra [definition]: For a commutative F-algebra A, the A-module Ω_(A/F) generated by symbols da, a∈A, subject to additivity, F-linearity and d(ab)=a·db+b·da.
- L-adic cyclotomic character [definition]: The ℓ-adic cyclotomic character χ_ℓ of an absolute Galois group is the continuous character to ℤ_ℓ× characterized by σ(ζ)=ζ^(χ_ℓ(σ)) for every root of unity ζ of ℓ-power order.
- Least upper bound in a partially ordered set [definition]: For A⊆P, its least upper bound is an upper bound s satisfying s≤b for every upper bound b of A, when such an s exists.
- Lebesgue integral [definition]: For measurable f≥0, ∫f dμ is the supremum of integrals of nonnegative simple functions below f. For signed f with ∫abs(f)dμ<∞, define ∫f=∫f⁺−∫f⁻.
- Limit of a function between metric spaces [definition]: For f defined near an accumulation point a of a metric space, lim_{x→a}f(x)=L means that for every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that 0<d(x,a)<δ implies d(f(x),L)<ε.
- Limit of a real function at a point [definition]: The relation lim_{x→a}f(x)=L means that for every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that 0<|x−a|<δ in the domain implies |f(x)−L|<ε.
- Limit of a real sequence [definition]: A sequence (aₙ) converges to L∈ℝ if for every ε>0 there is N such that n≥N implies abs(aₙ−L)<ε.
- Linear combination of vectors [definition]: A finite sum a₁v₁+⋯+aₙvₙ of vectors multiplied by scalars from their vector space’s ground field; the empty sum is zero.
- Linear map between vector spaces [definition]: A map between vector spaces over the same field preserving addition and scalar multiplication.
- Linear representation of a group [definition]: A group homomorphism from a group G to the invertible linear maps of a vector space, equivalently a linear action of G.
- Linear span of a set of vectors [definition]: The subspace consisting of all finite linear combinations of a specified set of vectors.
- Linearly disjoint finite field extensions [definition]: Finite extensions L/K and M/K in a common overfield are linearly disjoint over K when [LM:K]=[L:K][M:K], where LM is their compositum. Equivalently a K-basis of L remains linearly independent over M.
- Linearly independent family of vectors [definition]: A family of vectors is linearly independent if every finite linear combination equalling zero has all coefficients zero.
- Local Haar measure induced by an elliptic differential [construction]: For E over a local field K, a nonzero invariant differential ω and a Haar measure dz on(K,+) define a translation-invariant measure |ω| on E(K): in a local analytic coordinate z with ω=a(z)dz, use |a(z)|_K dz. At a complex place the associated real2-dimensional measure is understood, and the chosen additive Haar normalization is part of the data.
- Local Tamagawa number of an elliptic curve [definition]: For E over a nonarchimedean local field K with finite residue field k, let E⁰(K) be the points reducing into the identity component of its Néron special fiber. The local Tamagawa number is c_K=[E(K):E⁰(K)]=|Φ(k)|, where Φ is the component group. It is1 at good-reduction places.
- Local commutative ring [definition]: A local ring is a commutative ring with exactly one maximal ideal. A homomorphism of local rings is local if it sends the maximal ideal of its source into the maximal ideal of its target.
- Local minimal differential of an elliptic curve [definition]: Over a discretely valued local field, a local minimal elliptic differential is dx/(2y+a₁x+a₃) for a minimal integral Weierstrass equation. It is determined up to multiplication by a unit of the valuation ring.
- Local ℓ-adic Galois representation [definition]: For a nonarchimedean local field K of residue characteristic p and a prime ℓ≠p, a local ℓ-adic representation is a continuous linear action of Gal(K̄/K) on a finite-dimensional vector space over a finite extension F/ℚ_ℓ, with its ℓ-adic topology. Its inertia-fixed vectors form V^I_K.
- Localization of a commutative ring [definition]: For a multiplicatively closed subset S of a commutative ring R containing 1, S⁻¹R consists of fractions r/s, identifying r/s with r′/s′ when t(rs′−r′s)=0 for some t in S, with the usual fraction operations.
- Locally compact Hausdorff space [definition]: A Hausdorff topological space in which every point has a neighbourhood with compact closure.
- Locally compact group [definition]: A topological group whose underlying space is locally compact and Hausdorff.
- Locally ringed space [definition]: A locally ringed space is a topological space X with a sheaf of commutative rings O_X whose stalk at each point is a local ring. A morphism consists of a continuous map f:X→Y and compatible ring maps O_Y(U)→O_X(f⁻¹U) whose induced maps on stalks are local.
- Lower ramification groups of a local field extension [construction]: For a finite Galois extension L/K of nonarchimedean local fields, normalize v_L(L×)=ℤ. For real t≥−1 set G_t={σ∈Gal(L/K):v_L(σa−a)≥t+1 for every a∈O_L}. Then G_(−1) is the full Galois group, G₀ is inertia and G₁ is wild inertia.
- Matrix over a ring [definition]: A rectangular array of elements of a ring; addition is entrywise and matrix multiplication uses row-by-column sums.
- Maximal extension unramified outside a finite set of places [definition]: Fix a separable closure of a number field K and a finite set S of places containing the infinite places. The field K_S is the compositum in that closure of all finite extensions of K unramified at each finite place outside S. It is Galois over K, and G_(K,S)=Gal(K_S/K) has its profinite topology.
- Maximal ideal [definition]: A maximal ideal of a commutative ring R is a proper ideal contained in no larger proper ideal.
- Measurable map between measurable spaces [definition]: A function for which the preimage of each measurable subset of the codomain is measurable in the domain.
- Measurable simple real function [definition]: A measurable real function with finite range. It can be written as a finite sum ∑ a_i 1_{A_i} with measurable level sets A_i.
- Measurable space [definition]: A set equipped with a specified σ-algebra of subsets.
- Measure [definition]: A measure μ maps a σ-algebra to [0,∞], sends ∅ to 0, and satisfies μ(⋃ₙAₙ)=∑ₙμ(Aₙ) for every pairwise disjoint sequence of measurable sets.
- Meromorphic continuation [definition]: A meromorphic function on a larger connected complex domain that agrees with a given function on its original open domain.
- Meromorphic function of one complex variable [definition]: A function on a complex domain locally expressible as a quotient of holomorphic functions with denominator not identically zero; zeros of the denominator give poles after common factors cancel.
- Metric space [definition]: A set X with a function d:X×X→[0,∞) such that d(x,y)=0 exactly when x=y, d(x,y)=d(y,x), and d(x,z)≤d(x,y)+d(y,z).
- Minimal integral Weierstrass model over a local field [definition]: For an elliptic curve over a discretely valued characteristic-zero local field, an integral Weierstrass model has a_i in the valuation ring, and is minimal if the valuation of its discriminant is least among all integral Weierstrass equations for the same curve.
- Minimal polynomial of an algebraic element [definition]: The unique monic polynomial of least positive degree over F vanishing at an algebraic element α; it generates the ideal of all polynomials over F vanishing at α.
- Model of an abelian variety over a discrete valuation ring [construction]: For an abelian variety A/K and a discrete valuation ring R with fraction field K, an R-model is a scheme 𝒜→Spec R with a specified isomorphism 𝒜_K≅A. Its generic fiber is the base change to Spec K, and its special fiber is the base change to the residue field of R.
- Modular parametrization of an elliptic curve [definition]: A modular parametrization over K is a nonconstant morphism of algebraic curves π:X₁(N)_K→E for some N; for a rational elliptic curve the source may be X₀(N). Its pullback carries invariant differentials on E to weight-two cusp differentials.
- Module over a commutative ring [definition]: An abelian group M with an action of a commutative ring R satisfying r(m+n)=rm+rn, (r+s)m=rm+sm, (rs)m=r(sm), and 1m=m. Unlike a vector space, its scalar ring need not be a field.
- Modulus of a complex number [definition]: For z=a+bi, its modulus is the nonnegative real number |z|=√(a²+b²).
- Monomorphism in a category [definition]: A morphism f:X→Y of a category is a monomorphism if it is left cancellable: f∘a=f∘b implies a=b for every object W and all morphisms a,b:W→X.
- Morphism of schemes [definition]: A morphism of schemes is a morphism of their locally ringed spaces. On affine opens it is induced contravariantly by a unital homomorphism of commutative rings. An S-scheme is a scheme with a specified morphism to S; morphisms over S commute with these structure maps.
- Morphism of schemes of finite type [definition]: A morphism X→S is locally of finite type if locally on affine source and target charts it comes from a ring map R→A with A generated by finitely many elements as an R-algebra. It is of finite type if it is also quasi-compact: inverse images of quasi-compact open subsets are quasi-compact.
- Multiplicative group of a field [definition]: For a field F, F× is the abelian group of its nonzero elements under multiplication.
- Multiplicity of a polynomial root [definition]: The multiplicity of a root a of a polynomial P is the largest m≥1 for which (x−a)^m divides P. A root of multiplicity1 is simple.
- Natural isomorphism [definition]: A natural transformation whose component at every object is an isomorphism.
- Natural logarithm [definition]: The inverse ln:(0,∞)→ℝ of the real exponential function.
- Natural number [definition]: One of 0, 1, 2, …; natural numbers count finite collections.
- Natural transformation [definition]: A family of maps between the values of two functors, commuting with every morphism in their source category.
- Nilpotent ideal of a commutative ring [definition]: An ideal I for which I^n=0 for some positive integer n, meaning every product of n elements of I is zero.
- Nonarchimedean local field [definition]: A field complete for a nontrivial discrete valuation and having a finite residue field.
- Nonnegative real square root [definition]: For t≥0, √t is the unique real number y≥0 satisfying y²=t.
- Nonsplit Cartan subgroup of GL₂ over a finite field [definition]: Let k₂/k be a quadratic extension of finite fields. Multiplication by k₂× on the two-dimensional k-vector space k₂ embeds it in GL₂(k). A conjugate of this subgroup is a nonsplit Cartan subgroup; it is cyclic of order |k|²−1.
- Norm in a finite field extension [definition]: For a finite field extension L/K, N_(L/K)(a) is the determinant of the K-linear multiplication map x↦ax on L.
- Normal algebraic field extension [definition]: An algebraic field extension L/k is normal if every irreducible polynomial in k[X] with a root in L splits into linear factors over L. Equivalently, the minimal polynomial over k of every element of L splits over L.
- Normal subgroup [definition]: A subgroup H of G satisfying gHg⁻¹=H for every g∈G.
- Normalized local multiplicative norm on a number field [definition]: At a real place use the usual absolute value, at a complex place use the square of the usual complex modulus, and at a finite place v use |x|_v=(#k_v)^(−ord_v(x)), with |0|_v=0. The complex normalization is multiplicative but need not satisfy the ordinary triangle inequality.
- Normalizer of a subgroup [definition]: For a subgroup H of a group G, its normalizer is N_G(H)={g∈G:gHg⁻¹=H}. It is a subgroup of G containing H, and H is normal in N_G(H).
- Number field [definition]: A finite extension of ℚ.
- Néron local volume formula for an elliptic curve [theorem]: Let E/K have a minimal integral Weierstrass model over a nonarchimedean local field with residue size q, let ω_min be a minimal invariant differential and normalize additive Haar measure by vol(O_K)=1. Then ∫_(E(K))|ω_min|=c_K/L_K(E,1), where c_K=[E(K):E⁰(K)] and L_K is the local Hasse–Weil Euler factor.
- Néron model of an abelian variety [definition]: For an abelian variety A/K over the fraction field of a discrete valuation ring R, its Néron model is a smooth separated R-scheme 𝒜 of finite type with generic fiber A such that Hom_R(Z,𝒜)→Hom_K(Z_K,A) is bijective for every smooth R-scheme Z. Its commutative group-scheme structure is the unique extension of that of A.
- Open interval in the real line [definition]: For real a<b, the set (a,b)={x∈ℝ:a<x<b}.
- Order in an imaginary quadratic field [definition]: An order in an imaginary quadratic fieldK is a subringR⊆O_K containing1 and of finite index inO_K. It has the formR=ℤ+cO_K for a unique positive integerc, its conductor.
- Order of vanishing of a holomorphic function [definition]: For f holomorphic near z₀ and not identically zero, ord_(z₀)f is the unique integer r≥0 for which f(z)=(z−z₀)^rg(z) with g holomorphic and g(z₀)≠0. Equivalently r is the first index of a nonzero Taylor coefficient; the identically zero function has order+∞.
- Ordered field [definition]: A field with a total order preserved by addition, in which the product of two positive elements is positive.
- Ordered pair [definition]: A pair (x,y) with distinguished first and second entries; (x,y)=(x′,y′) exactly when x=x′ and y=y′.
- P-adic local field [definition]: A p-adic local field is a finite extension K of ℚ_p, equipped with the uniquely extended p-adic topology and valuation.
- Partial order [definition]: A reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive binary relation, usually written ≤.
- Place of a number field [definition]: An equivalence class of nontrivial absolute values on a number field; its completion is denoted F_v. Archimedean and nonarchimedean places give its infinite and finite places.
- Polynomial over a field [definition]: A formal finite sum a₀+a₁x+⋯+aₙxⁿ with coefficients in a field, with the usual coefficientwise addition and multiplication.
- Polynomial ring over a commutative ring [definition]: The ring R[X₁,…,X_n] of formal finite sums of monomials with coefficients in R, with coefficientwise addition and the usual multiplication of monomials.
- Preimage of a subset under a function [definition]: For f:A→B and E⊆B, f⁻¹(E)={x∈A:f(x)∈E}.
- Prime ideal of a commutative ring [definition]: A proper ideal P of a commutative ring R such that ab in P implies a in P or b in P.
- Prime number [definition]: An integer p>1 is prime if its only positive divisors are 1 and p.
- Prime spectrum and Zariski topology [definition]: Spec R is the set of prime ideals of a commutative ring R with closed sets V(I)={𝔭:I⊆𝔭}. Its standard open subsets are D(f)={𝔭:f∉𝔭}; these form a topology basis.
- Prime-power torsion subgroup of an abelian group [definition]: For an abelian group A and a prime ℓ, A[ℓ^m]={a:ℓ^m a=0}, and A[ℓ∞]=⋃_(m≥0)A[ℓ^m].
- Product formula for a number field [theorem]: For every nonzero a in a number field F, ∏_v|a|_v=1 using normalized local multiplicative norms at every finite and infinite place. Only finitely many factors differ from1.
- Product of a family of objects in a category [definition]: For a family (X_i)_{i∈I} of objects of a category, a product is an object P with morphisms p_i:P→X_i such that for every object W and morphisms f_i:W→X_i there is a unique f:W→P with p_i∘f=f_i for all i.
- Product topology [definition]: On a Cartesian product of topological spaces, the topology with basic open sets restricting finitely many coordinates to open subsets and leaving all other coordinates unrestricted.
- Profinite group [definition]: A profinite group is a compact Hausdorff totally disconnected topological group. Finite groups have their discrete topology in this convention.
- Projective general linear group PGL₂ over a finite field [definition]: For a finite field k, PGL₂(k)=GL₂(k)/{aI:a∈k×}. It acts faithfully on the projective line P¹(k), by the action of matrices on one-dimensional subspaces of k².
- Projective scheme over a field [definition]: A k-scheme is projective if it admits a closed immersion over k into some projective space Pⁿ_k. Projectivity includes the specified algebraic scheme structure, rather than only its set of geometric points.
- Projective space as a scheme [construction]: Projective n-space over a field k is obtained by gluing the n+1 standard affine charts Spec k[x₀/x_i,…,x_n/x_i], omitting x_i/x_i, with the standard coordinate-ratio changes on overlaps. Its points over an extension field are nonzero vectors modulo common nonzero scaling.
- Projective space over a field [definition]: For a field F, projective n-space consists of nonzero vectors in F^(n+1) modulo multiplication by nonzero scalars; points are written [x₀:⋯:x_n]. Homogeneous polynomial equations define its projective algebraic subsets.
- Proper invertible ideal class of a quadratic order [definition]: For an orderR in an imaginary quadratic fieldK, a proper invertible fractionalR-ideal is a rank-twoℤ-latticeI⊂K withaI⊆R for some nonzeroa∈R, multiplier ring{x∈K:xI⊆I}=R, andII′=R for another fractionalR-idealI′. Their products modulo principal idealsαR,α∈K×, formPic(R).
- Proper morphism of schemes [definition]: A morphism of schemes is proper if it is separated, of finite type and universally closed: after every base change the map on underlying topological spaces sends closed sets to closed sets. A scheme over a field is complete if its structure morphism is proper.
- Quotient conjugation action on first group cohomology [definition]: For N normal in G and a G-module M, g acts on a cocycle z:N→M by(g·z)(n)=g·z(g⁻¹ng). This induces an action of G/N on H¹(N,M), because inner N-actions induce the identity on cohomology. For profinite groups and discrete modules use continuous cocycles.
- Quotient module [definition]: For a submodule N of an R-module M, the cosets M/N carry addition and scalar multiplication induced from M.
- Quotient of a commutative ring by an ideal [definition]: For an ideal I of R, the cosets R/I form a ring under (r+I)+(s+I)=r+s+I and (r+I)(s+I)=rs+I.
- Quotient of a group by a normal subgroup [definition]: For a normal subgroup H of G, the cosets G/H form a group under (gH)(g′H)=gg′H.
- Quotient of an abelian group by a subgroup [definition]: For an abelian group G and subgroup H,the cosets G/H form an abelian group under (g+H)+(g′+H)=g+g′+H.
- Quotient topology [definition]: For a surjection q:X→Y, a subset U⊆Y is open exactly when q⁻¹(U) is open in X.
- Radon measure on a locally compact Hausdorff space [definition]: A Borel measure on a locally compact Hausdorff space that is finite on compact sets, outer regular on Borel sets (each has measure equal to the infimum over open supersets) and inner regular on open sets (each has measure equal to the supremum over compact subsets). This is the Folland–Cohn convention; on σ-compact spaces it coincides with compact inner regularity on all Borel sets.
- Ramification index of a finite extension of discretely valued fields [definition]: For L/K with compatible normalized discrete valuations v_L,v_K, the positive integer e satisfying v_L(x)=e v_K(x) for every x in K×; equivalently [v_L(L×):v_L(K×)]=e.
- Rational function over a field [definition]: An element of k(x), represented by a quotient p(x)/q(x) of polynomials over k with q≠0; two quotients are equal when their cross-products agree.
- Rational number [definition]: A real x is rational if x=a/b for integers a,b with b≠0; otherwise it is irrational.
- Rational point of a scheme [definition]: For a k-scheme X and a field extension L/k, an L-rational point of X is a morphism Spec L→X over Spec k. The set of these morphisms is denoted X(L); this includes k-rational points when L=k.
- Real exponential function [definition]: The real function exp(x)=∑_{n≥0}xⁿ/n!, whose series converges for every real x.
- Real number [definition]: A real number is an element of a complete ordered field ℝ; completeness means every nonempty subset bounded above has a least upper bound.
- Real sequence [definition]: A real sequence is a function a:ℕ₊→ℝ, written (aₙ)ₙ≥1.
- Reduction type of an elliptic curve over a local field [definition]: Reduce a minimal integral Weierstrass equation modulo the maximal ideal. Nonsingular reduction is good; nodal reduction is multiplicative, split if both tangent directions at the node are rational over the residue field and nonsplit otherwise; the remaining singular case is additive reduction.
- Regulator of an elliptic curve over a number field [construction]: For a ℤ-basis P₁,…,P_r of E(K)/E(K)_tors, the regulator is Reg(E/K)=det(⟨P_i,P_j⟩_K), using the specified relative Néron–Tate pairing. The rank-zero determinant is1. A basis change in GL_r(ℤ) does not alter it; torsion factors are not incorporated into this regulator.
- Relative Néron–Tate height pairing on an elliptic curve [construction]: Fix an elliptic curve E/K and let h_K(x(P)) be relative logarithmic projective height, with x(O)=∞. Put q_K(P)=lim_(n→∞)4^(−n)h_K(x([2ⁿ]P)). The relative Néron–Tate pairing is ⟨P,Q⟩_K=(q_K(P+Q)−q_K(P)−q_K(Q))/2; thus ⟨P,P⟩_K=q_K(P). This fixes the x-coordinate normalization and the relative, rather than degree-divided absolute, convention.
- Relative logarithmic height on projective space [construction]: For [a₀:⋯:a_n]∈ℙⁿ(K), its relative logarithmic height over the number field K is h_K=∑_v log max_i|a_i|_v using normalized local multiplicative norms. The product formula makes this independent of homogeneous scaling; division by[K:ℚ] gives the absolute-height convention.
- Representable functor [definition]: A functor naturally isomorphic to Hom(A,−), or to Hom(−,A) in the contravariant case.
- Residue field at a prime ideal [definition]: For a commutative ring R and prime ideal 𝔭, its residue field is κ(𝔭)=R_𝔭/𝔭R_𝔭, equivalently the fraction field of R/𝔭. For a maximal ideal 𝔪 it is R/𝔪.
- Residue field of a discretely valued field [definition]: The quotient O_v/m_v of the valuation ring by its maximal ideal.
- Restricted product of topological groups [definition]: For groups G_i with chosen open subgroups K_i, the group of tuples g_i with g_i∈K_i for all but finitely many i, with the topology whose basic opens are products of open sets equal to K_i outside finitely many coordinates.
- Riemann surface [definition]: A Riemann surface is a connected Hausdorff second-countable topological space covered by coordinate neighborhoods homeomorphic to open subsets of ℂ, with holomorphic transition maps. The charts define holomorphic functions and maps; its complex dimension is one.
- Ring of integers of a number field [definition]: The subring O_K of a number field K consisting of its elements integral over Z, namely the roots in K of monic integer-coefficient polynomials.
- Ring of p-adic integers [definition]: For a prime p, ℤ_p is the subring {x∈ℚ_p:v_p(x)≥0}, with its p-adic topology.
- Root of unity [definition]: In a field, a root of unity is an element ζ with ζⁿ=1 for some positive integer n; its order is the least such n.
- S-ideal class group of a number field [definition]: For a number field K and a finite set S of places containing all infinite places, Cl_S(K) is Cl(K) modulo the subgroup generated by the classes of its finite prime ideals in S. Equivalently it is the ideal class group of the ring of S-integers.
- Scheme [definition]: A scheme is a locally ringed space admitting an open cover by affine schemes, with the given local-ringed-space structures on those opens.
- Semidirect product of groups [definition]: Given a homomorphism α:G→Aut(H), the group H⋊_αG has underlying set H×G and multiplication (h,g)(h′,g′)=(hα(g)(h′),gg′).
- Separable algebraic field extension [definition]: An algebraic extension L/F is separable if the minimal polynomial over F of every element of L has distinct roots in an algebraic closure.
- Separable closure of a field [definition]: A separable algebraic extension F^sep/F that has no further nontrivial finite separable algebraic extension, equivalently the separable elements in a chosen algebraic closure.
- Separable polynomial over a field [definition]: A polynomial having no repeated root in an algebraic closure of its coefficient field.
- Separated morphism of schemes [definition]: A morphism X→S is separated if its diagonal X→X×_S X is a closed immersion. A scheme is separated over a specified base if its structure morphism is separated.
- Sequence in a set [definition]: A function from the natural numbers, or the positive natural numbers, to that set.
- Set [definition]: A collection of distinct elements, regarded as one object.
- Sheaf of Kähler differentials of a scheme [construction]: For a scheme X over k, Ω¹_(X/k) is the sheaf of O_X-modules obtained by gluing the modules Ω¹_(A/k) on affine charts Spec A with their localization identifications. Its global sections are regular differential one-forms; a morphism induces pullback of these differentials.
- Sheaf on a topological space [definition]: A sheaf of sets on X assigns a set F(U) to each open U and restriction maps compatible with identities and composition, such that sections agreeing on every overlap of any open cover glue to one unique section. Sheaves of groups, rings or modules carry the respective operations compatibly with restriction.
- Sign of a permutation [definition]: The sign of a permutation of {1,…,n} is (−1) to the number of inversions, where an inversion is a pair i<j with σ(i)>σ(j).
- Signature of a number field [definition]: The signature (r₁,r₂) of a number field K consists of the number r₁ of embeddings K→ℝ and half the number of nonreal embeddings K→ℂ, counted in complex-conjugate pairs. Thus [K:ℚ]=r₁+2r₂.
- Smooth finitely presented commutative algebra [definition]: A finitely presented R-algebra A such that, for every R-algebra B with nilpotent ideal I, every R-algebra map A→B/I lifts to A→B. This is the infinitesimal lifting formulation of smoothness for affine finite-presentation algebras.
- Smooth morphism of affine finite-presentation spaces [definition]: A morphism represented contravariantly by A→B is smooth when B, as an A-algebra, is finitely presented and satisfies the nilpotent-ideal lifting property.
- Smooth morphism of schemes [definition]: A morphism of schemes is smooth if locally on affine source and target charts its corresponding algebra map is smooth, meaning finitely presented and formally smooth for nilpotent-ideal lifting. Smoothness over a field is used for nonsingular geometric fibers, not merely regularity over an imperfect field.
- Smooth projective algebraic curve [definition]: A smooth projective geometrically connected curve over k is a one-dimensional k-scheme smooth over k, geometrically connected, and admitting a closed immersion into some projective space over k.
- Special linear group SL₂(ℤ) [definition]: The group of2×2 integer matrices [[a,b],[c,d]] satisfying ad−bc=1, with matrix multiplication.
- Special linear group over a commutative ring [definition]: The group SL_n(R) of square matrices over a commutative ring R whose determinant is 1, with matrix multiplication.
- Split Cartan subgroup of GL₂ over a finite field [definition]: For a finite field k, a split Cartan subgroup of GL₂(k) is the stabilizer of each of two distinct lines in k². In a basis on these lines it is the full group of invertible diagonal matrices, isomorphic to k××k×.
- Splitting of a prime in a number-field extension [definition]: A nonzero prime ideal𝔭 ofO_K splits completely in a finite extensionL/K if𝔭O_L is a product of[L:K] distinct prime ideals, each with residue-field degree1. In a quadratic extension this means two distinct primes of ramification index and residue degree1.
- Square-class group of a field [definition]: For a field F, its square-class group is F×/(F×)², where (F×)² is the subgroup of nonzero squares.
- Squarefree integer [definition]: A positive integer divisible by no square of a prime.
- Stalk of a sheaf [definition]: The stalk F_x is the set of germs of sections near x: pairs(U,s) with x∈U and s∈F(U), identified when their restrictions agree on some smaller neighborhood of x. Algebraic operations on a sheaf induce the same operations on its stalks.
- Subfield generated by a subset [definition]: For S⊆L in a field extension L/F, F(S) is the smallest subfield of L containing F and S.
- Subgroup [definition]: A subset of a group containing the identity and closed under multiplication and inverses.
- Submodule of a module over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup of a module stable under multiplication by every scalar of its coefficient ring.
- Subset [definition]: A⊆B means every element of A is an element of B.
- Sum of a nonnegative series [definition]: For aₙ≥0, define ∑ₙ≥1aₙ as the supremum of its finite partial sums; the value may be infinite.
- Symmetric group of a finite set [definition]: The group of all bijections of a finite set to itself under composition.
- Tate module of an abelian variety [construction]: For an abelian variety A over a characteristic-zero field k and a prime ℓ, T_ℓA=lim←A[ℓⁿ](k̄) with multiplication-by-ℓ transition maps. It is a free ℤ_ℓ-module of rank2dim A with its continuous absolute Galois action; V_ℓA=T_ℓA⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)ℚ_ℓ.
- Tate module of an elliptic curve [definition]: For an elliptic curve E in characteristic zero and a prime ℓ, T_ℓ(E)=lim←E[ℓⁿ], with transition maps multiplication by ℓ. It is a rank-two ℤ_ℓ-module with a continuous Galois action.
- Tensor product of commutative algebras [definition]: For commutative R-algebras A,B, the module A⊗_R B with multiplication (a⊗b)(a′⊗b′)=aa′⊗bb′ and unit 1⊗1; it is the coproduct of A and B among commutative R-algebras.
- Tensor product of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: For R-modules M,N, the R-module M⊗_R N with a bilinear map (m,n)↦m⊗n through which every R-bilinear map M×N→P factors uniquely by an R-linear map.
- Topological group [definition]: A group with a topology for which multiplication G×G→G and inversion G→G are continuous.
- Topological ring [definition]: A ring with a topology for which addition, multiplication and additive inversion are continuous; the rings here are commutative with identity.
- Topological space [definition]: A set X with a family τ of subsets, called open sets, containing ∅ and X and closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
- Torsion subgroup E[n] of an elliptic curve [definition]: For an elliptic curve E over F, E[n] is the subgroup of E(F̄) annihilated by multiplication by n. In characteristic zero it is a free rank-two ℤ/nℤ-module with its natural action of Gal(F̄/F).
- Total order [definition]: A partial order in which any two elements are comparable.
- Totally disconnected topological space [definition]: A topological space is totally disconnected if every nonempty connected subset has exactly one point.
- Trace of a square matrix [definition]: The sum of the diagonal entries of a square matrix.
- Uniform convergence of functions into a metric space [definition]: A sequence fₙ:X→(Y,d) converges uniformly to f if for every ε>0, d(fₙ(x),f(x))<ε for every x∈X once n is sufficiently large independently of x.
- Union of a family of sets [definition]: The set of elements belonging to at least one member of the family.
- Unit group of the ring of integers of a number field [definition]: For a number field K, its unit group O_K× consists of the u∈O_K for which u⁻¹ also lies in O_K, with multiplication. Its torsion elements are exactly the roots of unity in K.
- Upper ramification groups of a local field [construction]: For a finite Galois local extension with lower groups G_t, let φ(u)=∫₀^u[G₀:G_t]^(−1)dt for u≥0, let ψ be its increasing inverse and put G^v=G_(ψ(v)). For the absolute Galois group, G_K^v is the inverse limit of these upper groups over finite Galois subextensions; the quotient-compatible upper numbering is used.
- Upper triangular subgroup of GL₂ over a field [definition]: For a field k, the upper triangular subgroup of GL₂(k) consists of matrices [[a,b],[0,d]] with a,d∈k× and b∈k. Its conjugates are exactly the stabilizers of one-dimensional subspaces of k².
- Valuation ring of a discretely valued field [definition]: For a discrete valuation v on K, the ring O_v={x:v(x)≥0}; its maximal ideal is m_v={x:v(x)>0}.
- Vector space [definition]: An abelian group with scalar multiplication by a field satisfying a(v+w)=av+aw, (a+b)v=av+bv, (ab)v=a(bv), and 1v=v.
- Vector subspace [definition]: A subset of a vector space containing zero and closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
- Zero morphism in a category with a zero object [definition]: In a category with a zero object 0, the zero morphism X→Y is the composite X→0→Y of the unique morphisms through 0. Any composite of a zero morphism with another morphism is again a zero morphism.
- Zero object in a category [definition]: An object 0 of a category such that for every object X there is exactly one morphism 0→X and exactly one morphism X→0.
- p-adic valuation of a rational number [definition]: For a prime p and nonzero rational x, v_p(x) is the unique integer r such that x=p^r a/b with integers a,b not divisible by p; put v_p(0)=+∞.
- σ-algebra [definition]: A σ-algebra on Ω contains Ω and is closed under complements in Ω and countable unions.
- σ-algebra generated by a family of subsets [definition]: The smallest σ-algebra containing that family, equivalently the intersection of all σ-algebras on the ambient set that contain it.
