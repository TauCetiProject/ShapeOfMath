LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Stationary hitting-time tail bound by the killed principal eigenvalue
   For an irreducible reversible finite Markov chain with stationary law π and a set A whose killed matrix on Aᶜ has largest eigenvalue λ_A, the chain started from π satisfies P_π(T_A>n)≤π(Aᶜ)λ_Aⁿ for every n≥0.
   Source: explains, Lemma 3.8 (iii) with the component decomposition in the proof of (3.8), pp. 15–17
   Note: Proves the discrete-time bound P_{π_B}[T_A>t]≤γ₁ᵗ for the killed chain; cites Aldous–Fill for the continuous-time analogue. Not a priority claim.
