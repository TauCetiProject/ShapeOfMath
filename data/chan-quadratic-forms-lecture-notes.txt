LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Finiteness of the class number of a genus of quadratic ℤ-lattices
   A genus of nondegenerate quadratic ℤ-lattices contains only finitely many isometry classes; their number is the class number of the genus.
   Source: explains, Theorem 7.5, p. 50
   Note: Lecture-note statement and proof; not a priority claim.

2. Finiteness of isometry classes of ℤ-lattices with given rank and discriminant
   For each n≥1 and nonzero rational d there are only finitely many isometry classes of nondegenerate quadratic ℤ-lattices of rank n and discriminant d.
   Source: explains, Theorem 7.4, pp. 49–50
   Note: Lecture-note statement and proof via Hermite’s bound.
