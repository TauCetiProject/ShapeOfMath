1. Krylov–Rozovskii well-posedness of monotone SPDEs in a Gelfand triple
   On a Gelfand triple V⊂H⊂V* with cylindrical Wiener noise, let progressively measurable A:[0,T]×V×Ω→V* and B:[0,T]×V×Ω→L₂(U,H) be hemicontinuous, weakly monotone (2⟨A(u)−A(v),u−v⟩+‖B(u)−B(v)‖²_{HS}≤C‖u−v‖²_H), coercive (2⟨A(v),v⟩+‖B(v)‖²_{HS}+c‖v‖^α_V≤f+C‖v‖²_H) and bounded (‖A(v)‖^{α/(α−1)}_{V*}≤C‖v‖^α_V+f), α>1, f integrable adapted. Each X₀∈L²(Ω,F₀;H) gives a unique continuous adapted H-valued variational solution with E(sup_t‖X_t‖²_H+∫₀ᵀ‖X_t‖^α_Vdt)<∞.
   Source: explains, Theorem 4.2.4 with proof, pp.75–90
   Note: Exposition and proof of the Krylov–Rozovskii theorem ([KR79]); not its origin.

2. Variational solution of an SPDE in a Gelfand triple
   On a Gelfand triple V⊂H⊂V* with cylindrical Wiener noise W on U and progressively measurable A:[0,T]×V×Ω→V*, B:[0,T]×V×Ω→L₂(U,H), a variational solution of dX=A(t,X)dt+B(t,X)dW is a continuous adapted H-valued process X whose dt⊗P-class lies in L^α([0,T]×Ω;V)∩L²([0,T]×Ω;H) and which satisfies, almost surely for all t, X_t=X₀+∫₀ᵗA(s,X̄_s)ds+∫₀ᵗB(s,X̄_s)dW_s, the first integral a V*-valued Bochner integral, X̄ a progressively measurable V-valued version.
   Source: explains, Definition 4.2.1, p.73
   Note: Definition of variational solution as used in the Krylov–Rozovskii theorem.
