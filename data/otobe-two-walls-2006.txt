LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Supremum stability of the parabolic double-obstacle solution map
   For fixed strictly separated continuous walls on the circle and Lipschitz drift f, subtract a continuous free driving field v from a reflected heat solution and write the remainder z. On each finite interval, changing v and the initial data changes z by at most C_T times their supremum-norm changes. The same estimate holds uniformly for the lower and upper penalized equations, independently of the penalty parameters.
   Source: explains, Lemmas 2.3–2.4 and §2.3
   Note: Interval form with zero initial data; proof inspected for cone-022; no priority claim.
