LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Doob’s strong-Feller irreducible mixing theorem
   A stochastically continuous Markov semigroup on a Polish space which is strong Feller and topologically irreducible at each positive time has at most one invariant probability measure; if it exists, P_t(x,·) converges to it in total variation for every x. Strong Feller means bounded Borel functions become continuous, and irreducibility means P_t(x,O)>0 for each nonempty open O.
   Source: explains, §1; Theorem 1, Remark 2, §3, Appendix A
   Note: New coupling proof of Doob’s theorem (regular case); not an origin claim (Doob 1948).
