REQUIRED CONCEPT FRONTIER (47)

- Algebra and uniform closure of nilsequences: For a fixed d≥1, basic d-step nilsequences form a complex algebra stable under conjugation and integer shifts; the same is true with no step bound. Their uniform closures, the d-step nilsequences and nilsequences, are uniformly closed algebras with the same invariances.
- Bergelson–Leibman polynomial mean-ergodic theorem on the rational complement: Let G be a finitely generated nilpotent unitary group on H and g:ℤ→G a polynomial sequence with g(0)=1. Let E=⟨g(ℤ)⟩ and H_rat=⋃_(l≥1){u:P^l u=u for every P∈E}. If u⊥H_rat, then (2N+1)^(−1)∑_(n=−N)^Ng(n)u→0 in Hilbert norm.
- Bochner representation on the integer lattice: Every positive definite function φ:ℤᵈ→ℂ has a unique finite positive Borel measure μ on ℝᵈ/ℤᵈ such that φ(n)=∫exp(2πi⟨n,x⟩)dμ(x). Its mass is φ(0), so it is a probability measure exactly when φ(0)=1.
- Bohr characterizations of almost periodic sequences: For a bounded complex sequence on ℤ, almost periodicity is equivalent both to relative norm compactness of its integer translates in ℓ∞(ℤ), and to uniform approximability by finite linear combinations of the sequences exp(2πint), t∈ℝ/ℤ.
- Canonical trace of a noncommutative torus: The canonical faithful tracial state τ on A_Θ is determined on finite ordered Fourier sums by τ(∑_r a_r u^r)=a_0. It extends continuously to A_Θ; the ordered monomials form an orthonormal basis of the Hilbert completion for ⟨a,b⟩=τ(b*a), so that completion identifies with ℓ²(ℤᵈ).
- Compact and weak part of a unitary representation: For a unitary representation of a group G on H, the compact vectors form a closed invariant subspace H_c, equal to the closed span of finite-dimensional invariant subspaces. Its orthogonal complement H_w is invariant, and H=H_c⊕H_w. For every unitary representation on K the diagonal tensor representation on H_w⊗K has no nonzero invariant vector.
- Compact vectors of one unitary operator: For a unitary T, the compact-vector subspace H_c(T) is the closed linear span of its eigenvectors. For every positive integer k, H_c(Tᵏ)=H_c(T).
- Conjugate complex Hilbert space: For a complex Hilbert space H, its conjugate H̄ has the same additive group, scalar multiplication c·ū=(conjugate(c)u)̄, and inner product ⟨ū,v̄⟩=conjugate(⟨u,v⟩). A unitary U induces Ūū=(Uu)̄.
- Connected simply connected realization of basic nilsequences: Every basic nilsequence can be realized as the restriction of a continuous test function along a nilrotation on a compact nilmanifold whose ambient nilpotent Lie group is connected and simply connected, allowing an increase of the dimension.
- Contractivity of star homomorphisms of C*-algebras: Every star homomorphism φ:A→B of C*-algebras is contractive: ‖φ(a)‖≤‖a‖. An injective star homomorphism is isometric; in particular every star automorphism is isometric.
- Discrete antiderivative of a polynomial: For every real polynomial P there is a real polynomial Q with Q(0)=0 and Q(n+1)−Q(n)=P(n). Thus for n≥0, Σ_(j=0)^(n−1)P(j)=Q(n), and the same polynomial identity extends to signed sums for negative n.
- Distality of nilrotations: Every translation on a compact nilmanifold is a distal homeomorphism. Connectedness of the nilpotent Lie group is not required.
- Eigenvectors of a tensor product of unitaries: For unitary U on H and V on K, every vector w with (U⊗V)w=λw is a norm-convergent sum ∑_j c_j u_j⊗v_j, where (u_j) and (v_j) are orthonormal families, Uu_j=α_j u_j, Vv_j=β_j v_j, and α_jβ_j=λ for nonzero terms.
- Embedding independence of contractive approximation rank: For a Banach space X, a finite set Ω⊆X and δ>0, the least d admitting contractive maps X→ℓ∞ᵈ→Y approximating a CA embedding ι:X→Y on Ω within δ is independent of the CA embedding. Here a CA embedding is an isometry admitting such finite-set approximations for every Ω,δ.
- Entropy formula for an integer toral automorphism: For A∈GL_d(ℤ), the induced homeomorphism T_A:x↦Ax of ℝᵈ/ℤᵈ has topological entropy ∑_(j=1)^d max(0,log|λ_j|), where λ_j are the complex eigenvalues of A with multiplicity.
- Every point of a distal compact metric system is minimal: Every point in a distal compact metric dynamical system is a minimal point.
- Exactness of a noncommutative torus: Every finite-dimensional noncommutative torus A_Θ is an exact C*-algebra.
- Exponential diffeomorphism for simply connected nilpotent Lie groups: For a connected simply connected nilpotent real Lie group G, exp_G is a diffeomorphism from its Lie algebra onto G. In particular each g∈G has a unique mth root exp_G((1/m)log_G(g)) for every positive integer m.
- Fubini’s theorem for integrable functions: An integrable function on the product of σ-finite measure spaces has integrable almost-everywhere sections, and its product integral equals either iterated integral.
- Gelfand representation of a commutative unital C*-algebra: For a commutative unital C*-algebra A, its character space Â is compact Hausdorff, and Γ:A→C(Â), Γ(a)(χ)=χ(a), is an isometric star isomorphism.
- Gelfand–Naimark–Segal representation theorem for a state: For every state ρ on a C*-algebra A there are a Hilbert space H_ρ, a star representation π_ρ:A→B(H_ρ) and a unit cyclic vector ξ_ρ with ρ(a)=⟨π_ρ(a)ξ_ρ,ξ_ρ⟩. The cyclic representation is unique up to a unitary carrying the cyclic vector to the other one.
- Green–Tao Möbius orthogonality for nilsequences: Every nilsequence (a_n) is Möbius-orthogonal: N^(−1)∑_(n=1)^N μ(n)a_n→0. This qualitative consequence extends the basic nilmanifold theorem to uniform limits.
- Host–Kra density of elementary theta sequences: The 2-step nilsequences are the uniformly closed shift-invariant linear span of sequences exp(2πins)exp(πin(n−1)t)∏_(j=1)^dω_n(α_j,β_j), where s,t∈ℝ/ℤ, d≥0 and α₁,…,α_d,β₁,…,β_d are rationally independent modulo one. Their linear span is also dense among 2-step nilsequences in the quadratic mean norm.
- Host–Kra–Maass local cube criterion for nilsequences: For d≥2, a bounded complex sequence a is a (d−1)-step nilsequence iff for every ε>0 some L≥1 and δ>0 have this property: for all k,n₁,…,n_d∈ℤ, if |a_(k+∑η_jn_j+t)−a_(k+t)|<δ for every |t|≤L and every η∈{0,1}ᵈ except η=(1,…,1), then |a_(k+∑n_j)−a_k|<ε.
- Interlacing nilsequences on finitely many arithmetic progressions: Let m≥1 and a be a bounded complex sequence on ℤ. If each subsequence a_(mt+r), 0≤r<m, is a basic nilsequence, a nilsequence, or an almost nilsequence with symmetric Cesàro-null remainder, respectively, then a has the same respective property.
- Intersections of tensor product subspaces: For finitely many closed subspaces M_i⊆H and N_i⊆K, ⋂_i(M_i⊗N_i)=(⋂_iM_i)⊗(⋂_iN_i), where each tensor product is the closed Hilbert tensor subspace of H⊗K.
- Kerr–Li entropy lower bound for a noncommutative toral automorphism: If α_S is a noncommutative toral automorphism and λ_i are the eigenvalues of S, counted with multiplicity, then ht(α_S)≥½∑_(|λ_i|>1)log|λ_i|.
- Kerr–Li equality of classical and approximation entropies: For a homeomorphism T of a compact Hausdorff space X and α_T(f)=f∘T on C(X), h_t(α_T)=h_c(α_T)=h_top(T), where h_t is Voiculescu–Brown entropy and h_c is contractive approximation entropy.
- Kerr–Li positive CA entropy and dual-ball dynamics: Let α be an isometric automorphism of a Banach space X and T_α(ω)=ω∘α on the weak-star compact dual unit ball. If a closed T_α-invariant set Z has evaluation X→C(Z) an isomorphism onto a closed subspace, the conditions h_c(α)>0, h_top(T_α)>0, h_top(T_α)=∞, and h_top(T_α|Z)>0 are equivalent.
- Kronecker’s root-of-unity theorem: If a monic integer polynomial has only nonzero complex roots, all of modulus at most one, then every root is a root of unity.
- Lebesgue dominated convergence theorem: If measurable fₙ converge almost everywhere to f and |fₙ|≤g for an integrable g, then f is integrable and ∫|fₙ−f|→0; consequently their integrals converge.
- Leibman’s compact-vector criterion for nilpotent unitary actions: If a finitely generated nilpotent group G=⟨T₁,…,T_r⟩ acts unitarily on H, then H_c(G)=⋂_(T∈G)H_c(T)=⋂_(j=1)^rH_c(T_j).
- Norm density of Fourier polynomials in a noncommutative torus: Finite linear combinations of the ordered monomials u₁^(r₁)⋯u_d^(r_d), r∈ℤᵈ, are norm dense in A_Θ.
- Polynomial phases are basic nilsequences: For every real polynomial p, the sequence exp(2πip(n)), n∈ℤ, is a basic nilsequence.
- Polynomial powers of a quasi-unipotent matrix on residue classes: If an invertible complex d×d matrix A has all eigenvalues roots of unity, some m≥1 satisfies (Aᵐ−I)ᵈ=0, and A^(mt+r)=Aʳ∑_(j=0)^(d−1)binom(t,j)(Aᵐ−I)^j for every t∈ℤ and 0≤r<m. Thus every entry is polynomial in t; if A is real the coefficients are real.
- Product normal form of polynomial sequences in a nilpotent group: For a nilpotent group G, the sequences g:ℤ→G annihilated by a sufficiently high iterate of Dg(n)=g(n)⁻¹g(n+1) are exactly finite products U₁^(p₁(n))⋯U_r^(p_r(n)) with U_j∈G and integer-valued polynomials p_j. If g(0)=1, the factors can be chosen with p_j(0)=0.
- Representation independence of completely positive approximation rank: For faithful star representations π:A→B(H) and ρ:A→B(K), finite Ω⊆A and δ>0, the least finite-dimensional C*-rank through which completely positive contractions approximate π on Ω within δ equals the corresponding rank for ρ, allowing the value infinity.
- Riesz–Markov representation on a compact space: Every positive linear functional on the continuous real functions on a compact Hausdorff space is integration against a unique finite regular Borel measure.
- Scalar-commuting unitaries generate a two-step nilpotent group: If unitary operators U₁,…,U_d satisfy U_iU_j=ζ_(ij)U_jU_i with scalar |ζ_(ij)|=1, their generated group is nilpotent of class at most two: every group commutator is a scalar operator and is therefore central.
- Smooth noncommutative torus: For the ordered monomials u^r=u₁^(r₁)⋯u_d^(r_d) in A_Θ, the smooth noncommutative torus A_Θ^∞ consists of the norm-convergent sums ∑_(r∈ℤᵈ)a_r u^r with rapidly decreasing coefficients. It has the topology of their rapid-decrease seminorms and the product and involution inherited from A_Θ.
- Subgroups of finitely generated nilpotent groups are finitely generated: Every subgroup of a finitely generated nilpotent group is itself finitely generated and nilpotent.
- Syndetic return times of a minimal point: If x is a minimal point of a compact dynamical system and U is an open neighborhood of x, then {n∈ℤ:Tⁿx∈U} is syndetic. Conversely syndetic returns to every neighborhood imply minimality.
- Uniformly closed algebra of nilsequence plus null sequences: The bounded sequences that are sums of a nilsequence and a symmetric Cesàro-null sequence form a uniformly closed complex algebra invariant under conjugation and shifts. The same holds for a fixed nilpotency step bound.
- Weak-star compactness of a unital C*-algebra state space: The states S(A) of a unital C*-algebra form a weak-star compact convex subset of the dual unit ball. Every star automorphism α induces the weak-star homeomorphism ρ↦ρ∘α of S(A).
- Weyl equidistribution theorem for real polynomials: If a real polynomial has at least one irrational coefficient of positive degree, then its values on the integers are equidistributed modulo one.
- Weyl exponential-sum criterion: A two-sided real sequence x_n is equidistributed modulo one if and only if (2N+1)⁻¹∑_(n=−N)^N exp(2πikx_n)→0 for every nonzero integer k. The analogous equivalence holds for one-sided Cesàro averages.
- Zero Voiculescu–Brown entropy implies zero CA entropy: If α is a star automorphism of an exact C*-algebra and h_t(α)=0, then h_c(α)=0.

1. Noncommutative toral automorphism

2. Voiculescu–Brown entropy of a C*-algebra automorphism
   For an exact C*-algebra A with faithful representation π and automorphism α, let rcp(π,Ω,δ) be the least rank of a finite-dimensional C*-algebra B through which contractive completely positive maps approximate π on finite Ω within δ; rank is the dimension of a maximal abelian subalgebra. The entropy is sup_(Ω,δ)limsup_(n→∞)n^(−1)log rcp(π,⋃_(j=0)^(n−1)αʲΩ,δ).
   Source: relies_on, Definition 2.1; §5
   Note: Zero completely-positive approximation entropy is the central hypothesis.

3. Contractive approximation entropy of a Banach-space isometry
   For an isometric automorphism α of a Banach space X, choose an isometric embedding ι:X→Y admitting finite-set approximations through contractive maps X→ℓ∞^d→Y. Let rc(Ω,δ) be the least such d for error below δ on finite Ω. The contractive approximation entropy is sup_(Ω,δ)limsup_(n→∞)n^(−1)log rc(⋃_(j=0)^(n−1)αʲΩ,δ).
   Source: relies_on, §2.1.2
   Note: Used in the C*-versions-of-Sarnak equivalence discussion.

4. State of a C*-algebra
   A state on a C*-algebra is a positive complex linear functional of norm one; on a unital C*-algebra this is equivalent to positivity and ρ(1)=1.
   Source: relies_on, §§1–2,5
   Note: Arbitrary states, not just point evaluations or trace states.

5. Gelfand–Naimark–Segal representation theorem for a state
   For every state ρ on a C*-algebra A there are a Hilbert space H_ρ, a star representation π_ρ:A→B(H_ρ) and a unit cyclic vector ξ_ρ with ρ(a)=⟨π_ρ(a)ξ_ρ,ξ_ρ⟩. The cyclic representation is unique up to a unitary carrying the cyclic vector to the other one.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem 2.4; §5.3

6. Green–Tao Möbius orthogonality for nilsequences

7. Nilsequence

   Every star homomorphism φ:A→B of C*-algebras is contractive: ‖φ(a)‖≤‖a‖. An injective star homomorphism is isometric; in particular every star automorphism is isometric.

- Smooth vector field on a manifold [definition]: An R-linear derivation of the algebra C∞(M) of smooth real functions on a smooth manifold M. In local coordinates it acts as ∑_i X^i(x)∂_i with smooth coefficient functions.
- Space of continuous real functions on a compact Hausdorff space [definition]: For a compact Hausdorff space K, C(K) is the real Banach space of continuous functions K→ℝ with norm ||f||∞=max_K|f|.
- Star homomorphism between C*-algebras [definition]: A star homomorphism φ:A→B between C*-algebras is a complex-linear multiplicative map satisfying φ(a*)=φ(a)*. The algebras and map need not be unital. A unital star homomorphism additionally preserves the multiplicative identities when they exist.
- Star representation of a C*-algebra [definition]: A star representation of a C*-algebra A on a complex Hilbert space H is a star-preserving algebra homomorphism π:A→B(H). It is cyclic if π(A)ξ is dense in H for some vector ξ.
- Subgroup [definition]: A subset of a group containing the identity and closed under multiplication and inverses.
- Submodule of a module over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup of a module stable under multiplication by every scalar of its coefficient ring.
- Subset [definition]: A⊆B means every element of A is an element of B.
- Subspace topology [definition]: For A⊆X, the topology whose open sets are A∩U for U open in X.
- Sum of a nonnegative series [definition]: For aₙ≥0, define ∑ₙ≥1aₙ as the supremum of its finite partial sums; the value may be infinite.
- Syndetic subset of the integers [definition]: A set S⊆ℤ is syndetic if some positive integer L makes every integer interval {a,a+1,…,a+L} meet S.
- Tensor product of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: For R-modules M,N, the R-module M⊗_R N with a bilinear map (m,n)↦m⊗n through which every R-bilinear map M×N→P factors uniquely by an R-linear map.
- Tensor product of vector spaces [definition]: The vector space V⊗W with its universal bilinear map from V×W.
- Topological entropy by finite open covers [definition]: For a continuous self-map T of a nonempty compact Hausdorff space X, h_top(T)=sup_𝒰 lim_(n→∞)n⁻¹log N(⋁_(j=0)^(n−1)T^(−j)𝒰), where 𝒰 ranges over finite open covers and N is the least cardinality of a subcover. The join consists of intersections of one set from each cover.
- Topological group [definition]: A group with a topology for which multiplication G×G→G and inversion G→G are continuous.
- Topological space [definition]: A set X with a family τ of subsets, called open sets, containing ∅ and X and closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
- Topology generated by a family of seminorms [definition]: For seminorms (p_i) on a vector space, a neighborhood base at zero consists of finite intersections of sets {x:p_i(x)<ε}; neighborhoods at other points are translates.
- Total order [definition]: A partial order in which any two elements are comparable.
- Tracial state on a C*-algebra [definition]: A tracial state on a C*-algebra A is a state τ satisfying τ(ab)=τ(ba) for all a,b∈A. It is faithful if τ(a*a)=0 implies a=0.
- Uniform convergence of functions into a metric space [definition]: A sequence fₙ:X→(Y,d) converges uniformly to f if for every ε>0, d(fₙ(x),f(x))<ε for every x∈X once n is sufficiently large independently of x.
- Union of a family of sets [definition]: The set of elements belonging to at least one member of the family.
- Unit circle [definition]: The subspace S¹={(x,y)∈ℝ²:x²+y²=1}, often identified with a2π-periodic angular coordinate and equipped with arc-length measure.
- Unitary element of a unital C*-algebra [definition]: In a unital C*-algebra A, an element u is unitary if u*u=uu*=1; equivalently its adjoint is its inverse.
- Unitary operator on a complex Hilbert space [definition]: A surjective complex linear map preserving the inner product.
- Unitary representation of a topological group [definition]: A group homomorphism π from a topological group to the unitary operators of a complex Hilbert space, strongly continuous in the sense that g↦π(g)v is norm-continuous for each vector v.
- Vector space [definition]: An abelian group with scalar multiplication by a field satisfying a(v+w)=av+aw, (a+b)v=av+bv, (ab)v=a(bv), and 1v=v.
- Vector subspace [definition]: A subset of a vector space containing zero and closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
- Weak-star topology on a continuous dual [definition]: The weak-star topology σ(X*,X) on the continuous dual of a normed space X is the topology of pointwise convergence on X.
- σ-algebra [definition]: A σ-algebra on Ω contains Ω and is closed under complements in Ω and countable unions.
- σ-algebra generated by a family of subsets [definition]: The smallest σ-algebra containing that family, equivalently the intersection of all σ-algebras on the ambient set that contain it.
- σ-finite measure [definition]: A measure whose whole underlying space is a countable union of measurable sets of finite measure.
