NEW CONCEPTS

Category
  Objects and morphisms with associative composition and identity morphisms.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §2, Definition 2.1

Functor
  A map of categories preserving objects, morphisms, identities and composition.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §2

Natural transformation
  A family of maps between the values of two functors, commuting with every morphism in their source category.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §2

Equivalence of categories
  A functor with an inverse up to natural isomorphism.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §2

Strong monoidal functor
  A functor equipped with coherent isomorphisms preserving tensor products and the tensor unit.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §2.4

Tensor equivalence
  A strong monoidal functor whose underlying functor is an equivalence of categories.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §2.4

Monoidal natural transformation
  A natural transformation between monoidal functors compatible with their tensor-product and unit maps.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §2.4

Dual object in a monoidal category
  An object paired with another by evaluation and coevaluation maps satisfying the two zigzag identities; left and right duals reverse the order of the factors.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §2.10

Double-dual functor
  The covariant monoidal endofunctor X↦X** obtained by taking a chosen dual twice in a rigid monoidal category.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §§2.10 and 4.7

Endomorphism algebra of an object in a linear category
  The vector space End(X), with multiplication given by composition and unit the identity of X.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §§1.2–1.3

Idempotent endomorphism
  An endomorphism e:X→X satisfying e∘e=e.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Homological Algebra, §12.4, tag 09SF

Splitting of an idempotent endomorphism
  Maps r:X→Y and i:Y→X with ir=e and ri=id_Y exhibit Y as the image of the idempotent e.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Homological Algebra, §12.4, tag 09SF

Additive category
  A category with abelian-group morphism sets, bilinear composition, a zero object and finite biproducts.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §1.2, Definition 1.2.1

Abelian category
  An additive category with kernels and cokernels in which every monomorphism is a kernel and every epimorphism is a cokernel.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §1.3, Definition 1.3.1

Subobject in a category
  An equivalence class of monomorphisms into a fixed object, where two are equivalent if an isomorphism of their domains commutes with the maps.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §1.3, Definition 1.3.5

Zero object in a category
  An object that is both initial and terminal.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §12

Finite biproduct
  An object with injections and projections making it simultaneously the finite product and coproduct of the given objects.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §1.2

Adjoint functors
  Functors F:C→D and G:D→C with natural bijections Hom_D(FX,Y)≅Hom_C(X,GY); F is left adjoint to G.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §24

Representable functor
  A functor naturally isomorphic to Hom(A,−), or to Hom(−,A) in the contravariant case.
  Learn from: stacks-project, Categories, §3

Tensor category over a field
  A locally finite k-linear abelian rigid monoidal category with a k-bilinear tensor product and End(1)=k. Semisimplicity is not required.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §§4.1 and 4.16

Twist in a braided monoidal category
  A natural automorphism θ of the identity with θ₁=id and θ_{X⊗Y}=c_{Y,X}c_{X,Y}(θ_X⊗θ_Y).
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §8.10

Tensor ideal of morphisms
  A family of linear subspaces of morphism spaces closed under composition on either side and tensoring with arbitrary morphisms.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §§2.10 and 4.11; compare negligible morphisms in the paper

Quotient of a linear category by an ideal
  Keep the objects and replace each morphism space by its quotient by the corresponding ideal subspace.
  Learn from: morrison-snyder-2012, Definition 3.2, with the general quotient construction

Vector space
  An abelian group with scalar multiplication by a field, satisfying the distributive and associativity laws.
  Learn from: representation-theory-notes-2011, §1.2, linear-algebra background

Bilinear map
  A map of two vector-space variables that is linear in each variable separately.
  Learn from: conrad-tensor-products, §3

Tensor product of vector spaces
  The vector space V⊗W with its universal bilinear map from V×W.
  Learn from: conrad-tensor-products, §§1 and 3

Field
  A commutative ring in which 1≠0 and every nonzero element is invertible.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 1, Fields

Associative algebra over a field
  A vector space with an associative bilinear multiplication and a multiplicative identity.
  Learn from: representation-theory-notes-2011, §1.2

Module over an associative algebra
  A vector space on which the algebra acts linearly, compatibly with multiplication and the identity.
  Learn from: representation-theory-notes-2011, §1.3

Idempotent element of an algebra
  An element e satisfying e²=e.
  Learn from: morrison-jones-wenzl-2015, §2

Orthogonal idempotents in an algebra
  Idempotents e and f satisfying ef=fe=0.
  Learn from: representation-theory-notes-2011, §2.3

Invertible element of an algebra
  An element with a two-sided multiplicative inverse.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 1, Rings

Split semisimple algebra
  A finite-dimensional algebra isomorphic to a finite product of full matrix algebras over its base field.
  Learn from: representation-theory-notes-2011, §§2.3 and 2.5

Temperley–Lieb algebra
  TL_n(δ) is spanned by planar noncrossing pairings of n top and n bottom points. Multiply by stacking and replace every closed loop by δ; equivalently its generators satisfy eᵢ²=δeᵢ, eᵢeᵢ₊₁eᵢ=eᵢ and eᵢeⱼ=eⱼeᵢ for abs(i−j)>1.
  Learn from: morrison-jones-wenzl-2015, §1; δ=−[2] in that note

Colored operad
  A collection of operations with specified input and output types, with associative substitution into inputs and identity operations.
  Learn from: morrison-snyder-2012, §2.3, gluing shaded tangles

Planar isotopy of tangle diagrams
  A continuous deformation of embedded diagrams preserving their incidences, boundary components and marked data.
  Learn from: morrison-snyder-2012, Definition 2.2

Fusion matrix
  For an object X in a fusion category, the matrix with entries N_{X,Y}^Z recording the multiplicity of Z in X⊗Y, indexed by simple objects.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §4.5

Grothendieck ring of a fusion category
  The free abelian group on simple isomorphism classes, with addition induced by direct sum and multiplication by tensor product.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §4.5

Spectral radius of a finite matrix
  The maximum absolute value of the complex eigenvalues of the matrix.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §3.2

Perron–Frobenius theorem for nonnegative matrices
  A nonnegative real matrix has a nonnegative eigenvalue equal to its spectral radius. If it is irreducible, that eigenvalue is simple and has a strictly positive eigenvector, unique up to scalar.
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §3.2, Theorem 3.2.1

Squared norm of a simple object in a rigid tensor category
  The choice-independent product of the two quantum traces obtained from an isomorphism X→X** and its inverse dual; in a pivotal category it equals dim(X)dim(X*).
  Learn from: egno-tensor-categories-2015, §7.21, Definition 7.21.2

Type II₁ factor
  An infinite-dimensional von Neumann algebra with center ℂ1 admitting a faithful normal tracial state.
  Learn from: jones-von-neumann-notes, §6.1, Definition 6.1.10

Hilbert bimodule over von Neumann algebras
  A Hilbert space with commuting normal unital *-representations of M and Nᵒᵖ, giving left M and right N actions.
  Learn from: thom-connes-fusion-2006, §2.1

Tracial L²-space of a finite von Neumann algebra
  The Hilbert completion of M for the inner product ⟨x,y⟩=τ(y*x), with the natural left and right multiplication actions.
  Learn from: jones-von-neumann-notes, Chapter 7 and Chapter 10

Connes fusion of Hilbert bimodules
  The relative tensor product of an M–N and an N–P Hilbert bimodule, formed using bounded vectors and completion in the canonical relative inner product, producing an M–P Hilbert bimodule.
  Learn from: thom-connes-fusion-2006, §5; the note treats the same-algebra case and explains the generalization

Von Neumann dimension of a Hilbert module
  A Hilbert module over a finite factor is represented by a projection on a sum of copies of L²(M); its dimension is the extended matrix trace of that projection.
  Learn from: jones-von-neumann-notes, §10.1

Hyperfinite von Neumann algebra
  A von Neumann algebra that is the weak closure of an increasing union of finite-dimensional *-subalgebras.
  Learn from: jones-von-neumann-notes, §15.1

Field extension
  An inclusion of one field into another.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 1, Extension fields

Number field
  A finite extension of ℚ.
  Learn from: milne-number-theory-notes, Introduction and Chapter 2

Algebraic integer
  A complex number satisfying a monic polynomial with integer coefficients.
  Learn from: milne-number-theory-notes, Chapter 2

Root of unity
  An element ζ satisfying ζⁿ=1 for some positive integer n; its order is the least such n.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 5, Cyclotomic extensions

Field embedding
  An injective homomorphism of fields preserving the multiplicative identity.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapters 1–2

Splitting field for a semisimple algebra
  A field extension K/k for which K⊗_k A is a product of full matrix algebras over K.
  Learn from: representation-theory-notes-2011, §§2.3–2.5; compare Brauer's theorem in Morrison–Snyder §2.1

Galois group of a finite Galois extension
  The group of field automorphisms of the extension that fix the base field pointwise.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 3

Normal closure of a number field
  The smallest normal extension of ℚ inside a fixed algebraic closure that contains the given number field.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 2, Normal closures

Fundamental theorem of finite Galois theory
  For a finite Galois extension L/k, intermediate fields correspond bijectively, with inclusions reversed, to subgroups of Gal(L/k), by taking fixed fields.
  Learn from: milne-galois-notes, Chapter 3
