1. Weak well-posedness of two rank-based Brownian particles
   For two real Brownian particles whose drift and positive definite covariance matrix are constant in the two rank chambers and are permuted with the particle ranks, with a fixed measurable lexicographic rule at ties, the corresponding rank-based stochastic equation has a nonexplosive weak solution unique in law.
   Source: discusses, §2, paragraph after (2.4)
   Note: States weak existence and uniqueness in law for rank-chamber piecewise-constant coefficients, citing Stroock–Varadhan and Bass–Pardoux without proof.
