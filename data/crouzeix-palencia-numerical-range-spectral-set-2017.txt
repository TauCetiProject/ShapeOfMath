LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Crouzeix’s numerical-range functional-calculus theorem
   There is an absolute finite constant C such that ∥f(T)∥≤C sup_{z∈W(T)}|f(z)| for every bounded Hilbert-space operator T and f holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closure of W(T).
   Source: explains, §1 (3) and §3 Theorem 6, pp. 1–6
   Note: Proof with constant 1+√2; the existence of an absolute constant was earlier work of Crouzeix (2007). Not an origin claim for the theorem concept.

2. Contractivity of the Cauchy transform of boundary conjugates on a convex domain
   Let Ω⊂ℂ be a bounded open convex set with smooth boundary and f continuous on the closure of Ω and holomorphic in Ω. Then g(z)=(2πi)⁻¹∫_∂Ω conj(f(σ))(σ−z)⁻¹dσ extends continuously to the closure of Ω, is holomorphic in Ω, satisfies sup|g|≤sup|f|, and maps ∂Ω into the convex hull of f(∂Ω).
   Source: explains, §2 Lemma 1 and proof, p. 3
   Note: Stated and proved here using the double-layer potential; continuity credited to C. Neumann. Originality not assessed.
