LOCATED CONCEPTS (6)

1. Dual of a normed vector lattice
   The dual E′ of a normed vector lattice E, ordered by φ≥0 when φ(f)≥0 for all f≥0, is an order complete Banach lattice under the dual norm. The modulus of φ is given on positive elements by |φ|(f)=sup{|φ(g)|:|g|≤f}, so |φ(g)|≤|φ|(|g|) and ‖|φ|‖=‖φ‖.
   Source: explains, Chapter II, Proposition 4.2 and Proposition 5.5, pp. 71-72, 85-86
   Note: Order dual lattice operations and the norm dual of a normed vector lattice as an order complete Banach lattice.

2. Range of a positive projection on a Banach lattice
   If P is a positive projection on a Banach lattice E, its range PE, ordered by the cone E₊∩PE, is a vector lattice with sup_{PE}(x,y)=P(x∨y), and a Banach lattice under a norm equivalent to the norm of E. If P is strictly positive, PE is a vector sublattice of E.
   Source: explains, Chapter III, Proposition 11.5, p. 214
   Note: Statement and proof.

3. Classification of finite-dimensional Archimedean vector lattices
   A real vector lattice of finite dimension n≥1 is Archimedean if and only if it is lattice isomorphic to ℝⁿ with the coordinatewise order; equivalently it has a basis of pairwise disjoint positive elements whose coordinate functionals are positive.
   Source: explains, Chapter II, Theorem 3.9 and Corollary 1, pp. 69-70
   Note: Statement and proof.

4. Jentzsch theorem for irreducible kernel operators on Lᵖ
   Let (X,μ) be σ-finite, 1≤p≤∞, and Q a bounded operator on Lᵖ(μ) given by a measurable kernel q≥0. Suppose some power of Q is compact and q gives positive mass to (X∖Y)×Y whenever μ(Y)>0 and μ(X∖Y)>0. Then the spectral radius r(Q) is positive and a simple eigenvalue with an eigenvector positive μ-almost everywhere, and no other eigenvalue has a positive eigenvector. If q>0 almost everywhere, every other eigenvalue has modulus less than r(Q).
   Source: explains, Chapter V, Theorem 6.6 and proof, p. 337
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.

5. Ando–Krieger theorem on positive spectral radius
   Let E be a Banach lattice with order continuous norm and T>0 an irreducible positive operator in the band of regular operators generated by the order continuous finite-rank operators, for example an irreducible positive kernel operator on Lᵖ(μ), 1≤p<∞. Then the spectral radius r(T) is strictly positive.
   Source: explains, Chapter V, Theorem 6.5, p. 336
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.

6. Perron–Frobenius theorem for irreducible positive operators with a resolvent pole
   Let T>0 be an irreducible positive operator on a Banach lattice with r(T)>0 a pole of the resolvent. Then r(T) is a pole of order one with residue a rank-one projection φ⊗u, φ strictly positive and u quasi-interior; the eigenspace for r(T) is spanned by u; each peripheral eigenvalue is simple; and r(T) is the only eigenvalue with a positive eigenvector.
   Source: explains, Chapter V, Theorem 5.2 and Corollary, pp. 329–330
