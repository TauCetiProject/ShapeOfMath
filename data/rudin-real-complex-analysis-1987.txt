LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Runge’s approximation theorem
   If K⊂ℂ is compact and f is holomorphic on an open neighbourhood of K, then f is a uniform limit on K of rational functions whose poles lie outside K; the poles can be taken in a prescribed set meeting every bounded component of ℂ∖K, together with ∞.
   Source: explains, Theorem 13.6 and proof, pp. 270–271
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.

2. Nonemptiness of the spectrum in a complex Banach algebra
   Every element of a nonzero complex Banach algebra with identity has nonempty spectrum. The usual proof applies Liouville’s theorem to φ((a−λ1)⁻¹) for continuous linear functionals φ.
   Source: explains, Theorem 18.6 and proof, p. 359
