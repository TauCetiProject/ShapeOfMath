LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Maximal coupling of probability measures
   For probabilities μ,ν on a standard Borel space, there is a joint law(X,Y) with marginalsμ,ν and P(X≠Y)=d_TV(μ,ν). Every coupling has P(X≠Y)≥d_TV(μ,ν).
   Source: explains, §2.5 Theorem 2.12 and proof, pp. 15–16
   Note: Abstract construction of a maximal coupling via densities with respect to µ+ν; exposition, not the origin.

2. Quantile coupling for usual stochastic order
   For probability measures mu,nu on the real line, write Q_mu(u)=inf{x:mu((−∞,x])≥u}, 0<u<1. If U is uniform on(0,1), then Q_mu(U) has law mu, and mu≤stnu if and only if Q_mu(u)≤Q_nu(u) for every0<u<1. For probability kernels on the real line the quantile can be chosen jointly measurable in the parameter and u.
   Source: explains, §7.1, Theorems 7.1, 7.3, 7.4
   Note: Monotone quantile coupling; not a priority claim.
