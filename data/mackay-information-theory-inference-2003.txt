1. Gibbs’ inequality for relative entropy
   For probability distributions p and q on the same finite set, the relative entropy satisfies D(p∥q)≥0, with equality exactly when p=q.
   Source: explains, §2.6 (2.46); solution to Exercise 2.26

2. Equality case of Jensen’s inequality for strictly convex functions
   If φ is strictly convex on an interval containing the values of an integrable real random variable X, φ(X) is integrable and Eφ(X)=φ(EX), then X is almost surely constant.
   Source: explains, §2.7, p. 35
   Note: States the strict-convexity equality case; not a priority claim.
