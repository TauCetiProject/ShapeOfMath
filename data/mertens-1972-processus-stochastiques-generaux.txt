LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Mertens right-jump regularization of a strong supermartingale
   For a real class-D strong optional supermartingale X on a usual filtration over [0,T], the sum I_t=∑_(0≤s<t)(X_s−X_(s+)) is a predictable left-continuous purely discontinuous nondecreasing process with I₀=0 and E I_T<∞, and X+I is a càdlàg class-D supermartingale. Right limits are only taken for s<T.
   Source: explains, Ch. I §2 Lemme a)–b) and proof, pp. 51–53
   Note: Proves the regularization X'=X+I, I=∑_(s<t)(X_s−X_(s+)) predictable left-continuous increasing with I=∑λ_n1_]T_n,∞[, for a strong supermartingale with X_0∈L¹ on the half-line; the catalogue’s finite-horizon class-D form is a specialization. Originality not assessed.

2. Path regularity of strong optional supermartingales (Mertens)
   Under the usual conditions, almost every path of a real strong optional supermartingale X on [0,∞) has finite left and right limits at every time and is right upper semicontinuous; in particular X_(t+)≤X_t for every t, almost surely.
   Source: explains, Ch. I §1 Théorème 2, p. 51
   Note: States that almost all paths of a strong supermartingale are regulated and right upper semicontinuous; proof by Théorème 1 and discrete-time convergence (proof not inspected). Originality not assessed.
