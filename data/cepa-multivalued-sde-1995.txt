LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Uniform convergence after undoing a monotone clock
   Let τ_n:[0,∞)→[0,∞) be increasing homeomorphisms fixing0, converging locally uniformly to a continuous nondecreasing unboundedτ, and let continuous y_n converge locally uniformly to y. If y is constant on each level interval ofτ, then x(t)=y(s) forτ(s)=t is well-defined and continuous, and y_n∘τ_n⁻¹ converges to x uniformly on compact intervals.
   Source: explains, §5.1.2, Théorème 5.3, pp. 96–97
   Note: Deterministic time-change criterion stated without proof (θ_n strictly increasing, τ_n=θ_n⁻¹, y_n=x_n∘τ_n, condition (5.49)); attributed to a sufficient condition of Kurtz 1991. Not a priority claim.
