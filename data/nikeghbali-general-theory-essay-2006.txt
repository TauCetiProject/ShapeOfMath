LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Optional projection of a measurable process
   On a filtered probability space satisfying the usual conditions, the optional projection of a bounded or nonnegative B(ℝ₊)⊗F-measurable process X is the optional process ᵒX, unique up to indistinguishability, such that ᵒX_τ1_(τ<∞)=E[X_τ1_(τ<∞)|F_τ] almost surely for every stopping time τ.
   Source: explains, §4.1, Theorem 4.1 and Definition 4.2
   Note: Existence and uniqueness of the optional projection with proof via the optional section theorem and monotone class; standard material, no origin claim.
