LOCATED CONCEPTS (4)

1. Generic flatness theorem
   Let f:X→Y be a dominant morphism of schemes of finite type over a Noetherian ring, with Y reduced. Then Y has a dense open subscheme V such that f⁻¹(V)→V is faithfully flat (Demazure–Gabriel I §3 3.7; EGA IV 6.9.1).
   Source: explains, I §3 3.7
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Compatibility of scheme-theoretic images with flat base change
   For a quasi-compact quasi-separated morphism f:X→Y, the scheme-theoretic image, the smallest closed subscheme of Y through which f factors, is cut out by the kernel of O_Y→f_*O_X. If g:Y′→Y is flat, the scheme-theoretic image of the base change X×_YY′→Y′ is g⁻¹ of the scheme-theoretic image of f (Demazure–Gabriel I §2 4.14).
   Source: explains, I §2 4.14
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

3. Openness of projections of products over a field
   For schemes X and Y over a field k, the projection X×_kY→X is open. In particular, for a field extension K/k the morphism X_K→X is open and surjective (Demazure–Gabriel I §3 3.12).
   Source: explains, I §3 3.12
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

4. Orbit of a reduced algebraic group as the quotient by its stabilizer
   Let a reduced algebraic group G act on a separated algebraic scheme X over k and let x∈X(k). The orbit map g↦gx is faithfully flat onto the orbit O_x with its reduced structure and induces an isomorphism G/G_x→O_x.
   Source: explains, II §5 n°3, Propositions 3.1–3.3
   Note: Proof of the orbit-map properties cited by Milne 1.65(c); not a priority claim.
