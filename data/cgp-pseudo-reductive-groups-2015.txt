LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Dynamic subgroups P_G(λ), U_G(λ), Z_G(λ) of a cocharacter
   For an affine group scheme G over a ring k and a cocharacter λ, let G_m act by t·g=λ(t)gλ(t)⁻¹. For a k-algebra R, P_G(λ)(R) consists of the g∈G(R) whose orbit map, an R[t,t⁻¹]-point of G, comes from an R[t]-point (lim_{t→0} t·g exists); U_G(λ)(R) of those with limit 1; Z_G(λ)(R) of those centralizing λ.
   Source: explains, §2.1, Lemmas 2.1.4–2.1.5
   Note: Construction of P_G(λ), U_G(λ), Z_G(λ) over any ring.

2. Dynamic parabolic subgroup schemes
   For a smooth affine group scheme G over a ring k and a cocharacter λ:G_m→G, the functor P_G(λ) of points whose λ-conjugates have a limit at t=0 is a smooth closed subgroup scheme, with connected fibers if G has them. If G is reductive and λ factors through a maximal torus, P_G(λ) is a parabolic subgroup scheme.
   Source: explains, §2.1, Proposition 2.1.8
   Note: Proves representability, smoothness and fibral connectedness; the parabolic clause is Conrad Example 5.2.2.
