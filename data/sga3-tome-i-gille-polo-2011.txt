LOCATED CONCEPTS (5)

1. Quotient of a scheme by an equivalence relation
   Let u₀,u₁:R⇉X be morphisms of S-schemes such that (u₀,u₁):R→X×_SX is a monomorphism whose T-points form an equivalence relation on X(T) for every S-scheme T. A quotient is a morphism u:X→Y with u∘u₀=u∘u₁ that is a cokernel of (u₀,u₁) among S-schemes and for which R→X×_YX is an isomorphism; when it exists it is unique up to unique isomorphism.
   Source: explains, Exposé V §1–3 (groupoïdes, conoyaux); Milne Appendix B.1–B.6
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Existence of quotients of equivalence relations admitting a quasi-section
   Let u₀,u₁:R⇉X be an equivalence relation of S-schemes admitting a quasi-section: a subscheme U⊆X such that u₁ restricted to u₀⁻¹(U) is finite, locally free and surjective onto X, and every set of mutually equivalent points of U lies in an affine open of U. Then the quotient u:X→Y exists, is a cokernel also in ringed spaces, is surjective, satisfies R≅X×_YX, and is faithfully flat when u₀ is flat (SGA 3 V 6.1).
   Source: explains, Exposé V §6, Lemme 6.1
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

3. Generic existence of quotients by flat equivalence relations
   Let S be Noetherian and u₀,u₁:R⇉X an equivalence relation of S-schemes with u₁ flat of finite type, X of finite type over S and R→X×_SX quasi-finite. Then X has a dense saturated open subscheme W on which the induced relation admits a quasi-section, so its quotient W→V exists and is faithfully flat, open and of finite presentation (SGA 3 V 8.1).
   Source: explains, Exposé V §8, Théorème 8.1
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

4. Quotient by a finite locally free equivalence relation
   Let u₀,u₁:R⇉X be an equivalence relation of S-schemes with u₁ finite locally free such that every equivalence class lies in an affine open of X. Then the quotient p:X→Y exists; p is finite locally free, integral and open, Y is affine if X is, R≅X×_YX, Y is also the fppf sheaf quotient, and its formation commutes with base change (SGA 3 V 4.1).
   Source: explains, Exposé V §4, Théorème 4.1
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

5. Existence of the quotient of an algebraic group by an algebraic subgroup
   For an algebraic subgroup H of an algebraic group G over a field k there is a separated algebraic k-scheme G/H with a G-action and a point o∈(G/H)(k) with stabilizer H whose orbit map G→G/H is faithfully flat; it is unique up to unique isomorphism.
   Source: explains, Exposé VI_A, Théorème 3.2 and proof 3.2.1–3.2.5
   Note: Inspected proof of existence, faithful flatness and separatedness of G/H; not a priority claim.
