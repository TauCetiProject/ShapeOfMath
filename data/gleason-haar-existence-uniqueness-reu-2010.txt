LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Existence and uniqueness of Haar measure on a locally compact group
   Every locally compact Hausdorff group admits a left Haar measure, a nonzero left-invariant Radon measure (finite on compact sets, outer regular on Borel sets, inner regular on open sets), and any two left Haar measures differ by a positive constant factor.
   Source: explains, Theorems 4.3 and 4.6, pp. 6–13
   Note: Student exposition of the classical existence and uniqueness proof (covering numbers, Tychonoff, Carathéodory; Fubini and Riesz uniqueness). Not an origin claim.

2. Haar measure on a locally compact group
   A nonzero Radon measure on a locally compact group, i.e. a Borel measure finite on compact sets, outer regular on Borel sets and inner regular on open sets, invariant under left translation: μ(gA)=μ(A). A right Haar measure is defined analogously with right translations.
   Source: explains, Definitions 2.8 and 2.10, pp. 2–3
   Note: Defines left Haar measure as a nonzero left-invariant regular Borel measure with regular = finite on compacts, outer regular, inner regular on opens. Exposition; not an origin claim.
