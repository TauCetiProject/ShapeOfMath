1. Sobolev regularity for the smooth Dirichlet Poisson problem
   On a bounded smooth domainΩ, a weak zero-boundary solutionu∈H₀¹(Ω) of−Δu=f withf∈H^m(Ω),m≥0integer, belongs toH^{m+2}(Ω), with anH^{m+2} estimate controlled byf andthe domain.

2. Boundary H² regularity for second-order elliptic Dirichlet problems
   Let U⊂ℝⁿ be bounded with C² boundary and L=−∑∂_j(a^{ij}∂_i)+∑b^i∂_i+c uniformly elliptic with a^{ij}∈C¹(Ū), b^i,c∈L^∞(U). If f∈L²(U) and u∈H₀¹(U) weakly solves Lu=f, then u∈H²(U) and ‖u‖_{H²}≤C(‖f‖_{L²}+‖u‖_{L²}) with C depending only on U and the coefficients.
