LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Logarithmic S-units containing the places above ℓ
   Let K be a number field, ℓ a prime and S a finite set of finite places of K containing every place above ℓ. An element of ℤ_ℓ⊗_ℤK× is a local logarithmic unit at every finite place outside S exactly when it lies in ℤ_ℓ⊗_ℤE_S, where E_S is the group of S-units with the archimedean places adjoined to S. In particular the global logarithmic unit group lies in ℤ_ℓ⊗_ℤ(ℓ-units of K).
   Source: explains, Scolie 3.3(i), p.313
   Note: Stated with logarithmic S-units defined as the kernel of the logarithmic S-divisor map; matched to the catalogue definition through Theorem 1.4(i). No priority claim assessed.

2. Norm compatibility of global logarithmic units
   For a finite extension L/K of number fields and a prime ℓ, the map ℤ_ℓ⊗L×→ℤ_ℓ⊗K× induced by the field norm N_(L/K) sends the global logarithmic unit group of L into that of K, because logarithmic divisor maps commute with the arithmetic and logarithmic norms.
   Source: explains, Proposition 2.4 and proof, pp.309–310
   Note: Logarithmic divisor maps commute with norms. No priority claim assessed.

3. Finite generation and compactness of global logarithmic units
   For a number field K and prime ℓ, its global logarithmic unit group Ẽ_K is a finitely generated ℤ_ℓ-module. With its natural ℓ-adic module topology it is a compact Hausdorff group; field norm maps between these modules are continuous.
   Source: explains, Scolie 3.3 and Proposition 3.4, pp.313–314
   Note: Finite generation through the ℓ-unit comparison; topology not discussed there. No priority claim assessed.
