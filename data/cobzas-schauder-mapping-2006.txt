LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Schauder–Tychonoff fixed-point theorem
   Every continuous self-map of a nonempty compact convex subset of a Hausdorff locally convex real topological vector space has a fixed point.
   Source: explains, Theorem 2.3, pp. 4–5
   Note: Singbal’s proof as reproduced; the theorem is Tychonoff’s (1935).

2. Minkowski functional of a convex absorbing set
   For a convex absorbing subset B of a real vector space, p_B(x)=inf{λ>0:x∈λB}. If B is symmetric, p_B is a seminorm; for an open convex symmetric neighbourhood of 0 in a topological vector space it is continuous and B={x:p_B(x)<1}.
   Source: explains, §1, (1.4)–(1.6)
   Note: Recalled definition.
