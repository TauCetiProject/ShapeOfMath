LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Closability of a superposition of closable forms
   Let μ=∫μ_s λ(ds) be a mixture of measures and E(f,g)=∫E_s(f,g)λ(ds) for nonnegative symmetric forms E_s on a common domain D. If E_s is closable on L²(μ_s) for λ-almost every s, then E is closable on L²(μ).
   Source: explains, Lemma 3.4 (proof in Appendix A.1, same as Osada 1996 Theorem 4)
   Note: Statement in the configuration-space setting; Appendix proof not inspected.
