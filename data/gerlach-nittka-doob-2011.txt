LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Khas’minskii’s regularity theorem for strong Feller irreducible semigroups
   If a Markov transition semigroup P_t on a topological state space is strong Feller at a time s>0 and irreducible at a time t>0, meaning P_t(x,U)>0 for every state x and nonempty open U, then it is (s+t)-regular: the transition probabilities P_{s+t}(x,·) for all states x are mutually equivalent measures.
   Source: explains, §1, p.2
   Note: States Khas’minskii’s criterion; attributed there to [DPZ96] / Khas’minskii.
