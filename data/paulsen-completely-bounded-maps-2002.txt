LOCATED CONCEPTS (6)

1. Stinespring dilation theorem
   For a unital C*-algebra A and a completely positive map φ:A→B(H), there are a Hilbert space K, a star representation π:A→B(K) and a bounded V:H→K with φ(a)=V*π(a)V for all a∈A; moreover ‖φ‖=‖φ‖_cb=‖V‖²=‖φ(1)‖.
   Source: explains, Theorem 4.1, pp. 43–45
   Note: Textbook proof via the semi-inner product on A⊗H.

2. Wittstock extension theorem
   If X⊆Y are operator spaces and φ:X→B(H) is completely bounded, then φ extends to a completely bounded map Y→B(H) with the same cb norm. In particular B(H), and each matrix algebra M_n(ℂ), is injective for completely contractive maps.
   Source: explains, Theorem 8.2, pp. 99–100
   Note: Proof via the off-diagonal lemma and Arveson extension.

3. Haagerup–Paulsen–Wittstock factorization of completely bounded maps
   For a C*-algebra A and a completely bounded map φ:A→B(H), there are a star representation π:A→B(K) and bounded operators V₁,V₂:H→K with φ(a)=V₁*π(a)V₂ for all a∈A and ‖V₁‖‖V₂‖=‖φ‖_cb.
   Source: explains, Theorems 8.3–8.4, pp. 101–103
   Note: Unital case.

4. Arveson extension theorem for operator systems
   If S is an operator system in a unital C*-algebra A and φ:S→B(H) is completely positive, then φ extends to a completely positive map A→B(H); that is, B(H) is injective in the category of operator systems and completely positive maps.
   Source: explains, Theorem 7.5, p. 86
   Note: Textbook statement and proof.

5. Paulsen off-diagonal lemma
   Let X be a subspace of a unital C*-algebra A, φ:X→B a linear map into a unital C*-algebra, and S_X={[λ1,x;y*,μ1]:λ,μ∈ℂ,x,y∈X}⊆M₂(A) the Paulsen operator system. Then [λ1,x;y*,μ1]↦[λ1,φ(x);φ(y)*,μ1] is completely positive on S_X if and only if φ is completely contractive.
   Source: explains, Lemma 8.1, pp. 98–99
   Note: Textbook statement and proof (the completely contractive direction).

6. Operator system in a unital C*-algebra
   An operator system is a self-adjoint linear subspace S of a unital C*-algebra A containing the unit. Each M_n(S)⊆M_n(A) inherits a positive cone, so positive and completely positive maps on S are defined as for C*-algebras.
   Source: explains, Ch. 2, p. 9
   Note: Definition.
