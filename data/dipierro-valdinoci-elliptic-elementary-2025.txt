LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Calderón–Zygmund Dirichlet estimate on a C¹,¹ domain
   For a bounded C¹,¹ domain Ω and1<p<∞, every u∈W²,ᵖ(Ω)∩W₀¹,ᵖ(Ω) satisfies ‖u‖_{W²,ᵖ}≤C(‖Δu‖_p+‖u‖_p). The constant is uniform under domain approximations with uniformly controlled C¹,¹ charts.
   Source: explains, Theorem 5.3.4 (g=0, Laplacian) and proof, pp. 628–629
   Note: Global estimate on C¹,¹ domains; constant dependence in footnote 4.

2. Interior W²,ᵖ estimate for nondivergence-form elliptic equations
   Let a_ij be continuous and uniformly elliptic on the unit ball B₁, let b_i,c be bounded and 1<p<∞. If u∈W²,ᵖ(B₁) solves ∑a_ij∂_iju+∑b_i∂_iu+cu=f a.e. with f∈Lᵖ(B₁), then ‖u‖_{W²,ᵖ(B_{1/2})}≤C(‖u‖_{Lᵖ(B₁)}+‖f‖_{Lᵖ(B₁)}), with C depending only on n, p and the coefficients.
   Source: explains, Theorem 5.3.1 and proof, pp. 621–624
   Note: Interior Calderón–Zygmund estimate.

3. Boundary W²,ᵖ estimate on a half-ball for nondivergence-form elliptic equations
   Let a_ij be continuous and uniformly elliptic on the half-ball B₁⁺, let b_i,c be bounded and 1<p<∞. If u∈W²,ᵖ(B₁⁺) solves ∑a_ij∂_iju+∑b_i∂_iu+cu=f a.e. with f∈Lᵖ and u=g on the flat boundary in the trace sense, g∈W²,ᵖ(B₁⁺), then ‖u‖_{W²,ᵖ(B₁/₂⁺)}≤C(‖u‖_{Lᵖ}+‖g‖_{W²,ᵖ}+‖f‖_{Lᵖ}) over B₁⁺.
   Source: explains, Theorem 5.3.3 and proof, pp. 624–627
   Note: Local boundary estimate by reflection.
