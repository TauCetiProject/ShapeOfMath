LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Analytic map between analytic manifolds over a local field
   For analytic manifolds X,Y over a local field K, a map f:X→Y is analytic if it is continuous and its expressions in charts are given near every point by convergent K-power series. Its derivative T_xf at x is the K-linear map whose matrix in charts is the Jacobian of first-order coefficients; f is an analytic isomorphism if it is bijective with analytic inverse.
   Source: explains, Part II, Ch. III §§5, 8, pp. 78–82
   Note: Selected learning reference; not a priority claim.

2. Analytic inverse function theorem over a local field
   For an analytic map between finite-dimensional manifolds over R,C or a finite extension of Q_p, an invertible derivative at a point gives an analytic local inverse. A surjective derivative gives local analytic sections after choosing complementary coordinates.
   Source: explains, Part II, Ch. II pp. 73–75; Ch. III §§9–10, pp. 83–85
   Note: Exposition and proof over any complete valued field; not a priority claim.
