LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Division by a monic polynomial over a commutative ring
   For a commutative ring R, a polynomial a∈R[X] and a monic b∈R[X] of degree d, there are unique q,r∈R[X] with a=bq+r and r=0 or deg r<d. Hence R[X]/(b) is a free R-module with basis the classes of 1,X,…,X^(d−1).
   Source: explains, Thm 7.10; Example 7.39
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Degree and leading coefficient of a polynomial
   For a nonzero polynomial f=a₀+a₁X+⋯+a_nX^n over a commutative ring with a_n≠0, the degree deg f is n and the leading coefficient is a_n. By convention the zero polynomial has degree −∞.
   Source: explains, §7.2.2
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.

3. Monic polynomial over a commutative ring
   A polynomial over a commutative ring is monic if it is nonzero and its leading coefficient, the coefficient of its highest-degree term, equals 1.
   Source: explains, §7.2.2
   Note: Selected learning or proof reference; not a priority claim.
