1. Burkholder–Davis–Gundy inequalities for càdlàg local martingales at p≥1

2. Lp bound for the increasing parts of a strong supermartingale
   Let p>1 and let X be a real strong optional supermartingale on a usual filtration over [0,T] with E sup_(t≤T)|X_t|^p<∞. In its Mertens decomposition X=X₀+M−A−I, with predictable càdlàg nondecreasing A and predictable left-continuous nondecreasing purely discontinuous I starting at0, one has E A_T^p+E I_T^p≤C_p E sup_(t≤T)|X_t|^p, with C_p depending only on p.
   Source: explains, §3 Theorem3.1 pp40–41; finite-horizon p>1 shift
