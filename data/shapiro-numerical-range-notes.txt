LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Spectral inclusion in the closure of the numerical range
   For a bounded operator T on a nonzero complex Hilbert space, the spectrum σ(T) is contained in the closure of the numerical range W(T). In particular every λ∈σ(T) satisfies |λ|≤w(T).
   Source: explains, §5, Theorem 5.1 and proof, pp. 12–13
   Note: Exposition; not a priority claim.
