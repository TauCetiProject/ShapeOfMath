1. Positive stationary statistics for spatial Beverton–Holt dynamics
   Let D be a fixed primitive nonnegative d×d matrix with Dᵀ stochastic (so D is column-stochastic), c_j>0, and E_t a stationary ergodic sequence in[a,b]ᵈ with0<a≤b<∞. For X_i,t+1=∑_jD_ijE_j,t+1X_j,t/(1+c_jX_j,t), let r be the top exponent of D diag(E_t). If r>0, on the full two-sided environmental path space there is a unique strictly positive random equilibrium X*, and every nonzero nonnegative deterministic initial state has laws converging weakly to m=law(X*) and empirical state measures converging weakly to m almost surely.

2. Lyapunov continuity at uniformly supported nonnegative cocycles
   Let an ergodic homeomorphism of compact metric Ω preserve Q. Let A:Ω→M_d(ℝ) be continuous and nonnegative, with a fixed zero pattern and no zero rows. Then the top exponent γ(B) of continuous nonnegative cocycles is continuous at A in uniform matrix norm, even when A is reducible. In particular, this applies at a positive diagonal cocycle and to primitive cocycles with fixed positive entry pattern.
