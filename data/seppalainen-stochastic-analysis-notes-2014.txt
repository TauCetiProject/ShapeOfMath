LOCATED CONCEPTS (10)

1. C² Itô formula for a càdlàg semimartingale
   For a real càdlàg semimartingaleA andf∈C²(ℝ), f(A_t)=f(A_0)+∫_(0,t]f′(A_(s−))dA_s+(1/2)∫_(0,t]f″(A_(s−))d[A]^c_s+Σ_(0<s≤t)[f(A_s)−f(A_(s−))−f′(A_(s−))ΔA_s], where[A]^c is the continuous part of the quadratic variation.
   Source: explains, §6.1 Theorem6.1, pp208–210
   Note: Full selected general-cadlag proof inspected; continuous-quadratic-variation version obtained by canceling the jump atoms of the second-order term.

2. Optional sampling for a nonnegative càdlàg supermartingale
   On a usual filtration, if X is a nonnegative càdlàg supermartingale and sigma≤tau are bounded stopping times, then E[X_tau|F_sigma]≤X_sigma almost surely. No class-D assumption is needed for this bounded-time nonnegative formulation.
   Source: explains, §3.1 Theorem3.6, full proof pp89–92
   Note: Read complete finite-grid induction, dyadic approximation and truncation proof. For nonnegative supermartingale X, M=−X≤0 makes each truncation M∨c bounded, so its limit uses ordinary bounded DCT rather than the more general UI machinery in the primary.

3. Optional sampling for bounded continuous martingales
   If M is a bounded continuous martingale and σ≤τ are stopping times, then E[M_τ|F_σ]=M_σ. Unbounded-time versions use the almost-sure terminal limit of the uniformly integrable martingale.
   Source: explains, Theorem 3.6, pp. 91–92
   Note: Selected proof exposition for the finite-horizon form; not an origin claim.

4. Doob’s L² maximal inequality
   For a right-continuous square-integrable martingale M on[0,T], E sup_{s≤T}|M_s|²≤4E|M_T|².
   Source: explains, §3.2, Theorem 3.12 (Doob’s inequality) and proof, pp. 94–95, applied to |M| via Proposition 3.1(a), p. 87
   Note: Doob’s Lᵖ inequality for nonnegative right-continuous submartingales; the L² martingale case is p=2 applied to |M|. Exposition, not a priority claim.

5. Optional stopping for a right-continuous submartingale at truncated stopping times
   If M is a submartingale with right-continuous paths and σ,τ are stopping times, then for every finite T the variable M_{τ∧T} is integrable and E[M_{τ∧T}|F_σ]≥M_{σ∧τ∧T} almost surely.
   Source: explains, §3.1, Theorem 3.6 and proof, pp. 90–91
   Note: Exposition; not a priority claim.

6. Hitting times of open sets are stopping times for the right-continuous filtration
   If X is an adapted process with left- or right-continuous paths in a metric space and G is open, then τ_G=inf{t≥0:X_t∈G} is a stopping time for the filtration (F_{t+}); if (F_t) is right-continuous it is an (F_t)-stopping time.
   Source: explains, §2.1, Lemma 2.7 and proof, pp. 45–46
   Note: Exposition; not a priority claim.

7. Right-continuous submartingales remain submartingales for F_{t+}
   A submartingale with right-continuous paths with respect to a filtration (F_t) is also a submartingale with respect to the right-continuous filtration F_{t+}=∩_{u>t}F_u.
   Source: explains, §3.1, Proposition 3.2 and proof, p. 88
   Note: Exposition; not a priority claim.

8. Left Riemann-sum approximation of a semimartingale integral
   Let H be adapted càdlàg and X a càdlàg semimartingale. For deterministic partitions of mesh tending to0 on compact intervals, the left sums ∑H_(t_i)[X_(t_(i+1)∧t)−X_(t_i∧t)] converge ucp to ∫₀ᵗH_(s−)dX_s. For an adapted left-continuous locally bounded H the limit is ∫H_s dX_s.
   Source: explains, §5.3, Proposition 5.37, pp. 175–176
   Note: Full proof with stopping-time partitions; deterministic partitions are a special case.

9. Weighted Riemann-sum approximation of quadratic covariation
   For càdlàg semimartingales X,Y and adapted càdlàg G, the sums ∑G_(t_i)[X_(t_(i+1)∧t)−X_(t_i∧t)][Y_(t_(i+1)∧t)−Y_(t_i∧t)] along deterministic partitions of mesh tending to0 converge ucp to ∫₀ᵗG_(s−)d[X,Y]_s.
   Source: explains, §5.6, Proposition 5.64, pp. 200–201
   Note: Full short proof from Proposition 5.37 and Theorem 5.61.

10. Kolmogorov continuity criterion
   If a real-valued random field on[0,1]ᵈ satisfies E|X_x−X_y|ᵖ≤C|x−y|^{d+αp} for p>0,α>0, then it has a modification with locally β-Hölder paths for every0<β<α.
   Source: explains, Appendix B, Theorem B.20, pp. 376–378
   Note: Exposition and proof (Kolmogorov–Centsov criterion); not a priority claim.
