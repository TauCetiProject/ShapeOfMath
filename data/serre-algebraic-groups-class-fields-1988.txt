LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Quotient of a variety by a finite group
   Let a finite group G act on an algebraic variety V over an algebraically closed field such that every orbit lies in an affine open set, for example V quasi-projective. Then V/G exists: the projection V→V/G is finite, surjective and constant on orbits, its fibres are exactly the G-orbits, V/G is affine with coordinate ring A^G when V=Spec A is affine, k(V/G)=k(V)^G when V is irreducible, and V/G is normal when V is. If V and the action are defined over a subfield, so is V/G.
   Source: explains, Ch. III §12, Props. 18–19
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Geometric fibers of a finite group quotient of a curve
   For a finite group G acting by automorphisms on a smooth projective curve C over a characteristic-zero field k, the smooth projective curve with function field k(C)^G is its finite quotient C/G; each geometric fiber of C→C/G is exactly one G-orbit.
   Source: explains, Ch. III §12
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.
