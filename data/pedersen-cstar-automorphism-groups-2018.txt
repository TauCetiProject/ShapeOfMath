LOCATED CONCEPTS (4)

1. Upward directedness of the positive open unit ball of a C*-algebra
   In a C*-algebra A, the positive elements of norm strictly less than one form an upward directed set: for any two such a,b there is such a c with a≤c and b≤c. This yields the canonical positive contractive approximate identity.
   Source: explains, §1.4.2, pp. 15–16
   Note: First paragraph of the proof of Theorem 1.4.2.

2. Order reversal of inversion in a unital C*-algebra
   If x and y are invertible positive elements of a unital C*-algebra with x≤y, then y⁻¹≤x⁻¹.
   Source: explains, §1.3.6, p. 10
   Note: Proposition with proof.

3. C*-dynamical system
   A C*-dynamical system (A,G,α) consists of a C*-algebra A, a locally compact group G and a group homomorphism α:G→Aut(A) into the star automorphisms (bijective star homomorphisms A→A) that is continuous for pointwise norm convergence: s↦α_s(a) is norm-continuous for each a∈A.
   Source: explains, §7.4.1, pp. 311–312
   Note: Definition.

4. Reduced crossed product of a C*-dynamical system
   For a C*-dynamical system (A,G,α) with G locally compact and A⊆B(H) faithfully represented, let (π̃(a)ξ)(t)=α_(t⁻¹)(a)ξ(t) and (λ_sξ)(t)=ξ(s⁻¹t) on L²(G,H). The reduced crossed product is the norm closure of the operators ∫f(s)π̃(a)λ_s ds with a∈A, f∈C_c(G); it does not depend on the faithful representation, and λ_sπ̃(a)λ_s*=π̃(α_s(a)) implements α by unitaries.
   Source: explains, §7.7.1–7.7.5, pp. 332–334
   Note: Regular representations; reduced crossed product and independence of the faithful representation (7.7.5).
