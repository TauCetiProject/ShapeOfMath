LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Normalizer translations in a p-adic Bruhat–Tits apartment
   For a connected simply connected absolutely almost simple Q_p-group of positive split rank and a maximal split torus A, the apartment normalizer acts through its affine Weyl group with compact pointwise kernel. The translations induced by A(Q_p) form a full lattice of finite index in the apartment translation lattice; a special vertex has stabilizer whose linear action is the full finite Weyl group.
   Source: explains, §5.1.22, §5.2.1, §5.2.10 and proof
   Note: Statements of the normalizer action, translation formula and apartment fixator in the simply connected case; continues Bruhat–Tits I (1972). Not a priority claim.
