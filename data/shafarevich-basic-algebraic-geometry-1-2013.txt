LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Invariance of intersection numbers of divisors under linear equivalence
   Let V be a smooth projective variety of dimension g over an algebraically closed field k, D₁,…,D_(g−1) divisors and D∼D′ linearly equivalent divisors such that both (D₁,…,D_(g−1),D) and (D₁,…,D_(g−1),D′) have finite common support. Then ∑_v(D₁⋯D_(g−1)·D)_v=∑_v(D₁⋯D_(g−1)·D′)_v, where for effective divisors with local equations f₁,…,f_g at an isolated common point v the local multiplicity is dim_k O_(V,v)/(f₁,…,f_g), extended multilinearly to differences of effective divisors.
   Source: explains, Ch. 4 §1.3 Theorem 4.2 and proof, pp. 238-242
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Intersection number of divisors on a smooth projective variety
   For divisors D₁,…,D_g on a smooth projective variety V of dimension g over an algebraically closed field, (D₁,…,D_g) is the sum over points v of the common support of dim_k O_(V,v)/(f₁,…,f_g), f_i local equations, when the D_i are effective and meet in finitely many points; it is extended by linearity and invariance under linear equivalence to all g-tuples.
   Source: explains, Ch. 4 §§1.1, 1.4, Definitions 4.1-4.2 and the general definition, pp. 233-243
   Note: Learning reference for the definition; not a priority claim.
