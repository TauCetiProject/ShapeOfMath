LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Sarason’s identification of boundary local Dirichlet spaces with H(b)
   For ζ on the unit circle, let b be the non-extreme Schur function whose Pythagorean-pair quotient is b/a=z/(1−ζ̄z). Then the local Dirichlet space D_ζ, normed by ∥f∥²_{H²}+D_ζ(f), equals the de Branges–Rovnyak space H(b) with equality of norms.
   Source: introduces, Abstract; §1 p. 2134; Proposition 2, p. 2136
   Note: Sarason’s b_λ=(1−w₀)λ̄z/(1−w₀λ̄z) differs from the catalogue normalization b/a=z/(1−ζ̄z) by the unimodular factor λ̄, which does not change H(b) or its norm.
   Origin evidence: Checked p. 2134: Sarason states that the identification of D(δ_λ) with de Branges–Rovnyak spaces up to equivalent norms was noted by Richter–Sundberg [4, Prop. 2.4] and that ‘the novelty here is that the identification is norm preserving’; Proposition 2 proves the isometric equality.
