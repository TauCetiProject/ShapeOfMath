REQUIRED CONCEPT FRONTIER (12)

- Alternating tensors in the tensor square of a module: For an R-module M, the submodule of M⊗_R M consisting of tensors t with flip(t)=−t. This is a submodule convention, not the quotient definition of the exterior square; in characteristic 2 these conventions need not agree.
- Coefficientwise congruence of formal series: Two formal series with coefficients in a commutative ring R are congruent modulo an ideal I when every pair of corresponding coefficients differs by an element of I. Reduction applies R→R/I to each coefficient.
- Gritsenko–Nikulin’s product formulas for Igusa cusp forms: Write q=e^(2πiz₁), r=e^(2πiz₂), s=e^(2πiz₃), and let f(D) be the discriminant coefficients of φ₀,₁. The integrally normalized Igusa forms satisfy X₁₀=qrs∏(1−qⁿrˡsᵐ)^(2f(4nm−l²)) and X₃₅=q²rs²(q−s)∏(1−qⁿrˡsᵐ)^(f₂(4nm−l²)), where f₂(N)=8f(4N)+2((−N/2)−1)f(N)+f(N/4), using the Kronecker symbol and f(N/4)=0 unless 4 divides N. Products range over n,m≥0, arbitrary l when n+m>0, and l<0 when n=m=0; they converge near the standard cusp.
- Localization of a commutative ring: For a multiplicatively closed subset S of a commutative ring R containing 1, S⁻¹R consists of fractions r/s, identifying r/s with r′/s′ when t(rs′−r′s)=0 for some t in S, with the usual fraction operations.
- Nagaoka’s genus-two modular-form structure modulo 2 and 3: For p=2 or 3, every reduction modulo p of an integral even-weight full-group genus-two Siegel modular form is a unique polynomial over F_p in the reductions of Igusa’s X₁₀,Y₁₂,X₁₆, after forgetting the weight grading.
- Prime ideal of a commutative ring: A proper ideal P of a commutative ring R such that ab in P implies a in P or b in P.
- Ramanujan delta modular form: The normalized weight-12 cusp form Δ=(E₄³−E₆²)/1728=q∏_{n≥1}(1−q^n)^24 for SL₂(Z), where q=exp(2πiτ).
- Ring of integers of a number field: The subring O_K of a number field K consisting of its elements integral over Z, namely the roots in K of monic integer-coefficient polynomials.
- Siegel modular form for a congruence subgroup: A holomorphic function f on the degree-g Siegel upper half-space transforming as f((AZ+B)(CZ+D)⁻¹)=det(CZ+D)^k f(Z) under a congruence subgroup Γ⊂Sp(2g,Z), with holomorphy at all cusps; for g>1 the usual Koecher principle makes the cusp condition automatic.
- Sturm’s congruence bound for elliptic modular forms: For a weight-k holomorphic modular form on SL₂(ℤ) with p-integral rational Fourier coefficients a(n), vanishing a(n) modulo p for 0≤n≤⌊k/12⌋ forces all coefficients to vanish modulo p.
- Symmetric square of a module: For a module M over a commutative ring R, Sym²_R(M) is (M⊗_R M) modulo the relations x⊗y−y⊗x. In small characteristic this differs from the submodule of M⊗M generated by x⊗x; the paper explicitly uses the latter convention in its integral Witt targets.
- Witt operators for genus-two Siegel modular forms: The restriction of a genus-two modular form to block-diagonal matrices and its first two normalized transverse Taylor coefficients: f([[τ₁,z],[z,τ₂]])=W(f)+2W′(f)(2πiz)+W″(f)(2πiz)²+O(z³). Their targets are tensor products of elliptic modular or quasimodular form spaces, with parity determined by the weight.

LOCATED CONCEPTS (24)

1. Kikuta–Takemori’s sharp genus-two Sturm bound
   Let f be a scalar full-group degree-two Siegel modular form of weight k≥0 with coefficients in O_{K,𝔭}. Put b_k=⌊k/10⌋ for even k and ⌊(k−5)/10⌋ for odd k. If the coefficients indexed by T=(m,r/2;r/2,n)≥0 with m,n≤b_k vanish modulo 𝔭^ν, then all coefficients do, for every prime ideal 𝔭 and ν≥1. This bound is optimal whenever the weight-k space is nonzero.
   Source: introduces, Theorems 2.1, 2.4; §§5.1–5.5
   Note: Original odd-weight sharp extension; even-weight p≥5 case credited to Choi–Choie–Kikuta; p=2,3 supplied with corrected proof, not a second origin of the earlier statement.
