LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Morita equivalence for a full matrix algebra over a field
   For a field F and n≥1, the functor W↦F^n⊗_FW is an equivalence from F-vector spaces to left M_n(F)-modules, with inverse M↦e₁₁M. The natural evaluation maps give inverse functor isomorphisms.
   Source: explains, §17B, Theorem 17.20, pp. 470–471
   Note: Explicit equivalence U↦UE₁₁, V↦V^(n) for right modules over M_n(S).
