1. Sylow7-adic image for a non-CM rational7-isogeny
   If E/ℚ is geometrically non-CM and has a ℚ-rational isogeny of degree7, then its closed7-adic Tate image contains a Sylow pro-7 subgroup of GL₂(ℤ₇).
