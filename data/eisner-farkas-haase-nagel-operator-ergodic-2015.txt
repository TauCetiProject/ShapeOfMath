1. Maximal ergodic inequality for a nonnegative function
   If T preserves probability μ, g≥0 belongs to L¹(μ), and r>0, then μ{sup_(n≥1)n⁻¹∑_(j=0)^(n−1)g(T^jx)>r}≤‖g‖₁/r.
   Source: explains, Ch.11 §11.2, Theorem 11.11 (Maximal Ergodic Theorem) and Corollary 11.12 (Maximal Inequality), pp.203–205
   Note: Proves the inequality for positive Dunford–Schwartz operators, which includes Koopman operators of measure-preserving systems; p=1 gives the catalogue statement. Exposition only; the text credits Hopf (1954), Yosida–Kakutani (1939) and Garsia (1965), so no priority is assigned here.
