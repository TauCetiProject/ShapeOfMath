LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Essential spectral radius of the Banach-space adjoint
   For a bounded operator Q on a complex Banach space B and its adjoint Q′ on the dual space B*, the essential spectral radii agree: r_ess(Q′)=r_ess(Q). Since Q and Q′ also have the same spectral radius, Q is quasi-compact exactly when Q′ is.
   Source: explains, Corollary II.1(iii), p. 3; proof in Section V.1, p. 29
   Note: Proof via compact adjoints and the bidual embedding.

2. Essential spectral radius of a bounded operator
   For a bounded operator L on a complex Banach space, r_ess(L)=lim_n(inf_K‖L^n−K‖)^{1/n}, the infimum being over compact operators K. Equivalently it is the infimum of r₀ such that the spectrum of L outside the closed disc of radius r₀ consists of finitely many isolated eigenvalues with finite-dimensional generalized eigenspaces.
   Source: explains, Definition II.1 and Theorem II.1 (Nussbaum's formula), pp. 2-3
   Note: Both characterizations of r_ess.

3. Peripheral decomposition of a quasi-compact Markov kernel on a weighted-supremum space
   Let P be a Markov kernel with Pw/w bounded for a weight w≥1. If P is quasi-compact on B_w, then r(P)=1 and P=L+N with LN=NL=0, r(N)<1 and Lf=∑_{k=1}^sλ_kφ_k(f)f_k, where |λ_k|=1, the f_k are bounded measurable eigenfunctions (Pf_k=λ_kf_k), the φ_k are complex measures with ∫w d|φ_k|<∞, and φ_j(f_k)=δ_{jk}. In particular P is power-bounded on B_w.
   Source: explains, Lemma IV.5 and its proof, pp. 24-25
