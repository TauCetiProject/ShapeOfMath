LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Nonexplosion of finite-mean continuous-time Galton–Watson branching
   For independent rate-β lifetimes and a finite-mean integer offspring law, a continuous-time Galton–Watson process started from finitely many individuals has finitely many individuals and branching events on each bounded time interval almost surely.
   Source: explains, Theorem 3.1(b), Corollary 3.3 and Lemma 1.3, pp. 8–9, 14–15
   Note: General CMJ nonexplosion criterion specializing to this theorem; not a priority claim.
