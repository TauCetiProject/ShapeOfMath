REQUIRED CONCEPT FRONTIER (10)

- Banach contraction mapping theorem: Every map T:X→X on a nonempty complete metric space satisfying d(Tx,Ty)≤q d(x,y) for a fixed 0≤q<1 has a unique fixed point, and Tⁿx converges to it for every x.
- Endpoint traces of one-dimensional Sobolev functions: Every u∈H^N(0,1;𝕂ᵈ), N≥1, has continuous representatives for derivatives of order j<N and bounded linear traces u↦u^(j)(0),u^(j)(1). The complete endpoint jet map onto 𝕂^(2Nd) is surjective.
- Fundamental theorem for Banach-valued Sobolev functions: If u and its weak time derivative u′ belong to L¹(0,T;X) for a Banach space X, then u has an absolutely continuous representative satisfying u(t)−u(s)=∫_s^tu′(r)dr.
- Impedance passive boundary control and observation system: A boundary system (A,B,C) with state Hilbert space X and matching input/output space U is impedance passive if Re⟨Ax,x⟩_X≤Re⟨Bx,Cx⟩_U for every x∈D(A), where B and C are its boundary input and output maps.
- Komura–Kato generation theorem in Hilbert space: An m-dissipative relation A on a real Hilbert space generates a unique strongly continuous nonlinear contraction semigroup on the closure of D(A); for initial values in D(A), the orbit solves x′(t)∈Ax(t) and its right derivative is the minimal-norm section of A.
- Linear port-Hamiltonian differential operator on an interval: On L²((0,1);ℝᵈ), a port-Hamiltonian operator has Ax=∑_{k=0}^N P_k(Hx)^(k), with H bounded, symmetric and uniformly positive, P_N invertible, P_kᵀ=(−1)^{k+1}P_k for k≥1 and P₀+P₀ᵀ≤0; its maximal domain requires Hx∈H^N.
- Lipschitz monotone perturbation of a maximal monotone relation: If A is maximal monotone on a Hilbert space and B:H→H is monotone and globally Lipschitz, then A+B is maximal monotone.
- Matrix exponential: For a finite square real or complex matrix A, exp(A)=∑_{n≥0}Aⁿ/n!, with convergence in any matrix norm.
- Minty surjectivity theorem in Hilbert space: A monotone relation A on a Hilbert space is maximal monotone if and only if ran(I+λA)=H for one, equivalently every, λ>0.
- One-dimensional Sobolev interpolation into derivative sup norms: For integers 0≤j<N, there is C_{N,j} such that ||u^(j)||∞≤C_{N,j}(||u||₂+||u^(N)||₂)^a||u||₂^(1−a) for u∈H^N(0,1), where a=(j+1/2)/N.

1. Komura–Kato generation theorem in Hilbert space
   An m-dissipative relation A on a real Hilbert space generates a unique strongly continuous nonlinear contraction semigroup on the closure of D(A); for initial values in D(A), the orbit solves x′(t)∈Ax(t) and its right derivative is the minimal-norm section of A.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem2.9
   Note: Nonlinear generation theorem, imported.

2. Maximal monotone relation on a real Hilbert space
   A relation M⊆H×H on a real Hilbert space is monotone if ⟨u−v,x−y⟩≥0 for u∈Mx,v∈My, and maximal monotone if it has no proper monotone extension.
   Source: relies_on, §2,§4,§6
   Note: Boundary relations and controllers.

3. Linear port-Hamiltonian differential operator on an interval
   On L²((0,1);ℝᵈ), a port-Hamiltonian operator has Ax=∑_{k=0}^N P_k(Hx)^(k), with H bounded, symmetric and uniformly positive, P_N invertible, P_kᵀ=(−1)^{k+1}P_k for k≥1 and P₀+P₀ᵀ≤0; its maximal domain requires Hx∈H^N.
   Source: relies_on, Definition3.1
   Note: Differential system and Sobolev trace data.

4. Impedance passive boundary control and observation system
   A boundary system (A,B,C) with state Hilbert space X and matching input/output space U is impedance passive if Re⟨Ax,x⟩_X≤Re⟨Bx,Cx⟩_U for every x∈D(A), where B and C are its boundary input and output maps.
   Source: relies_on, §3–9
   Note: Energy inequality for static/dynamic interconnection.

5. Matrix exponential
   For a finite square real or complex matrix A, exp(A)=∑_{n≥0}Aⁿ/n!, with convergence in any matrix norm.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma3.7
   Note: Resolvent ODE solution.

6. Fundamental theorem for Banach-valued Sobolev functions
   If u and its weak time derivative u′ belong to L¹(0,T;X) for a Banach space X, then u has an absolutely continuous representative satisfying u(t)−u(s)=∫_s^tu′(r)dr.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem5.1;§7–9
   Note: Differentiating/integrating Lipschitz trajectories.
