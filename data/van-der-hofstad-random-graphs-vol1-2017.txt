1. Extinction of a subcritical Galton–Watson process
   If the offspring mean m satisfies m<1, a Galton–Watson process started from finitely many individuals becomes extinct almost surely; since E Z_n=Z_0mⁿ, its total progeny has finite mean Z_0/(1−m).
