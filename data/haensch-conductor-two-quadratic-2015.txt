REQUIRED CONCEPT FRONTIER (22)

- Duke–Schulze-Pillot ternary spinor-genus representation theorem: For a positive definite integral ternary quadratic ℤ-lattice L, every sufficiently large positive integer n that is primitively represented by some lattice in its proper spinor genus is represented by L itself. The conclusion asserts integral representation and does not require that L’s representing vector be primitive.
- Existence of a Jordan splitting over ℤ_p: Every nondegenerate quadratic ℤ_p-lattice has a Jordan decomposition. At oddp it can be split into orthogonal rankone blocks; atp2 one may require ranktwo modular blocks as well as rankone blocks before grouping equal modularities.
- Hilbert reciprocity over the rational numbers: For any a,b∈ℚ×, (a,b)_v=1 for all but finitely many rational places v, and ∏_v(a,b)_v=1, where v ranges over the finite primes and the real place with ℚ_∞=ℝ.
- Imaginary quadratic fields unramified outside two: An imaginary quadratic extension E/ℚ is unramified at every odd finite prime if and only if E=ℚ(i) or E=ℚ(√−2).
- Infinitely many prime-square nonrepresentations from a squarefree spinor exception: Let L be a positive definite integral ternary quadratic ℤ-lattice, and let t>0 be squarefree and a primitive spinor exception of its genus. If L does not represent t, then L fails to represent tq² for infinitely many rational primes q.
- Integral reflection criterion for a primitive local lattice vector: Let L be a quadratic ℤ_p-lattice in V and let v∈L be primitive with Q(v)≠0. The reflection τ_v(x)=x−2B(x,v)v/Q(v) preserves L if and only if2B(v,L)⊆Q(v)ℤ_p.
- Integral spinor norms of a unary and an even binary two-adic lattice: Put F=ℚ₂, S=(F×)² and U=ℤ₂×. Let L=⟨ε⟩⊥2^rB with r≥0 and ε,η∈U, where the binary form B is Q_B(x,y)=2ηxy or2η(x²+xy+y²). The inverse image in F× of θ(SO(L)) is US when r is odd and F× when r is even.
- Integral spinor norms of distinct-scale diagonal ternary two-adic lattices: Put F=ℚ₂ and let L=⟨1,2^iγ,2^jμ⟩ with 0<i<j and γ,μ∈ℤ₂×. Identify spinor images with inverse images in F×. If {i,j−i} meets{1,3} and {i,j,j−i} meets{2,4}, then θ(SO(L))=F×. Otherwise θ(SO(L)) is the product of θ(SO(⟨1,2^iγ⟩)) and θ(SO(⟨1,2^(j−i)γμ⟩)).
- Integral spinor norms of separated diagonal binary two-adic lattices: Put F=ℚ₂, S=(F×)², U=ℤ₂× and H_d={γ∈F×:(γ,d)₂=1}. For L=⟨1,2^rα⟩ with r≥1 and α∈U, identify θ(SO(L)) with its inverse image in F×. This image equals H_(−2α) for r=1 or3; H_(−α)∩US for r=2; S∪αS∪5S∪5αS for r=4; and S∪2^rαS for r≥5.
- Integral spinor norms of unary and equal-scale diagonal binary two-adic lattices: Put F=ℚ₂, S=(F×)², U=ℤ₂× and H_d={c∈F×:(c,d)₂=1}. For L=⟨ε⟩⊥2^r⟨γ,μ⟩ with r≥1 and ε,γ,μ∈U, identify θ(SO(L)) with its inverse image in F×. If γμ≡3 mod4, this image is US for r even and F× for r odd. If γμ≡1 mod4, it is H_(−γμ) when r≥4 and (2^rεγ,−γμ)₂=1, and F× otherwise.
- Isometry criterion for two-adic quadratic lattices with equal Jordan invariants: Let L,K be lattices in the same nondegenerate ℚ₂-quadratic space with identical Jordan invariants (t,ranks,s_i,n_i=2^(u_i)ℤ₂), and let L_(i),K_(i) be their first i Jordan blocks. Then L≅K overℤ₂ exactly when, for each i<t, the square class d(L_(i))/d(K_(i)) has a representative congruent to1 modulo n_i n_(i+1)/s_i², and ℚ₂K_(i)⊥⟨2^(u_i)⟩ contains a subspace isometric to ℚ₂L_(i) whenever n_(i+1)⊆4n_i. Here d is the Gram determinant modulo unit squares.
- Legendre symbol: For an odd prime p and an integer a, the Legendre symbol (a/p) is zero if p divides a, one if a is a nonzero square modulo p, and minus one otherwise.
- Normal form of an odd-prime universal ternary lattice: Let p be odd and L a nondegenerate quadratic ℤ_p-lattice of rank3 with Q(L)⊆ℤ_p. If Q(L)=ℤ_p, then L is isometric to⟨1,−1,−d⟩, where d is its Gram determinant up to a unit square. In particular its underlying ℚ_p-space is isotropic and θ(SO(L)) contains every unit square class.
- Normal forms of unimodular two-adic quadratic lattices: A positive-rank unimodularℤ₂-quadratic lattice with norm idealℤ₂ has an orthogonal basis of unit-norm vectors. An even unimodular lattice has rank2m with m≥1 and is isometric to H^⊥m or H^⊥(m−1)⊥A, where H and A have Gram matrices[[0,1],[1,0]] and[[2,1],[1,2]], respectively.
- Odd part of a nonzero rational number: The odd part of a nonzero rational a is a′=2^(−v₂(a))a, a unit ofℤ₂. If a is a nonzero integer, a′ is an odd integer; its residue modulo8 is therefore well-defined. This removes the entire2-power, not the odd square factors.
- Orthogonal complement of a vector in a quadratic lattice: For a quadratic ℤ_p-lattice L and a nonisotropic vector v in its ℚ_p-span, the lattice orthogonal complement of v is L∩v⊥={x∈L:B(v,x)=0}. Its rational span is v⊥; v need not itself belong toL.
- Parity of anisotropic places of a rational ternary quadratic form: For a nondegenerate ternary quadratic form over ℚ, the set of places v at which the same form over ℚ_v is anisotropic is finite and has even cardinality, including the real place among the places. Consequently a positive definite ternary form isotropic at every odd prime is anisotropic at2.
- Shift invariance of a normalized quadratic coset polynomial: Let N be a quadraticℤ-lattice,ν∈ℚN andh_ν(x)=Q(x)+2B(ν,x), with h_ν(N)⊆ℤ generating dℤ for d>0. For x₀∈N putω=ν+x₀. Then h_ω(N) generates the same ideal, and h_ω(x)/d is almost universal exactly when h_ν(x)/d is.
- Square criterion in a p-adic field Q_p: A nonzero p-adic rational a=p^v u with u∈ℤ_p× is a square in ℚ_p exactly when v is even and, for odd p, the residue of u is a square modulo p; for p=2 the condition on u is u≡1 mod 8.
- Unit local spinor norms imply one proper ternary spinor genus: Let L be a positive definite ternary quadratic ℤ-lattice. If for every finite prime p the group θ(SO(L_p)) contains the square class of every unit of ℤ_p, then the genus of L contains exactly one proper spinor genus.
- Unit norm criterion for unramified local abelian extensions: Let K be a finite extension ofℚ_p and L/K a finite abelian extension. Then O_K×⊆N_(L/K)(L×) if and only if L/K is unramified. The trivial extension is included.
- Unit representation and integral rotation spinor norms: Let L be a quadratic ℤ_p-lattice of rank at least2 with Q(L)⊆ℤ_p. If L represents every unit of ℤ_p, then θ(SO(L)) contains the square class of every unit of ℤ_p.

LOCATED CONCEPTS (38)

1. Coset of a quadratic lattice
   For a quadratic ℤ-lattice N and ν in its rational span, the coset ν+N carries the values Q(ν+x)=Q(ν)+2B(ν,x)+Q(x). Its conductor is the least positive integer m such that mν∈N.
   Source: relies_on, §1
   Note: The conductor-two coset encodes inhomogeneous representations.

2. Almost universal integer-valued polynomial
   An integer-valued polynomial on ℤⁿ is almost universal if it represents every sufficiently large positive integer at an integral argument.
   Source: relies_on, Abstract; §1
   Note: Defines the target representation property.

3. Norm ideal of a quadratic lattice
   The norm ideal n(N) of a quadratic lattice over ℤ or ℤ_p is the fractional ideal generated by all values Q(x) with x∈N; it is integral when the quadratic form is integer-valued.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem 1; §§2–3
   Note: Norm ideals control local possibilities.

4. Scale ideal of a quadratic lattice
   The scale ideal s(N) of a quadratic lattice over ℤ or ℤ_p is the fractional ideal generated by B(x,y) for x,y∈N, where B is the half-polarization of Q.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem 1; §§2–3
   Note: The 2-adic scale versus norm distinction is essential.

5. Discriminant square class of a quadratic lattice
   For a basis e₁,…,e_n of a quadratic lattice, its discriminant is det(B(e_i,e_j)), regarded up to multiplication by the square of a unit of the coefficient ring. Its rational square class is independent of the chosen basis.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem 1; §§2–3
   Note: Discriminant valuation and square-free part enter every case.

6. Primitive spinor exception of a positive definite ternary genus
   A positive integer t is a primitive spinor exception of a positive definite ternary lattice genus if it is represented primitively by the genus but not primitively by every proper spinor genus in it.
   Source: relies_on, §§2–3
   Note: The global obstruction is a primitive spinor-exception square class.

7. Spinor norm of an orthogonal transformation
   For a nondegenerate quadratic space over F of characteristic different from two, the spinor norm θ:O(V)→F×/(F×)² sends a product of reflections τ_(v₁)⋯τ_(v_r) to the square class of ∏Q(v_i). This is independent of the reflection factorization.
   Source: relies_on, §§2–3
   Note: Computations use integral rotations and local spinor norms.

8. Norm subgroup of a finite field extension
   For a finite field extension L/K, its norm subgroup is N_(L/K)(L×)⊆K×, the image of the multiplicative group under the field norm.
   Source: relies_on, §2; §3
   Note: Local norm groups for Q(i) and Q(√−2) bound spinor norms.

9. Hilbert symbol of a local field
   For a local field F of characteristic different from two and a,b∈F×, the Hilbert symbol (a,b)_F is +1 if ax²+by²=z² has a nonzero solution in F³ and −1 otherwise; it depends only on the square classes of a and b.
   Source: relies_on, §3, p. 14
   Note: Identifies the local norm subgroup in the even discriminant cases.

10. Square criterion in a p-adic field Q_p
   A nonzero p-adic rational a=p^v u with u∈ℤ_p× is a square in ℚ_p exactly when v is even and, for odd p, the residue of u is a square modulo p; for p=2 the condition on u is u≡1 mod 8.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma 12
   Note: Imported local square theorem.

39. Isometry criterion for two-adic quadratic lattices with equal Jordan invariants

ADDITIONAL PREREQUISITES (133)

- Abelian group [definition]: A group whose operation is commutative.
- Absolute value on a field [definition]: A map |·|:K→R_{≥0} that vanishes exactly at zero, is multiplicative and satisfies |x+y|≤|x|+|y|. It is nonarchimedean if the stronger bound |x+y|≤max(|x|,|y|) holds.
- Adele ring of a number field [definition]: The restricted product A_F=∏′_v F_v of all completions of a number field,where almost every finite component lies in the valuation ring O_v.
- Algebraic closure of a field [definition]: An algebraic extension of a field that is itself algebraically closed. This specifies an object, without including the existence or uniqueness-up-to-isomorphism theorem.
- Algebraic element of a field extension [definition]: An element α of L/F is algebraic over F if a nonzero polynomial in F[x] vanishes at α.
- Algebraically closed field [definition]: A field in which every nonconstant polynomial has a root.
- Associative algebra over a field [definition]: A vector space with an associative bilinear multiplication and a multiplicative identity.
- Basis of a free module [definition]: A family (e_i) in a module over a commutative ring such that every element is a unique finite linear combination of the e_i with coefficients in that ring. A module with such a family is free.
- Basis of a vector space [definition]: A linearly independent family of vectors whose linear span is the whole vector space.
- Bijection between sets [definition]: A function for which each element of the codomain is the image of exactly one element of the domain.
- Bilinear map [definition]: A map of two vector-space variables that is linear in each variable separately.
- Binary operation on a set [definition]: A function S×S→S.
- Binary relation on a set [definition]: A subset R⊆S×S; writing xRy means (x,y)∈R.
- Cartesian product of sets [definition]: A×B is the set of ordered pairs (a,b) with a∈A and b∈B.
- Cauchy sequence in a metric space [definition]: A sequence (xₙ) in a metric space (X,d) is Cauchy if for every ε>0 there is N such that d(xₙ,xₘ)<ε whenever n,m≥N.
- Closure of a subset of a topological space [definition]: The closure of A is the intersection of all closed subsets containing A; a set is closed when its complement is open.
- Commutative ring with identity [definition]: An abelian group under addition, together with an associative commutative multiplication with identity, distributing over addition. The zero ring is allowed.
- Compact topological space [definition]: A topological space in which every open cover has a finite subcover.
- Complement of a subset [definition]: For A⊆Ω, its complement Ω∖A consists of the elements of Ω that do not belong to A.
- Complete discretely valued field [definition]: A field with a discrete valuation v which is complete for the metric defined by any absolute value c^(−v(x)) with c>1, extended by |0|=0.
- Complete metric space [definition]: A metric space is complete if every Cauchy sequence in it converges to a point of that same space.
- Completion of a field with an absolute value [definition]: The metric completion of K for d(x,y)=|x−y|, with the field operations extended continuously. A completion is regarded as containing K through its canonical isometric embedding.
- Completion of a metric space [definition]: A complete metric space containing an isometric dense copy of the given metric space. It is unique up to an isometry fixing that copy.
- Complex number [definition]: A complex number is a pair of real numbers, written a+bi. Addition is componentwise and multiplication is (a+bi)(c+di)=(ac−bd)+(ad+bc)i, so i²=−1.
- Composition of functions [definition]: For f:A→B and g:B→C, the composite g∘f:A→C sends x to g(f(x)).
- Congruence modulo an integer [definition]: The relation a≡b (mod m) means that m divides a−b.
- Continuous map between topological spaces [definition]: A map is continuous if the preimage of every open subset of its target is open in its domain.
- Convergence of a sequence in a metric space [definition]: A sequence (xₙ) in a metric space (X,d) converges to x∈X if for every ε>0 there is N such that d(xₙ,x)<ε whenever n≥N.
- Dense subset of a metric space [definition]: A subset A of a metric space X is dense if every open ball centered in X with positive radius meets A.
- Determinant of a square matrix [definition]: For an n×n matrix A over a commutative ring, det A=∑_{σ∈Sₙ}sgn(σ)∏_{i=1}ⁿA_{i,σ(i)}, with the 0×0 determinant equal to one.
- Direct sum of vector spaces [definition]: The algebraic direct sum of a family of vector spaces consists of tuples with only finitely many nonzero coordinates, with coordinatewise operations.
- Discrete valuation on a field [definition]: A surjective map v:K×→Z satisfying v(xy)=v(x)+v(y) and v(x+y)≥min(v(x),v(y)), extended by v(0)=+∞.
- Disjoint sets [definition]: Two sets are disjoint when they have no common element; a family is pairwise disjoint when every two distinct members are disjoint.
- Divisibility of integers [definition]: An integer a divides b if b=ac for some integer c.
- Dual of a local quadratic lattice [definition]: For a nondegenerate quadratic ℤ_p-lattice L inside its ℚ_p-span V with half-polarization B, its dual is L#={v∈V:B(v,L)⊆ℤ_p}. The dual is taken with respect to B, so a half-integral polarization convention changes it.
- Field [definition]: A commutative ring in which 1≠0 and every nonzero element is invertible.
- Field extension [definition]: An inclusion of one field into another.
- Field of p-adic numbers [definition]: For a prime p, ℚ_p is the completion of ℚ for the p-adic absolute value |x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}.
- Finite field [definition]: A field whose underlying set is finite.
- Finite set [definition]: A set X is finite if it is bijective with {1,…,n} for some natural number n≥0; its cardinality is n.
- Finite unramified extension of a nonarchimedean local field [definition]: A finite extension of a complete discretely valued field whose ramification index is one and whose residue-field extension is separable.
- Finite-dimensional vector space [definition]: A vector space having a finite basis.
- Free abelian group [definition]: An abelian group with a family of elements such that every group element is a unique finite integer linear combination of them; the number of basis elements is its rank.
- Function between sets [definition]: An assignment taking each element of a specified domain set to exactly one element of a specified codomain set.
- Fundamental theorem of arithmetic [theorem]: Every integer n≥2 factors as a product of primes uniquely up to ordering.
- Galois field extension [definition]: An algebraic field extension that is both normal and separable.
- General linear group over a commutative ring [definition]: The group GL_n(R) of square matrices over a commutative ring R having a two-sided matrix inverse, with matrix multiplication.
- Genus of a quadratic Z-lattice [definition]: The genus of a quadratic ℤ-lattice N consists of the isometry classes of lattices whose rational quadratic space is isometric to that of N and which are isometric to N over ℤ_p for every prime p and over ℝ.
- Group [definition]: A set with an associative binary operation, an identity element, and a two-sided inverse for each element.
- Group action on a set [definition]: A map G×X→X, written (g,x)↦gx, satisfying ex=x and (gh)x=g(hx).
- Group homomorphism [definition]: A map f:G→H satisfying f(xy)=f(x)f(y) for all x,y.
- Hausdorff topological space [definition]: A topological space in which any two distinct points have disjoint open neighbourhoods.
- Homomorphism of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive map f:M→N of modules over the same commutative ring satisfying f(rm)=rf(m).
- Ideal of a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup I of a commutative ring R such that ri belongs to I for every r in R and i in I.
- Imaginary quadratic number field [definition]: An imaginary quadratic number field is a degree-two extension ofℚ with no embedding intoℝ, equivalently K=ℚ(√D) for a negative square-free integerD. A chosen embeddingK⊂ℂ fixes its complex realization.
- Integer [definition]: An integer is an element of the ordered ring ℤ, obtained by adjoining additive inverses to the natural numbers.
- Intersection of a family of sets [definition]: Inside a specified ambient set, the elements belonging to every member of the family.
- Invertible element of an algebra [definition]: An element with a two-sided multiplicative inverse.
- Irreducible polynomial [definition]: A nonzero nonunit polynomial over a specified coefficient ring that cannot be written as a product of two nonunits. Over a field, both factors would have smaller positive degree.
- Isometry of quadratic spaces and lattices [definition]: An isometry of quadratic spaces is a bijective linear map preserving Q. An isometry of quadratic lattices also carries the underlying lattice onto the target lattice.
- Isotropic and anisotropic quadratic spaces [definition]: A quadratic space is isotropic if it has a nonzero vector x with Q(x)=0; otherwise it is anisotropic.
- Jordan invariants of a two-adic quadratic lattice [definition]: For a nondegenerate quadratic ℤ₂-lattice with Jordan decomposition L=L₁⊥⋯⊥L_t ordered by increasing scale, its Jordan invariants are the number t, the ranks of the blocks, their scale ideals s_i=s(L_i), and their norm ideals n_i=n(L_i). Write n_i=2^(u_i)ℤ₂ and L_(i)=L₁⊥⋯⊥L_i. These ideal/rank data are independent of the chosen Jordan splitting; the actual blocks need not be isometric across splittings.
- Least upper bound in a partially ordered set [definition]: For A⊆P, its least upper bound is an upper bound s satisfying s≤b for every upper bound b of A, when such an s exists.
- Linear combination of vectors [definition]: A finite sum a₁v₁+⋯+aₙvₙ of vectors multiplied by scalars from their vector space’s ground field; the empty sum is zero.
- Linear map between vector spaces [definition]: A map between vector spaces over the same field preserving addition and scalar multiplication.
- Linear span of a set of vectors [definition]: The subspace consisting of all finite linear combinations of a specified set of vectors.
- Linearly independent family of vectors [definition]: A family of vectors is linearly independent if every finite linear combination equalling zero has all coefficients zero.
- Local field [definition]: A nondiscrete locally compact Hausdorff topological field. Both archimedean and nonarchimedean local fields are allowed.
- Localization of a quadratic Z-lattice [definition]: For a quadratic ℤ-lattice N and a prime p, its localization N_p=ℤ_p⊗_ℤN is a quadratic ℤ_p-lattice with the scalar-extended form inside ℚ_p⊗_ℤN.
- Locally compact Hausdorff space [definition]: A Hausdorff topological space in which every point has a neighbourhood with compact closure.
- Matrix over a ring [definition]: A rectangular array of elements of a ring; addition is entrywise and matrix multiplication uses row-by-column sums.
- Metric space [definition]: A set X with a function d:X×X→[0,∞) such that d(x,y)=0 exactly when x=y, d(x,y)=d(y,x), and d(x,z)≤d(x,y)+d(y,z).
- Minimal polynomial of an algebraic element [definition]: The unique monic polynomial of least positive degree over F vanishing at an algebraic element α; it generates the ideal of all polynomials over F vanishing at α.
- Modular quadratic lattice over ℤ_p [definition]: A nondegenerate quadratic ℤ_p-lattice L is pᵃ-modular, a∈ℤ, if L#=p^(−a)L inside its rational span. Equivalently its Gram matrix divided by pᵃ is invertible overℤ_p. The case a=0 is unimodular.
- Module over a commutative ring [definition]: An abelian group M with an action of a commutative ring R satisfying r(m+n)=rm+rn, (r+s)m=rm+sm, (rs)m=r(sm), and 1m=m. Unlike a vector space, its scalar ring need not be a field.
- Multiplicative group of a field [definition]: For a field F, F× is the abelian group of its nonzero elements under multiplication.
- Multiplicity of a polynomial root [definition]: The multiplicity of a root a of a polynomial P is the largest m≥1 for which (x−a)^m divides P. A root of multiplicity1 is simple.
- Natural number [definition]: One of 0, 1, 2, …; natural numbers count finite collections.
- Nonarchimedean local field [definition]: A field complete for a nontrivial discrete valuation and having a finite residue field.
- Nondegenerate quadratic space [definition]: A quadratic space (V,Q) over a field of characteristic different from two is nondegenerate if its polarized bilinear form has zero radical: B(x,y)=0 for every y forces x=0.
- Norm in a finite field extension [definition]: For a finite field extension L/K, N_(L/K)(a) is the determinant of the K-linear multiplication map x↦ax on L.
- Normal algebraic field extension [definition]: An algebraic field extension L/k is normal if every irreducible polynomial in k[X] with a root in L splits into linear factors over L. Equivalently, the minimal polynomial over k of every element of L splits over L.
- Number field [definition]: A finite extension of ℚ.
- Ordered field [definition]: A field with a total order preserved by addition, in which the product of two positive elements is positive.
- Ordered pair [definition]: A pair (x,y) with distinguished first and second entries; (x,y)=(x′,y′) exactly when x=x′ and y=y′.
- Orthogonal complement for a bilinear form [definition]: For a bilinear form b on V and subspace U, its right orthogonal complement is U⊥={v∈V:b(u,v)=0 for every u∈U}. This definition does not assume a positive inner product.
- Orthogonal direct sum of quadratic spaces [definition]: For quadratic spaces (V_i,Q_i), their orthogonal direct sum has underlying direct sum and Q((v_i))=∑Q_i(v_i); distinct summands are orthogonal for the polarized form. The same notation applies to lattice decompositions.
- Orthogonal group of a quadratic space [definition]: For a nondegenerate quadratic space V, O(V) is the group of linear automorphisms preserving Q and O⁺(V)=SO(V) is its determinant-one subgroup. For a lattice N, O(N) and O⁺(N) are the subgroups preserving N.
- Partial order [definition]: A reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive binary relation, usually written ≤.
- Place of a number field [definition]: An equivalence class of nontrivial absolute values on a number field; its completion is denoted F_v. Archimedean and nonarchimedean places give its infinite and finite places.
- Polynomial over a field [definition]: A formal finite sum a₀+a₁x+⋯+aₙxⁿ with coefficients in a field, with the usual coefficientwise addition and multiplication.
- Polynomial ring over a commutative ring [definition]: The ring R[X₁,…,X_n] of formal finite sums of monomials with coefficients in R, with coefficientwise addition and the usual multiplication of monomials.
- Positive definite rational quadratic form [definition]: A quadratic form on a finite-dimensional rational vector space is positive definite if its scalar extension to ℝ has Q(x)>0 for every nonzero vector x.
- Preimage of a subset under a function [definition]: For f:A→B and E⊆B, f⁻¹(E)={x∈A:f(x)∈E}.
- Prime number [definition]: An integer p>1 is prime if its only positive divisors are 1 and p.
- Primitive representation by a quadratic lattice [definition]: A scalar t is represented primitively by a quadratic lattice N if Q(x)=t for a primitive vector x∈N. Over ℤ, primitive means x∉mN for every integer m>1; over ℤ_p it means x∉pN.
- Product topology [definition]: On a Cartesian product of topological spaces, the topology with basic open sets restricting finitely many coordinates to open subsets and leaving all other coordinates unrestricted.
- Proper spinor genus of a quadratic lattice [definition]: In a rational quadratic space, the proper spinor genus of a lattice is its orbit under rational determinant-one isometries and adelic determinant-one isometries with trivial local spinor norms. It refines the genus and is the spinor-genus convention used in the source’s representation arguments.
- Quadratic Z-lattice [definition]: A quadratic ℤ-lattice N is a finitely generated free abelian subgroup spanning a finite-dimensional rational quadratic space, equipped with the restricted form Q. It is integral when Q(N)⊆ℤ; the bilinear form B=(Q(x+y)−Q(x)−Q(y))/2 need not then be integer-valued.
- Quadratic form over a field of characteristic different from two [definition]: On a finite-dimensional vector space over a field F with char(F)≠2, a quadratic form is a map Q with Q(ax)=a²Q(x) whose polarization B(x,y)=(Q(x+y)−Q(x)−Q(y))/2 is bilinear. Thus Q(x)=B(x,x).
- Quadratic lattice over ℤ_p [definition]: A quadratic ℤ_p-lattice in a nondegenerate quadratic space V over ℚ_p is a free ℤ_p-submodule L of finite rank spanning V overℚ_p, equipped with the restricted quadratic form. No integrality of Q(L) or B(L,L) is assumed unless specified.
- Quotient of a commutative ring by an ideal [definition]: For an ideal I of R, the cosets R/I form a ring under (r+I)+(s+I)=r+s+I and (r+I)(s+I)=rs+I.
- Quotient of an abelian group by a subgroup [definition]: For an abelian group G and subgroup H,the cosets G/H form an abelian group under (g+H)+(g′+H)=g+g′+H.
- Ramification index of a finite extension of discretely valued fields [definition]: For L/K with compatible normalized discrete valuations v_L,v_K, the positive integer e satisfying v_L(x)=e v_K(x) for every x in K×; equivalently [v_L(L×):v_L(K×)]=e.
- Rational number [definition]: A real x is rational if x=a/b for integers a,b with b≠0; otherwise it is irrational.
- Real number [definition]: A real number is an element of a complete ordered field ℝ; completeness means every nonempty subset bounded above has a least upper bound.
- Reflection in an anisotropic vector of a quadratic space [definition]: If Q(v)≠0, the reflection in v is τ_v(x)=x−2B(x,v)v/Q(v), an isometry of determinant −1 fixing v-perpendicular pointwise.
- Residue field of a discretely valued field [definition]: The quotient O_v/m_v of the valuation ring by its maximal ideal.
- Restricted product of topological groups [definition]: For groups G_i with chosen open subgroups K_i, the group of tuples g_i with g_i∈K_i for all but finitely many i, with the topology whose basic opens are products of open sets equal to K_i outside finitely many coordinates.
- Ring of p-adic integers [definition]: For a prime p, ℤ_p is the subring {x∈ℚ_p:v_p(x)≥0}, with its p-adic topology.
- Separable algebraic field extension [definition]: An algebraic extension L/F is separable if the minimal polynomial over F of every element of L has distinct roots in an algebraic closure.
- Separable polynomial over a field [definition]: A polynomial having no repeated root in an algebraic closure of its coefficient field.
- Sequence in a set [definition]: A function from the natural numbers, or the positive natural numbers, to that set.
- Set [definition]: A collection of distinct elements, regarded as one object.
- Sign of a permutation [definition]: The sign of a permutation of {1,…,n} is (−1) to the number of inversions, where an inversion is a pair i<j with σ(i)>σ(j).
- Square-class group of a field [definition]: For a field F, its square-class group is F×/(F×)², where (F×)² is the subgroup of nonzero squares.
- Squarefree integer [definition]: A positive integer divisible by no square of a prime.
- Subgroup [definition]: A subset of a group containing the identity and closed under multiplication and inverses.
- Submodule of a module over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup of a module stable under multiplication by every scalar of its coefficient ring.
- Subset [definition]: A⊆B means every element of A is an element of B.
- Subspace topology [definition]: For A⊆X, the topology whose open sets are A∩U for U open in X.
- Symmetric group of a finite set [definition]: The group of all bijections of a finite set to itself under composition.
- Tensor product of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: For R-modules M,N, the R-module M⊗_R N with a bilinear map (m,n)↦m⊗n through which every R-bilinear map M×N→P factors uniquely by an R-linear map.
- Topological field [definition]: A field with a topology making addition, multiplication, additive inversion and inversion on the nonzero elements continuous.
- Topological group [definition]: A group with a topology for which multiplication G×G→G and inversion G→G are continuous.
- Topological ring [definition]: A ring with a topology for which addition, multiplication and additive inversion are continuous; the rings here are commutative with identity.
- Topological space [definition]: A set X with a family τ of subsets, called open sets, containing ∅ and X and closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
- Total order [definition]: A partial order in which any two elements are comparable.
- Union of a family of sets [definition]: The set of elements belonging to at least one member of the family.
- Valuation ring of a discretely valued field [definition]: For a discrete valuation v on K, the ring O_v={x:v(x)≥0}; its maximal ideal is m_v={x:v(x)>0}.
- Vector space [definition]: An abelian group with scalar multiplication by a field satisfying a(v+w)=av+aw, (a+b)v=av+bv, (ab)v=a(bv), and 1v=v.
- Vector subspace [definition]: A subset of a vector space containing zero and closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
- p-adic valuation of a rational number [definition]: For a prime p and nonzero rational x, v_p(x) is the unique integer r such that x=p^r a/b with integers a,b not divisible by p; put v_p(0)=+∞.
