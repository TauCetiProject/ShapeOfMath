LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Quantile function of a probability measure on the real line
   For a Borel probability measure μ on ℝ with distribution function F(x)=μ((−∞,x]), the quantile function is Q_μ(u)=inf{x∈ℝ:F(x)≥u} for 0<u<1, the generalized inverse of F.
   Source: explains, Definition 2.1
   Note: Learning reference.

2. Quantile coupling for usual stochastic order
   For probability measures mu,nu on the real line, write Q_mu(u)=inf{x:mu((−∞,x])≥u}, 0<u<1. If U is uniform on(0,1), then Q_mu(U) has law mu, and mu≤stnu if and only if Q_mu(u)≤Q_nu(u) for every0<u<1. For probability kernels on the real line the quantile can be chosen jointly measurable in the parameter and u.
   Source: explains, Propositions 2.3(5) and 3.1(2)
   Note: Inversion method; not a priority claim.
