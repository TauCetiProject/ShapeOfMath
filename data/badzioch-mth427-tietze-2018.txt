LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Tietze extension theorem
   Every continuous real-valued function on a closed subset of a normal topological space extends continuously to the whole space; if the function takes values in [−1,1], the extension can have the same range.
   Source: explains, Theorems 11.1 and 11.8 with proofs, pp. 71–73
   Note: Textbook-style proof from Urysohn’s lemma; not a priority claim.
