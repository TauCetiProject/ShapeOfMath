LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Tate’s theorem relating K2 of a global field to Galois cohomology
   For a global field F and a prime ℓ different from its characteristic, Tate's Galois symbol map identifies the ℓ-primary part of K₂F with the torsion subgroup of H²(F,ℤ_ℓ(2)); equivalently H¹(F,(ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)(2)) modulo its maximal divisible subgroup is canonically isomorphic to the ℓ-primary part of K₂F.
   Source: explains, Theorems 5.4 and 6.6, pp.270 and 273
   Note: Tate says the isomorphism, conjectured by Lichtenbaum, is not new: a sketch of proof had appeared in his earlier talk; this paper gives the details. Original attribution left unresolved.
