2. Cardinality of finite subsets of an infinite well-ordered set
   If X is an infinite well-ordered set of cardinality κ, then |X^{<ω}|=|[X]^{<ω}|=κ, and a countable union of sets of cardinality at most κ has cardinality at most κ.
