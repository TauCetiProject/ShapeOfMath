LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Pasting lemma for a finite closed cover
   Maps on finitely many closed subsets covering a topological space glue to a continuous map if they are continuous and agree on every overlap.
   Source: explains, Lemma 1.24, pp. 13–14
   Note: Selected proof exposition; not an origin claim.

2. Path-connected spaces are connected
   Every path-connected topological space is connected.
   Source: explains, Proposition 1.64, pp. 36–37
   Note: Exposition with proof; not an origin claim.

3. Boundedness of continuous functions on compact spaces
   Every continuous real-valued function on a compact topological space is bounded above and below.
   Source: explains, Lemma 1.40, p. 25
   Note: Exposition with proof; not an origin claim.
