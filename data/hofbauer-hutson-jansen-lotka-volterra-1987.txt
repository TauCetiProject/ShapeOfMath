LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Permanence of a discrete population map
   A continuous map of the nonnegative orthant is permanent if it has a compact forward-invariant absorbing set contained in the strictly positive orthant: every strictly positive initial state eventually enters that set. In particular all species eventually have one common positive lower bound independent of the initial state.

2. Weighted boundary-equilibrium criterion for Lotka–Volterra permanence
   For Fᵢ(x)=xᵢexp(bᵢ+∑ⱼAᵢⱼxⱼ), suppose there is a compact forward-invariant set absorbing all nonnegative states. If some pᵢ>0 satisfy ∑ᵢpᵢ(bᵢ+∑ⱼAᵢⱼxⱼ*)>0 at every boundary fixed point x*, then F is permanent.
