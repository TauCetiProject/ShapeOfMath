1. Partition of unity on a local-field manifold
   For a compact subset of a finite-dimensional manifold over R,C or a finite extension of Q_p and a finite open cover, there are compactly supported smooth functions subordinate to the cover summing to1 near that compact subset. In the nonarchimedean case they may be locally constant.
   Source: explains, Theorem 4.5, p. 39 (real smooth case only)
   Note: Learning reference for the real-manifold case; not a priority claim.
