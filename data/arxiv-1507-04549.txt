1. Walnut representation on Wiener amalgam spaces
   For g,γ∈W(L∞,ℓ¹) with ⟨γ,g⟩≠0, the mixed Gabor operator extends boundedly to every W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ), 1≤p,q≤∞, and equals ⟨γ,g⟩⁻¹∑_n aᵈG_n·T_{n/b}, where G_n(x)=∑_k conjugate(g(x−n/b−ak))γ(x−ak); the operator series converges absolutely after a uniform translation bound.
   Source: relies_on, §4
   Note: Imported representation and off-diagonal estimates.

2. Feichtinger algebra S₀ on Euclidean space
   The Feichtinger algebra S₀(ℝᵈ) consists of f∈L²(ℝᵈ) for which V_gf∈L¹(ℝ²ᵈ) for a fixed nonzero Schwartz window g; choosing a different such window gives the same space.
   Source: relies_on, §5
   Note: Windows guaranteeing absolute Janssen convergence.

3. Lattice Gabor frame
   For g∈L²(ℝᵈ) and a,b>0, the family (M_{mb}T_{na}g)_{m,n∈ℤᵈ} is a lattice Gabor frame when it satisfies the Hilbert-space frame inequalities.
   Source: relies_on, §1–3
   Note: Frames and dual Gabor windows.

4. Wiener amalgam space W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ)
   For 1≤p,q≤∞ and Q=[0,1)ᵈ, W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ)(ℝᵈ) consists of measurable f with (||f·1_{Q+k}||_p)_{k∈ℤᵈ}∈ℓᵠ, with this sequence norm.
   Source: relies_on, §2–5
   Note: Ambient local/global norm.

5. Fourier coefficient of an integrable circle function
   For an integrable function g on the unit circle, its nth Fourier coefficient is ĝ(n)=(2π)⁻¹∫₀²π g(e^{it})e^{−int}dt, for n∈ℤ.
   Source: relies_on, §5
   Note: Periodic Fourier expansion of G_n.

6. Riemann integral
   A bounded f:[a,b]→ℝ has Riemann integral I if all tagged Riemann sums approach I as the maximum length of the partition intervals tends to zero.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma4.5
   Note: Locally Riemann integrable window products.

7. Lebesgue Lᵖ space
   For a measure space and 1≤p<∞, Lᵖ consists of measurable scalar functions modulo equality almost everywhere with ∫|f|ᵖ<∞, normed by (∫|f|ᵖ)¹ᐟᵖ. For p=∞ use the essential-supremum norm.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma4.6
   Note: Local convergence and absolute continuity of the integral.

8. Strong dense-sampling limit of Gabor operators on Wiener amalgams
   If g,γ∈W(L∞,ℓ¹) and ⟨γ,g⟩≠0, then ||S_{a,b;g,γ}f−f||_{W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ)}→0 as a,b→0 for every f∈W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ) with 1≤p,q<∞.
   Source: introduces, Theorem1.1(i),1≤p,q<∞
   Note: Extension of Sun’s Lᵖ convergence to Wiener amalgams.
   Origin evidence: Checked §1 explicitly contrasts Sun [16] on Lᵖ with the new amalgam claim and proves the finite-p finite-q case; the questionable q=∞ extension is not included in this concept.

9. Operator-norm dense-sampling limit of Gabor operators on Wiener amalgams
   If g,γ∈W(L∞,ℓ¹), ⟨γ,g⟩≠0, and conjugate(g)γ is locally Riemann integrable, then ||S_{a,b;g,γ}−I|| on W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ) tends to zero as a,b→0 for every 1≤p,q≤∞.
   Source: introduces, Theorem1.1(ii)
   Note: Uniform operator convergence under Riemann-integrability of the product.
   Origin evidence: Checked §1 and Remark4.8 specifically identify operator-norm convergence on Wiener amalgams as the contribution, while Walnut/Janssen/Wexler–Raz remain attributed earlier.

10. Associate duality of Wiener amalgam spaces
   For1≤p,q≤∞, the Köthe associate of W(Lᵖ,ℓᵠ) under the integral pairing is W(Lᵖ′,ℓᵠ′), with norm obtained as the supremum of |∫fg| over the unit ball; when p,q<∞ this represents its continuous dual.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma2.1;§§4–5
   Note: Operator pairings and the weak convergence formulation use the exact associate dual.

11. Correlation summability of Wiener windows
   For g,γ∈W(L∞,ℓ¹)(ℝᵈ), let G_{a,b;n}(x)=∑_{k∈ℤᵈ}conj(g(x−n/b−ak))γ(x−ak). Then ∑_n||G_{a,b;n}||∞≤(1+1/a)ᵈ(2+2b)ᵈ||g||_W||γ||_W, and aᵈ∑_{n≠0}||G_{a,b;n}||∞→0 as a,b→0.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma4.2;Proposition4.3;Lemma4.4
   Note: Absolute Walnut summation and vanishing off-diagonal part need both estimates.

12. Uniform Riemann sums for a Wiener-space function
   If f∈W(L∞,ℓ¹)(ℝᵈ) is locally Riemann integrable, then ess sup_y|aᵈ∑_{n∈ℤᵈ}f(y+an)−∫f|→0 as a↓0.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma4.5;operator-norm proofTheorem1.1
   Note: Uniform diagonal multiplier convergence under the extra local Riemann hypothesis.

13. L² convergence of densely sampled Gabor reconstruction
   For g,γ∈L² with⟨γ,g⟩≠0, suppose normalized self-Gabor operators S_{a,b;g,g} and S_{a,b;γ,γ} have uniformly bounded norms for0<a,b≤1. Then the normalized cross-Gabor operators are well-defined and converge strongly to the identity onL² as a,b→0.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem3.2;Lemma4.6
   Note: L² strong convergence starts the density/extrapolation argument; proof is imported and remains independently uncertified.

14. Adjoint-lattice coefficient summability for Feichtinger windows
   If g,γ∈S₀(ℝᵈ), then for every a,b>0, ∑_{l,n∈ℤᵈ}|⟨γ,M_{l/a}T_{n/b}g⟩|<∞.
   Source: relies_on, §5,Theorem5.2
   Note: This is the imported absolute-convergence input for Janssen’s representation.
