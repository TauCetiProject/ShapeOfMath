LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Theorem of the square for invertible sheaves on an abelian variety
   For an invertible sheaf L on an abelian variety A over a field k and points a,b∈A(k), t_(a+b)*L⊗L≅t_a*L⊗t_b*L, where t_a:A→A is translation by a.
   Source: explains, Theorem 6.7 and proof, p. 10
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Theorem of the cube
   Let U, V, W be complete varieties over a field k with k-points u₀, v₀, w₀. An invertible sheaf on U×V×W whose restrictions to {u₀}×V×W, U×{v₀}×W and U×V×{w₀} are trivial is itself trivial.
   Source: explains, Theorem 6.1, p. 8
   Note: Statement recalled with a proof sketch referring to Mumford 1970 §6; not a priority claim.

3. Projectivity of abelian varieties
   Every abelian variety over a field has an ample invertible sheaf, and hence is projective; the sheaf can be chosen symmetric, L≅[−1]*L.
   Source: explains, Theorem 7.1 and Corollary 7.2 with proofs, pp. 10-12
   Note: Selected proof reference (Weil's argument); not a priority claim.
