1. Integration identity for optional projection against a continuous increasing process
   For a nonnegative measurable process H with optional projection ^oH and an adapted continuous increasing integrable process A, E∫H_s dA_s=E∫(^oH)_s dA_s. The projection is characterized by (^oH)_τ=E[H_τ|F_τ] at finite stopping times where integrable.
   Source: explains, Theorem 9, implication 2⇒4
   Note: Change-of-variables proof that an adapted increasing IV process satisfies E[ξ·A_∞]=E[ᵒξ·A_∞]; expository, no origin claim.
