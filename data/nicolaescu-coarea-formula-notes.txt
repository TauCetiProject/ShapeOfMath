1. Coarea formula for a smooth scalar function
   For a smooth real functionf on an open subset ofℝⁿ and a nonnegative measurableg, ∫g(x)|∇f(x)|dx=∫_ℝ∫_{f⁻¹(t)}g(x)dH^(n−1)(x)dt, allowing infinity.
   Source: explains, Theorem 4.1, Lemma 4.2, Corollary 4.4, pp. 10–11
