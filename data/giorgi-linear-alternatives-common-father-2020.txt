LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Alternative for strict positive linear feasibility
   For a real matrix A and real column b, exactly one holds: Ax=b for some column x with every coordinate strictly positive; or there is a real column p with Aᵀp≥0, pᵀb≤0, and at least one strict inequality among these scalar inequalities.
   Source: explains, (12.2), p. 59, with Lemma 3, p. 53
   Note: Stated as a non-homogeneous version of Stiemke’s theorem and derived from Farkas’ theorem; not a priority claim.

2. Farkas’ lemma
   For a real m×n matrix A and real row b,exactly one holds: xA≤b for some real row x; or Ay=0 for some nonzero column y≥0 with by<0.
   Source: explains, Theorem 1, p. 45; §3
   Note: Statement of Farkas’ theorem in the form Ax=b, x≥0 versus yᵀA≥0, yᵀb<0; not a priority claim.
