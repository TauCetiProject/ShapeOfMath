1. Rellich analytic eigendecomposition for Hermitian matrix families
   If A(t) is a square complex matrix whose entries are real analytic on an open real interval I and A(t)=A(t)* for every t∈I, then A(t)=U(t)D(t)U(t)* with U(t) unitary and D(t) real diagonal, all entries of U and D real analytic throughout I.
