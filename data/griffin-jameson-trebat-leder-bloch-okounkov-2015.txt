REQUIRED CONCEPT FRONTIER (14)

- Bloch–Okounkov one-point theta formula: With F(z;τ)=½∑_(k≥0)⟨Q_k⟩_q(4πiz)^(k−1), one has F(z;τ)=η(τ)³/(2θ₁(2z;τ)), where θ₁(z;τ)=∑_(n∈ℤ)(−1)ⁿq^((n+1/2)²/2)exp(2πiz(n+1/2)). Equivalently θ₁=−i θ[1/2,1/2] in the catalogue’s characteristic convention.
- Bloch–Okounkov quasimodularity theorem: For every homogeneous shifted symmetric polynomial f on partitions of weight k, the q-bracket ⟨f⟩_q is a weight-k quasimodular form for SL₂(ℤ).
- Covariance of the Jacobi elliptic raising operator: For the weight-k index-m Jacobi modular slash action and real analytic φ, the elliptic raising operator Y_m satisfies Y_m(φ|_(k,m)γ)=(Y_mφ)|_(k+1,m)γ for every γ∈SL₂(ℤ). Thus Y_m preserves the index and raises the transformation weight by one, allowing nonholomorphic dependence on the elliptic variable.
- Euler’s totient congruence theorem: If a,n are integers with n≥1 and gcd(a,n)=1, then a^φ(n)≡1 modulo n.
- Fourier expansion of normalized level-one Eisenstein series: For even k≥4 and τ in the upper half-plane, the normalized level-one Eisenstein series satisfies E_k(τ)=1−(2k/B_k)∑_(n≥1)σ_(k−1)(n)q^n, where q=exp(2πiτ), σ_r(n)=∑_(d|n,d>0)d^r, and B_k is the kth Bernoulli number. The series converges locally uniformly in τ.
- Kummer congruences for p-regularized Bernoulli numbers: Let p≥5 be prime and r≥1. For positive even k₁,k₂ not divisible by p−1 and congruent modulo φ(pʳ), the p-integral rationals (1−p^(k_i−1))B_(k_i)/k_i are congruent modulo pʳ.
- Mod-p filtration of powers of E2: For p≥5 and every positive integer r, the reduction of E₂ʳ has level-one modular filtration r(p+1). The same formula holds for G₂ʳ, since G₂=−E₂/24 and 24 is a p-adic unit.
- Modular and elliptic transformations of odd theta: Put Θ(z;τ)=−iθ[½,½](τ,z), q=exp(2πiτ). Then Θ(z;τ+1)=exp(πi/4)Θ(z;τ), Θ(z/τ;−1/τ)=−i√(−iτ)exp(πiz²/τ)Θ(z;τ), Θ(z+1;τ)=−Θ(z;τ), and Θ(z+τ;τ)=−exp(−πiτ−2πiz)Θ(z;τ). The square root is the holomorphic branch positive at τ=i.
- Modular covariance of a Jacobi circular constant term: Suppose φ has weight-k index-m Jacobi modular covariance on Γ and is real analytic near z=0 apart from a finite-order singularity. Put Φ(z;τ)=exp(2πimz Im(z)/Im(τ))φ(z;τ). If A(τ)=lim_(r→0)∫₀¹Φ(r exp(2πit);τ)dt exists, then A(γτ)=(cτ+d)^kA(τ) for every γ∈Γ. This assertion is a transformation law and does not alone imply holomorphy or cusp growth.
- Partial-fraction identity for the partition-crank generating function: For |q|<1 and w away from the poles, ∏_(n≥1)(1−qⁿ)/((1−wqⁿ)(1−w^(−1)qⁿ))=(1−w)/(q;q)_∞ ∑_(n∈ℤ)(−1)ⁿq^(n(n+1)/2)/(1−wqⁿ).
- Quasimodular forms as Serre p-adic modular forms: For p≥5, every level-one quasimodular form with rational Fourier coefficients is a Serre p-adic modular form, since E₂,E₄,E₆ belong to the completion and generate the rational quasimodular ring.
- S and T transformations of the Dedekind eta function: For τ in the upper half-plane, η(τ+1)=exp(πi/12)η(τ) and η(−1/τ)=√(−iτ)η(τ), using the holomorphic square-root branch positive at τ=i.
- Simple zero lattice and derivative of odd theta: For τ in the upper half-plane, Θ(z;τ)=−iθ[½,½](τ,z) has exactly the simple zeros z∈ℤ+τℤ, and ∂Θ/∂z(0;τ)=2πiη(τ)³. In particular η has no zeros in the upper half-plane.
- Zagier’s leading Eisenstein coefficient of a one-point q-bracket: For every integer r≥1, the normalized one-point bracket 𝒬_(2r)=2^(2r−2)(2r−1)!⟨Q_(2r)⟩_q is a polynomial of degree r in G₂=−E₂/24 over the rational level-one modular-form ring. Its leading coefficient is (2r−1)!!8^(r−1)/r, where (2r−1)!!=1·3·⋯·(2r−1).

LOCATED CONCEPTS (26)

1. Bloch–Okounkov quasimodularity theorem
   For every homogeneous shifted symmetric polynomial f on partitions of weight k, the q-bracket ⟨f⟩_q is a weight-k quasimodular form for SL₂(ℤ).
   Source: relies_on, §1; §2.1
   Note: Undepleted bracket modularity is imported from Bloch–Okounkov and later Zagier.

2. Kummer congruences for p-regularized Bernoulli numbers
   Let p≥5 be prime and r≥1. For positive even k₁,k₂ not divisible by p−1 and congruent modulo φ(pʳ), the p-integral rationals (1−p^(k_i−1))B_(k_i)/k_i are congruent modulo pʳ.
   Source: relies_on, §1; §3.1
   Note: Constant-term congruences.

3. Euler’s totient congruence theorem
   If a,n are integers with n≥1 and gcd(a,n)=1, then a^φ(n)≡1 modulo n.
   Source: relies_on, §1; §3.1
   Note: Congruences for the prime-to-p coordinate powers.

4. Quasimodular forms as Serre p-adic modular forms
   For p≥5, every level-one quasimodular form with rational Fourier coefficients is a Serre p-adic modular form, since E₂,E₄,E₆ belong to the completion and generate the rational quasimodular ring.
   Source: relies_on, §3.2
   Note: Passes from rational quasimodular brackets to Serre’s completion.

5. Serre p-adic modular form
   For a prime p, a series f=∑a_n qⁿ in ℚ_p[[q]] is a Serre p-adic modular form if it is a uniform p-adic limit of rational-coefficient classical level-one modular forms: the infimum over n of v_p(a_n−a_(i,n)) tends to +∞.
   Source: relies_on, Definition 1; §3.2
   Note: Uniform coefficientwise p-adic completion is the definition used.

6. Mod-p filtration of a level-one modular q-series
   For p≥5 and f∈𝔽_p[[q]] admitting a modular lift, its filtration w(f) is the least nonnegative integer k such that f is the reduction of a weight-k, level-one modular form with p-integral rational Fourier coefficients.
   Source: relies_on, Definition 2; §3.3
   Note: The filtration is compared across congruent q-series.

7. Bloch–Okounkov one-point theta formula
   With F(z;τ)=½∑_(k≥0)⟨Q_k⟩_q(4πiz)^(k−1), one has F(z;τ)=η(τ)³/(2θ₁(2z;τ)), where θ₁(z;τ)=∑_(n∈ℤ)(−1)ⁿq^((n+1/2)²/2)exp(2πiz(n+1/2)). Equivalently θ₁=−i θ[1/2,1/2] in the catalogue’s characteristic convention.
   Source: relies_on, Equation (7)
   Note: Imported one-point identity underlies the Jacobi proof.

8. Jacobi theta function with characteristics
   For fixed real a,b, θ[a,b](τ,z)=∑_{n∈Z}exp(πi(n+a)²τ+2πi(n+a)(z+b)), with Im(τ)>0. The three even half-characteristics (1/2,0),(0,0),(0,1/2) give the standard θ₂,θ₃,θ₄ conventions.
   Source: relies_on, §4.2
   Note: Odd half-characteristic theta transformations and zero lattice.

9. Dedekind eta function
   For Im(τ)>0 and q=exp(2πiτ), η(τ)=exp(πiτ/12)∏_(n≥1)(1−qⁿ). The explicit exponential prefactor specifies the meaning of q^(1/24) without choosing a complex root.
   Source: relies_on, Equations (1),(7)
   Note: Partition normalization and one-point function.

10. Meromorphic Jacobi form of integral weight and index
   For integral k,m and a specified modular subgroup, a meromorphic Jacobi form obeys the weight-k index-m modular transformation and φ(z+rτ+s;τ)=exp(−2πim(r²τ+2rz))φ(z;τ). It may have poles in z, is holomorphic in τ away from its polar divisor, and has at most meromorphic cusp growth. Negative indices are allowed; Fourier expansions are taken in chambers avoiding poles.
   Source: relies_on, §4.2
   Note: Negative index and elliptic poles are essential.

21. Zagier’s leading Eisenstein coefficient of a one-point q-bracket
   For every integer r≥1, the normalized one-point bracket 𝒬_(2r)=2^(2r−2)(2r−1)!⟨Q_(2r)⟩_q is a polynomial of degree r in G₂=−E₂/24 over the rational level-one modular-form ring. Its leading coefficient is (2r−1)!!8^(r−1)/r, where (2r−1)!!=1·3·⋯·(2r−1).
   Source: relies_on, §3.3 p.8

   Source: relies_on, §3.3 p.8
   Note: Supplies the actual filtration equality, beyond the definition of filtration.

23. Modular and elliptic transformations of odd theta
   Put Θ(z;τ)=−iθ[½,½](τ,z), q=exp(2πiτ). Then Θ(z;τ+1)=exp(πi/4)Θ(z;τ), Θ(z/τ;−1/τ)=−i√(−iτ)exp(πiz²/τ)Θ(z;τ), Θ(z+1;τ)=−Θ(z;τ), and Θ(z+τ;τ)=−exp(−πiτ−2πiz)Θ(z;τ). The square root is the holomorphic branch positive at τ=i.
   Source: relies_on, §4.2
   Note: The multiplier and elliptic signs cancel in η³/Θ(2z).

24. Simple zero lattice and derivative of odd theta
   For τ in the upper half-plane, Θ(z;τ)=−iθ[½,½](τ,z) has exactly the simple zeros z∈ℤ+τℤ, and ∂Θ/∂z(0;τ)=2πiη(τ)³. In particular η has no zeros in the upper half-plane.
   Source: relies_on, §4.2
   Note: Controls the precise simple poles and eta/theta normalization.

25. S and T transformations of the Dedekind eta function
   For τ in the upper half-plane, η(τ+1)=exp(πi/12)η(τ) and η(−1/τ)=√(−iτ)η(τ), using the holomorphic square-root branch positive at τ=i.
   Source: relies_on, §4.2
   Note: Cancels the half-weight theta multiplier.

26. Covariance of the Jacobi elliptic raising operator
   For the weight-k index-m Jacobi modular slash action and real analytic φ, the elliptic raising operator Y_m satisfies Y_m(φ|_(k,m)γ)=(Y_mφ)|_(k+1,m)γ for every γ∈SL₂(ℤ). Thus Y_m preserves the index and raises the transformation weight by one, allowing nonholomorphic dependence on the elliptic variable.
   Source: relies_on, Proposition4.3
   Note: Justifies iterated raising despite loss of holomorphy.

27. Modular covariance of a Jacobi circular constant term
   Suppose φ has weight-k index-m Jacobi modular covariance on Γ and is real analytic near z=0 apart from a finite-order singularity. Put Φ(z;τ)=exp(2πimz Im(z)/Im(τ))φ(z;τ). If A(τ)=lim_(r→0)∫₀¹Φ(r exp(2πit);τ)dt exists, then A(γτ)=(cτ+d)^kA(τ) for every γ∈Γ. This assertion is a transformation law and does not alone imply holomorphy or cusp growth.
   Source: relies_on, Proposition4.4
   Note: Turns the pole-corrected circular average into modular covariance.

28. Fourier expansion of normalized level-one Eisenstein series
   For even k≥4 and τ in the upper half-plane, the normalized level-one Eisenstein series satisfies E_k(τ)=1−(2k/B_k)∑_(n≥1)σ_(k−1)(n)q^n, where q=exp(2πiτ), σ_r(n)=∑_(d|n,d>0)d^r, and B_k is the kth Bernoulli number. The series converges locally uniformly in τ.
   Source: relies_on, §2 Eisenstein series and§3 coefficientwise congruence/filtration computations
   Note: Actual reusable Fourier/modularity identification needed for the source coefficient calculations; separate from defining the lattice-sum Eisenstein series. The selected later proof inputs are not all made source-specific roots.

ADDITIONAL PREREQUISITES (115)

- Abelian group [definition]: A group whose operation is commutative.
- Absolute convergence of a real series [definition]: A real series ∑aₙ converges absolutely if ∑|aₙ| is finite; it converges conditionally if ∑aₙ converges and ∑|aₙ| diverges.
- Absolute value on a field [definition]: A map |·|:K→R_{≥0} that vanishes exactly at zero, is multiplicative and satisfies |x+y|≤|x|+|y|. It is nonarchimedean if the stronger bound |x+y|≤max(|x|,|y|) holds.
- Almost holomorphic modular form [definition]: For a specified congruence subgroup and integer weight k, a function obeying the scalar modular weight-k transformation, polynomial in 1/Im(τ) with holomorphic coefficients and of at most polynomial growth in Im(τ) after normalization at every cusp. This is the almost holomorphic convention called nearly holomorphic by Larson, and excludes cusp poles.
- Automorphism of a group [definition]: A bijective group homomorphism from a group to itself; automorphisms form a group under composition.
- Basis of a vector space [definition]: A linearly independent family of vectors whose linear span is the whole vector space.
- Bernoulli numbers [definition]: The Bernoulli numbers B_n∈ℚ are determined by t/(exp(t)−1)=∑_(n≥0) B_n tⁿ/n!, as an identity of formal power series; thus B₁=−1/2.
- Bijection between sets [definition]: A function for which each element of the codomain is the image of exactly one element of the domain.
- Binary operation on a set [definition]: A function S×S→S.
- Binary relation on a set [definition]: A subset R⊆S×S; writing xRy means (x,y)∈R.
- Cartesian product of sets [definition]: A×B is the set of ordered pairs (a,b) with a∈A and b∈B.
- Cauchy sequence in a metric space [definition]: A sequence (xₙ) in a metric space (X,d) is Cauchy if for every ε>0 there is N such that d(xₙ,xₘ)<ε whenever n,m≥N.
- Commutative ring with identity [definition]: An abelian group under addition, together with an associative commutative multiplication with identity, distributing over addition. The zero ring is allowed.
- Complete metric space [definition]: A metric space is complete if every Cauchy sequence in it converges to a point of that same space.
- Completion of a field with an absolute value [definition]: The metric completion of K for d(x,y)=|x−y|, with the field operations extended continuously. A completion is regarded as containing K through its canonical isometric embedding.
- Completion of a metric space [definition]: A complete metric space containing an isometric dense copy of the given metric space. It is unique up to an isometry fixing that copy.
- Complex derivative [definition]: For a complex-valued function on an open subset of C, f′(z) is the limit of (f(z+h)−f(z))/h as nonzero complex h→0, when it exists.
- Complex exponential function [definition]: The entire function exp(z)=∑ₙ₌₀∞zⁿ/n!; on imaginary arguments exp(it)=cos(t)+i sin(t).
- Complex number [definition]: A complex number is a pair of real numbers, written a+bi. Addition is componentwise and multiplication is (a+bi)(c+di)=(ac−bd)+(ad+bc)i, so i²=−1.
- Complex partial derivative [definition]: The complex derivative in one coordinate of a function of several complex variables, holding the other coordinates fixed.
- Complex power series [definition]: A series ∑ₙ₌₀∞aₙ(z−z₀)ⁿ with complex coefficients, considered at points where its partial sums converge.
- Composition of functions [definition]: For f:A→B and g:B→C, the composite g∘f:A→C sends x to g(f(x)).
- Congruence modulo an integer [definition]: The relation a≡b (mod m) means that m divides a−b.
- Congruence subgroup of SL₂(ℤ) [definition]: A subgroup of SL₂(ℤ) containing Γ(N)={M:M≡I moduloN} for some positive integer N.
- Continuous map between topological spaces [definition]: A map is continuous if the preimage of every open subset of its target is open in its domain.
- Convergence of a sequence in a metric space [definition]: A sequence (xₙ) in a metric space (X,d) converges to x∈X if for every ε>0 there is N such that d(xₙ,x)<ε whenever n≥N.
- Convergent real series [definition]: The series ∑ₙ≥1aₙ converges to S if its sequence of partial sums converges to S.
- Coprime integers [definition]: Integers whose greatest common divisor is1.
- Cusp of a subgroup of SL₂(Z) [definition]: An orbit of a subgroup Γ⊂SL₂(Z) on Q∪{∞} under fractional linear transformations; for a congruence subgroup its cusp width is the positive translation period after sending a representative to infinity.
- Dense subset of a metric space [definition]: A subset A of a metric space X is dense if every open ball centered in X with positive radius meets A.
- Determinant of a square matrix [definition]: For an n×n matrix A over a commutative ring, det A=∑_{σ∈Sₙ}sgn(σ)∏_{i=1}ⁿA_{i,σ(i)}, with the 0×0 determinant equal to one.
- Divisibility of integers [definition]: An integer a divides b if b=ac for some integer c.
- Euler totient function [definition]: The number φ(n) of integers 1≤a≤n coprime to n.
- Extended nonnegative real numbers [definition]: The nonnegative real numbers together with a greatest element ∞. Sums involving ∞ equal ∞, and 0·∞ is taken to be 0 when integrating simple functions.
- Factorial [definition]: For n≥1, n!=1·2···n, and 0!=1.
- Field [definition]: A commutative ring in which 1≠0 and every nonzero element is invertible.
- Field of p-adic numbers [definition]: For a prime p, ℚ_p is the completion of ℚ for the p-adic absolute value |x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}.
- Finite field [definition]: A field whose underlying set is finite.
- Finite set [definition]: A set X is finite if it is bijective with {1,…,n} for some natural number n≥0; its cardinality is n.
- Finite-dimensional vector space [definition]: A vector space having a finite basis.
- Formal power series over a commutative ring [definition]: An expression ∑a_α X^α with coefficients in a commutative ring and multi-indices α in N^n, added coefficientwise and multiplied by the finite Cauchy sums defining each coefficient, without any assertion of analytic convergence.
- Frobenius coordinates of an integer partition [definition]: For a partition with r diagonal cells, its Frobenius coordinates are (r;a₁,…,a_r;b₁,…,b_r), where a_i and b_i are the arm and leg lengths at diagonal cell i.
- Function between sets [definition]: An assignment taking each element of a specified domain set to exactly one element of a specified codomain set.
- General linear group over a commutative ring [definition]: The group GL_n(R) of square matrices over a commutative ring R having a two-sided matrix inverse, with matrix multiplication.
- Greatest common divisor of integers [definition]: For integers a,b not both zero, gcd(a,b) is their greatest positive common divisor.
- Group [definition]: A set with an associative binary operation, an identity element, and a two-sided inverse for each element.
- Group action on a set [definition]: A map G×X→X, written (g,x)↦gx, satisfying ex=x and (gh)x=g(hx).
- Group homomorphism [definition]: A map f:G→H satisfying f(xy)=f(x)f(y) for all x,y.
- Holomorphic Eisenstein series for SL₂(Z) [definition]: For even k≥4, the modular form E_k(τ)=½∑_(c,d)=1(cτ+d)^(−k), normalized to constant Fourier coefficient 1. The weight-two series E₂=1−24∑_{n≥1}σ₁(n)q^n is quasimodular rather than modular.
- Holomorphic function of one complex variable [definition]: A function on an open subset of ℂ is holomorphic if its complex derivative exists at every point of that subset.
- Holomorphic function of several complex variables [definition]: A complex function on an open subset of C^n that is locally representable by a convergent power series in the n complex coordinates. Vector-valued holomorphy for a finite-dimensional target means holomorphy of its coordinates in any basis.
- Holomorphy of a modular form at a cusp [definition]: After applying the weight-k slash action by a matrix sending a cusp to infinity, the resulting periodic function has a Fourier expansion in q_h=exp(2πiτ/h) with no negative powers, where h is the cusp width.
- Infinite product of complex numbers or functions [definition]: The limit of the finite partial products ∏_{j=1}^N a_j as N→∞, when it exists; for functions the mode of convergence is specified, such as locally uniform convergence on the stated domain. Formal products mean stabilization of each coefficient instead of analytic convergence.
- Infinite q-Pochhammer symbol [definition]: For∣q∣<1, (a;q)∞=∏_{n≥0}(1−aqⁿ).
- Integer [definition]: An integer is an element of the ordered ring ℤ, obtained by adjoining additive inverses to the natural numbers.
- Integer partition [definition]: An integer partition is a finite nonincreasing sequence of positive integers, including the empty sequence. Its size is the sum of its parts.
- Integral Heisenberg group for scalar-index Jacobi forms [definition]: For λ,μ in Z^g and κ in Z, use the triples (λ,μ,κ) with product (λ+λ′,μ+μ′,κ+κ′+λ·μ′−μ·λ′). The dot products are the finite coordinate sums; this specifies the integral normalization used in the scalar-index Jacobi slash action.
- Integral Jacobi group [definition]: The semidirect product of Sp(2g,Z) with the integral Heisenberg group, where the symplectic factor acts on the λ,μ coordinates and fixes the center; its action on H_g×C^g is the one used in Jacobi modular and elliptic transformation laws.
- Intersection of a family of sets [definition]: Inside a specified ambient set, the elements belonging to every member of the family.
- Jacobi theta function [definition]: The holomorphic series θ(τ,z)=∑_{n∈ℤ}exp(πin²τ+2πinz) for Im(τ)>0; this specifies the zero-characteristic theta convention.
- Least upper bound in a partially ordered set [definition]: For A⊆P, its least upper bound is an upper bound s satisfying s≤b for every upper bound b of A, when such an s exists.
- Limit of a function between metric spaces [definition]: For f defined near an accumulation point a of a metric space, lim_{x→a}f(x)=L means that for every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that 0<d(x,a)<δ implies d(f(x),L)<ε.
- Limit of a real function at a point [definition]: The relation lim_{x→a}f(x)=L means that for every ε>0 there is δ>0 such that 0<|x−a|<δ in the domain implies |f(x)−L|<ε.
- Limit of a real sequence [definition]: A sequence (aₙ) converges to L∈ℝ if for every ε>0 there is N such that n≥N implies abs(aₙ−L)<ε.
- Linear combination of vectors [definition]: A finite sum a₁v₁+⋯+aₙvₙ of vectors multiplied by scalars from their vector space’s ground field; the empty sum is zero.
- Linear span of a set of vectors [definition]: The subspace consisting of all finite linear combinations of a specified set of vectors.
- Linearly independent family of vectors [definition]: A family of vectors is linearly independent if every finite linear combination equalling zero has all coefficients zero.
- Matrix over a ring [definition]: A rectangular array of elements of a ring; addition is entrywise and matrix multiplication uses row-by-column sums.
- Meromorphic function of one complex variable [definition]: A function on a complex domain locally expressible as a quotient of holomorphic functions with denominator not identically zero; zeros of the denominator give poles after common factors cancel.
- Metric space [definition]: A set X with a function d:X×X→[0,∞) such that d(x,y)=0 exactly when x=y, d(x,y)=d(y,x), and d(x,z)≤d(x,y)+d(y,z).
- Modular form of integer weight on a congruence subgroup [definition]: A holomorphic function f on the upper half-plane satisfying f((aτ+b)/(cτ+d))=(cτ+d)^k f(τ) for matrices in a specified congruence subgroup, and holomorphic at every cusp.
- Natural number [definition]: One of 0, 1, 2, …; natural numbers count finite collections.
- Normalized one-point Bloch–Okounkov q-bracket [definition]: For k≥1, the normalized one-point q-bracket is 𝒬_k(τ)=2^(k−2)(k−1)!⟨Q_k⟩_q. Its p-depleted version is 𝒬_k^(p)=2^(k−2)(k−1)!⟨Q_k^(p)⟩_q. All nonconstant Fourier coefficients of the undepleted series are integral, and both series vanish for odd k.
- Ordered field [definition]: A field with a total order preserved by addition, in which the product of two positive elements is positive.
- Ordered pair [definition]: A pair (x,y) with distinguished first and second entries; (x,y)=(x′,y′) exactly when x=x′ and y=y′.
- Partial order [definition]: A reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive binary relation, usually written ≤.
- Polynomial over a field [definition]: A formal finite sum a₀+a₁x+⋯+aₙxⁿ with coefficients in a field, with the usual coefficientwise addition and multiplication.
- Polynomial ring over a commutative ring [definition]: The ring R[X₁,…,X_n] of formal finite sums of monomials with coefficients in R, with coefficientwise addition and the usual multiplication of monomials.
- Positive definite real matrix [definition]: A symmetric real matrix A such that xᵀAx>0 for every nonzero real column vector x.
- Preimage of a subset under a function [definition]: For f:A→B and E⊆B, f⁻¹(E)={x∈A:f(x)∈E}.
- Prime number [definition]: An integer p>1 is prime if its only positive divisors are 1 and p.
- Product topology [definition]: On a Cartesian product of topological spaces, the topology with basic open sets restricting finitely many coordinates to open subsets and leaving all other coordinates unrestricted.
- Quasimodular form [definition]: A holomorphic function whose weight-k modular transformation differs by a finite polynomial in c/(cτ+d), with holomorphic coefficient functions and the required cusp-growth conditions; equivalently the holomorphic part of an almost holomorphic modular form.
- Rational number [definition]: A real x is rational if x=a/b for integers a,b with b≠0; otherwise it is irrational.
- Real analytic function on an open Euclidean set [definition]: A real-valued or complex-valued function on an open subset of ℝᵈ is real analytic if, around each point a, it equals a power series ∑_(α∈ℕᵈ)c_α(x−a)^α that converges absolutely on some neighborhood of a.
- Real number [definition]: A real number is an element of a complete ordered field ℝ; completeness means every nonempty subset bounded above has a least upper bound.
- Real polynomial in finitely many variables [definition]: A finite real linear combination of monomials x₁^{a₁}⋯x_d^{a_d} with nonnegative integer exponents.
- Real sequence [definition]: A real sequence is a function a:ℕ₊→ℝ, written (aₙ)ₙ≥1.
- Riemann integral [definition]: A bounded f:[a,b]→ℝ has Riemann integral I if all tagged Riemann sums approach I as the maximum length of the partition intervals tends to zero.
- Scalar-valued Siegel modular form for the full modular group [definition]: A holomorphic function F on ℍ_g with F((AZ+B)(CZ+D)⁻¹)=det(CZ+D)^k F(Z) for integral symplectic matrices [[A,B],[C,D]], and a Fourier expansion supported on positive semidefinite half-integral symmetric matrices. For g=1 the cusp-holomorphy condition is included.
- Semidirect product of groups [definition]: Given a homomorphism α:G→Aut(H), the group H⋊_αG has underlying set H×G and multiplication (h,g)(h′,g′)=(hα(g)(h′),gg′).
- Sequence in a set [definition]: A function from the natural numbers, or the positive natural numbers, to that set.
- Set [definition]: A collection of distinct elements, regarded as one object.
- Shifted symmetric polynomial on integer partitions [definition]: A shifted symmetric polynomial on partitions is a polynomial in the Bloch–Okounkov functions Q₂,Q₃,…, with rational coefficients; its weight is determined by assigning Q_k weight k.
- Siegel upper half-space [definition]: The set ℍ_g of symmetric complex g×g matrices whose imaginary part is positive definite.
- Sign of a permutation [definition]: The sign of a permutation of {1,…,n} is (−1) to the number of inversions, where an inversion is a pair i<j with σ(i)>σ(j).
- Special linear group SL₂(ℤ) [definition]: The group of2×2 integer matrices [[a,b],[c,d]] satisfying ad−bc=1, with matrix multiplication.
- Subgroup [definition]: A subset of a group containing the identity and closed under multiplication and inverses.
- Subset [definition]: A⊆B means every element of A is an element of B.
- Sum of a nonnegative series [definition]: For aₙ≥0, define ∑ₙ≥1aₙ as the supremum of its finite partial sums; the value may be infinite.
- Symmetric group of a finite set [definition]: The group of all bijections of a finite set to itself under composition.
- Symmetric matrix [definition]: A square matrix A equal to its transpose, so aᵢⱼ=aⱼᵢ.
- Symplectic group over a commutative ring [definition]: Sp₂g(R) is the group of invertible 2g×2g matrices M over R satisfying MᵀJM=J for J=[[0,I],[-I,0]].
- Tagged Riemann sum [definition]: For a partition a=x₀<⋯<x_n=b and tags t_i∈[x_{i−1},x_i], the Riemann sum of f is ∑ᵢf(t_i)(x_i−x_{i−1}); the partition mesh is maxᵢ(x_i−x_{i−1}).
- Topological ring [definition]: A ring with a topology for which addition, multiplication and additive inversion are continuous; the rings here are commutative with identity.
- Topological space [definition]: A set X with a family τ of subsets, called open sets, containing ∅ and X and closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
- Total order [definition]: A partial order in which any two elements are comparable.
- Uniform convergence of functions into a metric space [definition]: A sequence fₙ:X→(Y,d) converges uniformly to f if for every ε>0, d(fₙ(x),f(x))<ε for every x∈X once n is sufficiently large independently of x.
- Union of a family of sets [definition]: The set of elements belonging to at least one member of the family.
- Vector space [definition]: An abelian group with scalar multiplication by a field satisfying a(v+w)=av+aw, (a+b)v=av+bv, (ab)v=a(bv), and 1v=v.
- Vector subspace [definition]: A subset of a vector space containing zero and closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
- Weight-k slash operator on the upper half-plane [definition]: For γ=[[a,b],[c,d]]∈SL₂(ℤ), (f|ₖγ)(τ)=(cτ+d)^{-k}f((aτ+b)/(cτ+d)).
- Young diagram of an integer partition [definition]: For a partition λ, its Young diagram is the set of cells (i,j) with 1≤j≤λ_i, drawn in rows of lengths λ_i.
- p-adic valuation of a rational number [definition]: For a prime p and nonzero rational x, v_p(x) is the unique integer r such that x=p^r a/b with integers a,b not divisible by p; put v_p(0)=+∞.
- p-integral rational number [definition]: For a prime p, a rational number is p-integral if it can be written a/b with integers a,b and p not dividing b.
