LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Structure of the invariant eigenprojection of a quasi-compact Markov kernel
   If a Markov kernel P is power-bounded and quasi-compact on a weighted-supremum space B_V and 1 is its only eigenvalue of modulus one, then P^n converges in operator norm to a finite-rank projection Π onto Ker(P−I), and Πf=∑_{k=1}^mμ_k(f)f_k for linearly independent nonnegative P-harmonic f_k∈B_V and P-invariant probabilities μ_k with μ_k(V)<∞.
   Source: explains, Theorem 1 and proof of ⇒, pp. 1090-1091
   Note: Representation Πf=∑μ_i(f)v_i of the (Cesàro) limit projection of a quasi-compact Markov kernel on B_w; GHL quote it for Corollary 6. Recorded as explains; Wu 2004 Prop. 4.6 not inspected, so no originality judgment.
