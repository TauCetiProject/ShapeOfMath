LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Non-modularity modulo p of the p-depleted Eisenstein divisor series
   Let p≥5 be prime and k a positive integer divisible by p−1. The reduction modulo p of ∑_(n≥1, p∤n)σ_(k−1)(n)qⁿ is not the reduction of any level-one modular form with p-integral rational coefficients and weight divisible by p−1.
   Source: explains, §2.2, Lemme with proof, p.416-11
   Note: Cited by Serre 1973 §1.3 as “le lemme de [20], p.416-11”.
