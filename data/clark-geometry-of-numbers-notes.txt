LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Covolume of a full lattice in Euclidean space
   For a full lattice Λ=ℤe₁+⋯+ℤeₙ⊂ℝⁿ, its covolume is the Lebesgue measure of the fundamental parallelepiped {∑aᵢeᵢ:0≤aᵢ<1}, which equals |det(e₁,…,eₙ)|. It does not depend on the ℤ-basis, since two bases differ by an integer matrix of determinant ±1.
   Source: explains, §1.3, p.7
   Note: Definition of covolume; not a priority claim.

2. Closedness of locally compact subgroups
   If G is a Hausdorff topological group and H is a subgroup that is locally compact in the subspace topology, then H is closed in G. In particular every discrete subgroup of a Hausdorff topological group is closed.
   Source: explains, §1.1 Proposition 1.1
   Note: Statement and proof; not a priority claim.

3. Structure of discrete subgroups of a finite-dimensional real vector space
   Every discrete subgroup Λ of a finite-dimensional real vector space V is generated over ℤ by a finite ℝ-linearly independent family; in particular Λ is a free abelian group of rank at most dim V. The quotient V/Λ is compact exactly when that family is a real basis of V.
   Source: explains, §1.1 Theorem 1.2; §1.3 Proposition 1.6
   Note: Exposition and proof; not a priority claim.
