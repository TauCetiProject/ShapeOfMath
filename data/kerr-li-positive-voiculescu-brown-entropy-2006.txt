LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Complete positivity passes to opposite C*-algebras
   For C*-algebras A,B let Aop,Bop have reversed multiplication and the same star and norm. If φ:A→B is completely positive, then the same linear map φop:Aop→Bop is completely positive; contractivity is also preserved.
   Source: explains, Lemma 2.2, p. 4
   Note: States and proves the lemma (proof credited to G. Elliott); originality not assessed.

2. Opposite C*-algebra
   For a C*-algebra A, the opposite C*-algebra Aᵒᵖ has the same Banach space, involution and norm as A with reversed multiplication a·b:=ba; it is again a C*-algebra. A linear map φ:A→B induces the same linear map φᵒᵖ:Aᵒᵖ→Bᵒᵖ.
   Source: explains, Notation 2.1, p. 4
   Note: Recalled construction.
