LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Local fundamental solution for an elliptic operator
   An elliptic differential operator has a distributional local fundamental solution at a point: applying the operator to the solution gives the Dirac distribution there. Sufficiently high elliptic powers have fundamental solutions with arbitrarily prescribed finite differentiability also at the source, while elliptic regularity gives smoothness away from it.
   Source: explains, §7.3 Theorem 7.3.1 (p. 174), with the Example after Theorem 7.4.1
   Note: Local two-sided solution operator for operators of constant strength, including elliptic operators; applied to δ it gives local fundamental solutions.

2. Hörmander’s local solution operator for operators of constant strength
   Let P(x,D) have C^∞ coefficients and constant strength near x₀. On every sufficiently small neighbourhood Ω of x₀ there is a linear map E:ℰ′(ℝⁿ)→ℰ′(Ω) with P(x,D)Ef=f and EP(x,D)u=u in Ω, and ‖Ef‖_{p,P̃₀k}≤C_k‖f‖_{p,k} for every temperate weight k (Hörmander, Theorem 7.3.1).
   Source: explains, §7.3 Theorem 7.3.1
   Note: Statement and proof.
