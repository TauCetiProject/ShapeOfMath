LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Pointwise measurable class of real functions
   A class G of real measurable functions on a measurable space S is pointwise measurable if it has a countable subclass G₀ such that every g∈G is the pointwise limit of a sequence in G₀. Then suprema over G agree pointwise with suprema over G₀.
   Source: explains, §4.2, Definition
