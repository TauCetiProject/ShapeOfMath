LOCATED CONCEPTS (5)

1. Carayol local–global compatibility for modular forms
   Let F be totally real and π a cuspidal automorphic representation of GL₂(A_F) whose archimedean components are discrete series D_(k_τ,w) with all k_τ≥2 of the parity of w, as for a Hilbert newform, in particular a classical newform of weight ≥2 over ℚ; if [F:ℚ] is even, assume π_v essentially square-integrable at some finite v. Then π has a strictly compatible system of two-dimensional λ-adic Galois representations whose restriction to the Weil group at each finite place 𝔭 of residue characteristic different from λ's is the λ-adic realization of the Weil–Deligne representation attached to π_𝔭 by the Hecke-normalized local Langlands correspondence. Hence its local L-factor and conductor at 𝔭 are those of π_𝔭.
   Source: explains, Théorème (A), §0.7
   Note: Carayol's own theorem; recorded as the inspected proof (introduction evidence not reviewed here).

2. Totally real number field
   A number field F is totally real if every field embedding F→ℂ has image contained in ℝ.
   Source: explains, §0.1
   Note: Selected reference; not a priority claim.

3. Discrete series representation D_(k,w) of GL₂(ℝ)
   For integers k≥2 and w of the same parity, D_(k,w) is the irreducible essentially square-integrable representation of GL₂(ℝ) occurring in the representation unitarily induced from the characters t↦|t|^((k−1−w)/2)sgn(t)^k and t↦|t|^((1−k−w)/2) of the diagonal torus; its central character is t↦t^(−w). These are the archimedean components of automorphic representations attached to holomorphic modular forms of weight k.
   Source: explains, §0.2
   Note: Selected reference; not a priority claim.

4. Weil–Deligne representation of a nonarchimedean local field
   For a nonarchimedean local field K with Weil group W_K and residue field of q elements, a Weil–Deligne representation over a field E of characteristic zero is a pair (ρ,N) of a homomorphism ρ:W_K→GL(V) on a finite-dimensional E-vector space, trivial on an open subgroup of inertia, and a nilpotent endomorphism N of V with ρ(w)Nρ(w)⁻¹=q^(−v(w))N, v(w) the power of geometric Frobenius induced by w. It is Frobenius-semisimple if ρ is semisimple. For E=E_λ with λ prime to q, w↦ρ(w)exp(t_λ(w)N) is a continuous λ-adic representation of W_K, its λ-adic realization; by Grothendieck's monodromy theorem every continuous λ-adic representation of W_K arises in this way.
   Source: explains, §0.5–0.6
   Note: Selected reference; not a priority claim.

5. Local Langlands correspondence for GL₂
   For a nonarchimedean local field F there is a unique bijection π↦σ(π) from irreducible admissible infinite-dimensional representations of GL₂(F) to two-dimensional Frobenius-semisimple Weil–Deligne representations of F over ℂ (with ρ irreducible or N≠0 when σ is not a sum of characters) such that, for every character χ of F^×, L(s,χπ)=L(s,χ⊗σ(π)) and ε(s,χπ,ψ)=ε(s,χ⊗σ(π),ψ); then det σ(π) corresponds to the central character and σ(χπ)=χ⊗σ(π). It was established by Jacquet–Langlands, Tunnell and Kutzko. Carayol's Hecke normalization is σ(π̃⊗|·|^(1/2)) in this normalization, compatible with automorphisms of the coefficient field.
   Source: explains, §0.5
   Note: Carayol cites Kutzko for the local correspondence; recorded only as the statement source.
