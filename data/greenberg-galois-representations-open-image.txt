LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Lifting character eigen-elements under coprime action on a pro-p group
   Let a finite abelian group Ω of exponent dividing p−1 act by continuous automorphisms on a topologically finitely generated pro-p group Π, and let ξ:Ω→ℤ_p^× be a character. Every ξ-eigenvector of the Frattini quotient of Π lifts to π∈Π with α(π)=π^ξ(α) for all α∈Ω. Consequently Π has a minimal topological generating set consisting of such eigen-elements.
   Source: explains, Prop. 2.1.1
   Note: Greenberg attributes the result to Boston [Bos], Prop. 2.3; recorded only as the inspected proof.

2. p-adic powers of an element of a pro-p group
   For a pro-p group Π, an element π∈Π and a∈ℤ_p, the power π^a is the limit of the integer powers π^(a_n) for any integers a_n converging to a in ℤ_p. It is well defined, lies in the closure of the cyclic subgroup generated by π, and satisfies π^(a+b)=π^aπ^b and (π^a)^b=π^(ab).
   Source: explains, §2.1, p. 5
