REQUIRED CONCEPT FRONTIER (14)

- Character idempotents for a prime-to-l cyclic group over Z_l: Let ℓ be odd and Δ cyclic of order d dividing ℓ−1. For every character χ:Δ→ℤ_ℓ×, e_χ=d^(−1)∑_(δ∈Δ)χ(δ)^(−1)δ is an idempotent in ℤ_ℓ[Δ]. These idempotents are pairwise orthogonal and sum to one, giving M=⊕_χ e_χM for every ℤ_ℓ[Δ]-module M.
- Coincidence of initial radicals for a logarithmically principal field: Let K contain μ_(2ℓ), with |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓᵐ. If its ℓ-primary logarithmic class group is trivial, then its initial ℤ_ℓ-extension radical, the ℓᵐ-torsion of its universal Tate kernel, and the ℓᵐ-torsion of its Hilbertian radical coincide.
- Cyclotomic binomial unit congruence: Let ℓ be prime and m≥1, with m≥2 if ℓ=2. For every n≥0, ((1+ℓᵐ)^(ℓⁿ)−1)/ℓ^(m+n) belongs to 1+ℓℤ_ℓ. In particular its numerator has ℓ-adic valuation m+n.
- Exact sequence for rationalizing a Z_l-module: For a ℤ_ℓ-module M let M_tor={x:ℓʳx=0 for some r≥0}. There is an exact sequence 0→M/M_tor→ℚ_ℓ⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)M→(ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)M→0 induced by ℤ_ℓ⊆ℚ_ℓ.
- Finite generation of the S-unit group: For a number field K and finite S containing the infinite places, the S-unit group is isomorphic to μ(K)×ℤʳ for a finite integer r, where μ(K) is the finite group of roots of unity in K.
- Intersection of ordinary and twisted cyclotomic radical invariants: In the cyclotomic setup with |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓᵐ and γ*=γ/(1+ℓᵐ), one has ℛ_(K∞)^(Γ_n)∩ℛ_(K∞)^(Γ_n*)=ℛ_(K_n)[ℓ^(m+n)], where Γ_n is generated by γ^(ℓⁿ) and Γ_n* by (γ*)^(ℓⁿ).
- Kummer descent in a cyclotomic Z_l-tower: Let K contain μ_(2ℓ), with |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓ^m, and put γ*=γ/(1+ℓ^m). The Kummer radical of K_n^ab K∞/K∞ is ℛ_(K∞)^(Γ_n*), where Γ_n* is generated by (γ*)^(ℓ^n). The radical for the compositum of all ℤ_ℓ-extensions of K_n is its maximal ℓ-divisible subgroup.
- L-adic Tate twist of a Galois module: For a ℤ_ℓ-module M with a Galois action and cyclotomic character χ_ℓ, its i-th Tate twist M(i), i∈ℤ, is the same underlying module with action σ⋅_i m=χ_ℓ(σ)^i(σ⋅m). Equivalently tensor with the rank-one module ℤ_ℓ(i), using the dual tensor power for negative i.
- Norm-coherent principal ideles and logarithmic units: For a cyclotomic tower K∞/K with K containing μ_(2ℓ), norm-compatible sequences in ℝ_(K_n)=ℤ_ℓ⊗K_n× are exactly the norm-compatible sequences of logarithmic units. Their projection to level zero is ⋂_n N_(K_n/K)(Ẽ_(K_n)).
- One-variable Iwasawa algebra over Z_l: For a prime ℓ and a procyclic group Γ with a chosen topological generator γ, its one-variable Iwasawa algebra is Λ=ℤ_ℓ[[T]], with γ represented by 1+T. This fixes the coordinate convention used for the completed group algebra.
- Perfect Kummer character pairing: Let n=ℓʳ be invertible in a field F containing μ_n, D⊆F×/(F×)ⁿ a subgroup, and L=F(x^(1/n):[x]∈D). Then Gal(L/F)×D→μ_n, (σ,[x])↦σ(x^(1/n))/x^(1/n), is a perfect character pairing. Passing to limits gives Gal(L/F) and the universal ℓ-primary Kummer radical as character duals over fields containing all ℓ-power roots of unity.
- Snake lemma for modules: For a commuting diagram of R-modules with exact rows 0→A→B→C→0 and 0→A′→B′→C′→0 and vertical maps a,b,c, there is a natural exact sequence 0→ker a→ker b→ker c→coker a→coker b→coker c→0.
- Tate’s cyclotomic invariant quotient for the l-primary K2: For K containing μ_(2ℓ), let I_n=(T_ℓ(μ)⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)ℛ_(K∞))^(Gal(K∞/K_n)). The ℓ-primary torsion subgroup of K₂^M(K_n) is canonically I_n/(I_n)_div, where (I_n)_div denotes its maximal divisible subgroup.
- Universal-radical invariants in a cyclotomic tower: For K containing μ_(2ℓ) and K∞/K its cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension, the natural map ℛ_(K_n)→ℛ_(K∞) is injective and has image ℛ_(K∞)^(Gal(K∞/K_n)).

LOCATED CONCEPTS (31)

1. Kummer descent in a cyclotomic Z_l-tower
   Let K contain μ_(2ℓ), with |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓ^m, and put γ*=γ/(1+ℓ^m). The Kummer radical of K_n^ab K∞/K∞ is ℛ_(K∞)^(Γ_n*), where Γ_n* is generated by (γ*)^(ℓ^n). The radical for the compositum of all ℤ_ℓ-extensions of K_n is its maximal ℓ-divisible subgroup.
   Source: relies_on, Theorem 3; §2
   Note: Imported description used to prove the norm-limit theorem.

2. One-variable Iwasawa algebra over Z_l
   For a prime ℓ and a procyclic group Γ with a chosen topological generator γ, its one-variable Iwasawa algebra is Λ=ℤ_ℓ[[T]], with γ represented by 1+T. This fixes the coordinate convention used for the completed group algebra.
   Source: relies_on, Conventions; Lemma 5
   Note: Actions of γ and the modified γ* are compared in a polynomial quotient.

3. Inverse limit of groups
   For groups G_n and homomorphisms f_n:G_(n+1)→G_n, the inverse limit is the subgroup of ∏G_n consisting of tuples (x_n) satisfying f_n(x_(n+1))=x_n for every n; for topological groups it carries the subspace topology of the product.
   Source: relies_on, §§2–4
   Note: Norm-compatible sequences and their initial coordinates.

4. Norm in a finite field extension
   For a finite field extension L/K, N_(L/K)(a) is the determinant of the K-linear multiplication map x↦ax on L.
   Source: relies_on, §§2–4
   Note: Transition maps in every inverse system.

5. Universal l-primary Kummer radical of a field
   For a field K and a prime ℓ, its universal ℓ-primary radical is ℛ_K=(ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)⊗_ℤK×. Torsion in K× disappears in this tensor product, so a representative ℓ^(−k)⊗x is zero exactly when x is a root of unity times an ℓ^k-th power in K.
   Source: relies_on, Definition 1; Proposition 2
   Note: Ambient divisible torsion modules.

6. L-adified multiplicative group of a field
   For a field F, the ℓ-adified multiplicative group used here is ℝ_F=ℤ_ℓ⊗_ℤF×. This tensor product, called the group of principal idèles in ℓ-adic class field theory, is distinct from an unrestricted inverse-limit completion unless an equivalence has been checked.
   Source: relies_on, §2 after Theorem 6
   Note: Quotient formulation of the radical.

7. Second Milnor K-group of a field
   For a field F, K₂^M(F) is (F×⊗_ℤF×) modulo the subgroup generated by a⊗(1−a) for a≠0,1. The class {a,b} is its universal Steinberg symbol, bilinear in both multiplicative arguments.
   Source: relies_on, §4
   Note: Universal Steinberg symbols and their kernels.

8. L-adic Tate module of roots of unity
   The Tate module T_ℓ(μ) is lim←μ_(ℓ^n), with transition maps ζ↦ζ^ℓ and the natural Galois action.
   Source: relies_on, §4
   Note: Tate twisting of the Kummer radical.

9. L-adic cyclotomic character
   The ℓ-adic cyclotomic character χ_ℓ of an absolute Galois group is the continuous character to ℤ_ℓ× characterized by σ(ζ)=ζ^(χ_ℓ(σ)) for every root of unity ζ of ℓ-power order.
   Source: relies_on, Conventions and §4
   Note: Explains both the γ* and inverse-γ twist conventions.

10. Universal l-primary Kummer radical of a field
   For a field K and a prime ℓ, its universal ℓ-primary radical is ℛ_K=(ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)⊗_ℤK×. Torsion in K× disappears in this tensor product, so a representative ℓ^(−k)⊗x is zero exactly when x is a root of unity times an ℓ^k-th power in K.
   Source: explains, Definition 1
   Note: Recalled or specified definition; earlier sources are credited.

11. Initial Kummer radical of Z_l-extensions
   Suppose K contains μ_(2ℓ), |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓ^m, and Z is the compositum of all ℤ_ℓ-extensions of K. Its initial radical is {ℓ^(−m)⊗x∈ℛ_K:K∞(x^(1/ℓ^m))⊆Z}, regarded inside the universal radical of K∞.
   Source: explains, Theorem 6
   Note: Recalled or specified definition; earlier sources are credited.

12. Universal Tate kernel in a cyclotomic Kummer radical
   For K containing μ_(2ℓ), write Γ=Gal(K∞/K) with χ_ℓ(γ)=1+ℓ^m. Let barΓ* be generated by γ^(−1)/(1+ℓ^m), acting on ℛ_(K∞). The universal Tate kernel is the maximal divisible subgroup of ℛ_(K∞)^(barΓ*); its ℓ^m-torsion is the initial exponent-ℓ^m kernel studied here.
   Source: explains, §4 before Theorem 11
   Note: Recalled or specified definition; earlier sources are credited.

13. Jaulent’s norm-limit description of the initial Z_l radical
   In the preceding cyclotomic setup, the initial Kummer radical is the projection to n=0 of lim←ℛ_(K_n)^(Γ*), with norm transition maps. Equivalently it is π₀ lim←(ℝ_(K_n)/(ℝ_(K_n)^(γ*−1)μ_(K_n))), where ℝ_F=ℤ_ℓ⊗F× and γ*=γ/(1+ℓ^m).
   Source: introduces, Theorem 6 and Scolie 7
   Note: New description of an existing mathematical object.
   Origin evidence: The abstract and introduction explicitly claim a new exact norm-limit description, derived from earlier Kummer descriptions in [6–7]. §3 distinguishes this equality from Seo’s weaker inclusions and explains the denominator correction; the old radical itself and Lemma 5 are not claimed as new.

14. Jaulent’s norm-limit description of the Tate kernel
   In the same cyclotomic setup, the ℓ^m-torsion of the universal Tate kernel is π₀ lim←ℛ_(K_n)^(barΓ*), equivalently π₀ lim←(ℝ_(K_n)/(ℝ_(K_n)^(barγ*−1)μ_(K_n))), with norm transitions and barγ*=γ^(−1)/(1+ℓ^m).
   Source: introduces, Theorem 11
   Note: New description of an existing mathematical object.
   Origin evidence: The introduction and §4 explicitly present the parallel norm-limit description as the additional contribution. Tate’s identification of K₂ and the earlier universal kernel are credited to [17] and [6–7], rather than introduced here.

15. Snake lemma for modules
   For a commuting diagram of R-modules with exact rows 0→A→B→C→0 and 0→A′→B′→C′→0 and vertical maps a,b,c, there is a natural exact sequence 0→ker a→ker b→ker c→coker a→coker b→coker c→0.
   Source: relies_on, §2 after Theorem6
   Note: The coefficient modules have torsion and are not vector spaces; this is the precise snake lemma used.

16. S-unit group of a number field
   For a number field K and a finite set S of its places containing all archimedean places, its S-unit group is E_S={x∈K×:|x|_v=1 for every v∉S}. The definition is independent of the representative absolute value of each place.
   Source: relies_on, §2 after Theorem6
   Note: Every finite tensor expression belongs to a finite-dimensional S-unit subspace.

17. Finite generation of the S-unit group
   For a number field K and finite S containing the infinite places, the S-unit group is isomorphic to μ(K)×ℤʳ for a finite integer r, where μ(K) is the finite group of roots of unity in K.
   Source: relies_on, §2 after Theorem6
   Note: Gives the needed finite-dimensional Q_l spaces.

18. Universal-radical invariants in a cyclotomic tower
   For K containing μ_(2ℓ) and K∞/K its cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension, the natural map ℛ_(K_n)→ℛ_(K∞) is injective and has image ℛ_(K∞)^(Gal(K∞/K_n)).
   Source: relies_on, Proposition2 and Theorem6 proof
   Note: Identifies finite-layer tensors inside the infinite radical.

19. Perfect Kummer character pairing
   Let n=ℓʳ be invertible in a field F containing μ_n, D⊆F×/(F×)ⁿ a subgroup, and L=F(x^(1/n):[x]∈D). Then Gal(L/F)×D→μ_n, (σ,[x])↦σ(x^(1/n))/x^(1/n), is a perfect character pairing. Passing to limits gives Gal(L/F) and the universal ℓ-primary Kummer radical as character duals over fields containing all ℓ-power roots of unity.
   Source: relies_on, §1 after Proposition2
   Note: Describes the Galois groups and tracks the cyclotomic character twist.

20. Tate’s cyclotomic invariant quotient for the l-primary K2
   For K containing μ_(2ℓ), let I_n=(T_ℓ(μ)⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)ℛ_(K∞))^(Gal(K∞/K_n)). The ℓ-primary torsion subgroup of K₂^M(K_n) is canonically I_n/(I_n)_div, where (I_n)_div denotes its maximal divisible subgroup.
   Source: relies_on, §4 preceding Theorem11
   Note: This exact imported K₂ theorem identifies the universal symbol kernel.

21. Cyclotomic binomial unit congruence
   Let ℓ be prime and m≥1, with m≥2 if ℓ=2. For every n≥0, ((1+ℓᵐ)^(ℓⁿ)−1)/ℓ^(m+n) belongs to 1+ℓℤ_ℓ. In particular its numerator has ℓ-adic valuation m+n.
   Source: relies_on, Lemma5
   Note: Controls the unit factors relating norms and ℓth powers.

22. Exact sequence for rationalizing a Z_l-module
   For a ℤ_ℓ-module M let M_tor={x:ℓʳx=0 for some r≥0}. There is an exact sequence 0→M/M_tor→ℚ_ℓ⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)M→(ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)⊗_(ℤ_ℓ)M→0 induced by ℤ_ℓ⊆ℚ_ℓ.
   Source: relies_on, §2 before Scolie7
   Note: Accounts for the actual tensor exact sequence, independently of the snake lemma.

23. Coincidence of initial radicals for a logarithmically principal field
   Let K contain μ_(2ℓ), with |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓᵐ. If its ℓ-primary logarithmic class group is trivial, then its initial ℤ_ℓ-extension radical, the ℓᵐ-torsion of its universal Tate kernel, and the ℓᵐ-torsion of its Hilbertian radical coincide.
   Source: relies_on, Final remark(iii)
   Note: This is the exact prior sufficient-condition result invoked in the final comparison of radicals.

24. Global logarithmic unit group
   For a number field K and prime ℓ, its logarithmic unit group is the subgroup of ℤ_ℓ⊗_ℤK× whose image in ℛ_(K_v) belongs to the local logarithmic unit group for every finite place v of K. These canonical local images are induced by K×→K_v×.
   Source: relies_on, §3 before Corollary10; §4 Corollary13
   Note: Identifies the norm-compatible principal-idèle coordinates.

25. Cyclotomic norms of logarithmic units
   For K∞=⋃K_n the cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension, its group of cyclotomic norms of logarithmic units is N_(K∞/K)(Ẽ_(K∞))=⋂_(n≥0)N_(K_n/K)(Ẽ_(K_n)), a subgroup of Ẽ_K; here Ẽ denotes the global logarithmic unit group. This is a global norm intersection inside ℤ_ℓ⊗K×.
   Source: relies_on, Corollaries10,13
   Note: Specifies the global norm subgroup whose image lies in both radicals.

26. Norm-coherent principal ideles and logarithmic units
   For a cyclotomic tower K∞/K with K containing μ_(2ℓ), norm-compatible sequences in ℝ_(K_n)=ℤ_ℓ⊗K_n× are exactly the norm-compatible sequences of logarithmic units. Their projection to level zero is ⋂_n N_(K_n/K)(Ẽ_(K_n)).
   Source: relies_on, §3 before Corollary10
   Note: Justifies the inverse-limit and projection identifications.

27. L-primary logarithmic class group of a number field
   For a number field K containing μ_(2ℓ), let K^lc be the compositum of its locally cyclotomic abelian pro-ℓ extensions. Its ℓ-primary logarithmic class group is Gal(K^lc/K∞), where K∞/K is cyclotomic. This is the degree-zero logarithmic class-group convention.
   Source: relies_on, Final remark(iii)
   Note: Triviality is a stated sufficient condition for the equality of radicals.

28. Hilbertian radical in a cyclotomic tower
   For K containing μ_(2ℓ), its Hilbertian radical is the subgroup of ℛ_K consisting of classes ℓ^(−r)⊗x such that, for every finite layer K_n of K∞/K, x lies in N_(K_n/K)(K_n×)(K×)^(ℓʳ).
   Source: relies_on, Final remark(iii)
   Note: Identifies the third radical in that sufficient-condition assertion.

29. Character idempotents for a prime-to-l cyclic group over Z_l
   Let ℓ be odd and Δ cyclic of order d dividing ℓ−1. For every character χ:Δ→ℤ_ℓ×, e_χ=d^(−1)∑_(δ∈Δ)χ(δ)^(−1)δ is an idempotent in ℤ_ℓ[Δ]. These idempotents are pairwise orthogonal and sum to one, giving M=⊕_χ e_χM for every ℤ_ℓ[Δ]-module M.
   Source: relies_on, Final remark(ii)
   Note: Provides the actual prime-to-ℓ eigenspace descent operation.

30. L-adic Tate twist of a Galois module
   For a ℤ_ℓ-module M with a Galois action and cyclotomic character χ_ℓ, its i-th Tate twist M(i), i∈ℤ, is the same underlying module with action σ⋅_i m=χ_ℓ(σ)^i(σ⋅m). Equivalently tensor with the rank-one module ℤ_ℓ(i), using the dual tensor power for negative i.
   Source: relies_on, Final remarks(i)–(ii)
   Note: Explains the sign and character shift between the two kernels.

31. Intersection of ordinary and twisted cyclotomic radical invariants
   In the cyclotomic setup with |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓᵐ and γ*=γ/(1+ℓᵐ), one has ℛ_(K∞)^(Γ_n)∩ℛ_(K∞)^(Γ_n*)=ℛ_(K_n)[ℓ^(m+n)], where Γ_n is generated by γ^(ℓⁿ) and Γ_n* by (γ*)^(ℓⁿ).
   Source: relies_on, Scolie4 and Theorem6 proof
   Note: Places successively divisible classes in the correct finite layer.

32. Leopoldt conjecture in the Z_l-extension form
   For every number field K and prime ℓ, if Z is the compositum of all ℤ_ℓ-extensions of K and r₂ is the number of complex places of K, then Gal(Z/K) is isomorphic as a topological group to ℤ_ℓ^(r₂+1).
   Source: explains, Introduction
   Note: Contextual motivation; the paper proves its norm identities without assuming Leopoldt.

ADDITIONAL PREREQUISITES (122)

- Abelian group [definition]: A group whose operation is commutative.
- Absolute Galois group [definition]: The group Gal(F^sep/F), with its Krull topology, where F^sep is a separable closure.
- Absolute value on a field [definition]: A map |·|:K→R_{≥0} that vanishes exactly at zero, is multiplicative and satisfies |x+y|≤|x|+|y|. It is nonarchimedean if the stronger bound |x+y|≤max(|x|,|y|) holds.
- Algebraic closure of a field [definition]: An algebraic extension of a field that is itself algebraically closed. This specifies an object, without including the existence or uniqueness-up-to-isomorphism theorem.
- Algebraic element of a field extension [definition]: An element α of L/F is algebraic over F if a nonzero polynomial in F[x] vanishes at α.
- Algebraically closed field [definition]: A field in which every nonconstant polynomial has a root.
- Associative algebra over a field [definition]: A vector space with an associative bilinear multiplication and a multiplicative identity.
- Basis of a free module [definition]: A family (e_i) in a module over a commutative ring such that every element is a unique finite linear combination of the e_i with coefficients in that ring. A module with such a family is free.
- Basis of a vector space [definition]: A linearly independent family of vectors whose linear span is the whole vector space.
- Bijection between sets [definition]: A function for which each element of the codomain is the image of exactly one element of the domain.
- Bilinear map [definition]: A map of two vector-space variables that is linear in each variable separately.
- Binary operation on a set [definition]: A function S×S→S.
- Binary relation on a set [definition]: A subset R⊆S×S; writing xRy means (x,y)∈R.
- Cartesian product of sets [definition]: A×B is the set of ordered pairs (a,b) with a∈A and b∈B.
- Cauchy sequence in a metric space [definition]: A sequence (xₙ) in a metric space (X,d) is Cauchy if for every ε>0 there is N such that d(xₙ,xₘ)<ε whenever n,m≥N.
- Cokernel of a module homomorphism [definition]: For an R-module homomorphism f:M→N, its cokernel is the quotient module N/f(M).
- Commutative ring with identity [definition]: An abelian group under addition, together with an associative commutative multiplication with identity, distributing over addition. The zero ring is allowed.
- Complete metric space [definition]: A metric space is complete if every Cauchy sequence in it converges to a point of that same space.
- Completion of a field with an absolute value [definition]: The metric completion of K for d(x,y)=|x−y|, with the field operations extended continuously. A completion is regarded as containing K through its canonical isometric embedding.
- Completion of a metric space [definition]: A complete metric space containing an isometric dense copy of the given metric space. It is unique up to an isometry fixing that copy.
- Composition of functions [definition]: For f:A→B and g:B→C, the composite g∘f:A→C sends x to g(f(x)).
- Compositum of field extensions [definition]: For subfields L_i of one overfield containing K, their compositum is the smallest subfield containing every L_i.
- Continuous map between topological spaces [definition]: A map is continuous if the preimage of every open subset of its target is open in its domain.
- Convergence of a sequence in a metric space [definition]: A sequence (xₙ) in a metric space (X,d) converges to x∈X if for every ε>0 there is N such that d(xₙ,x)<ε whenever n≥N.
- Cyclic group [definition]: A group G is cyclic if there is an element g such that every element of G equals gⁿ for some integer n. A finite cyclic group has a finite order equal to the order of a generator.
- Cyclotomic Z_l-extension of a nonarchimedean local field [definition]: For a finite extension F of ℚ_p and a prime ℓ, the cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension Fᶜ/F is the unique ℤ_ℓ subextension of F(μ_(ℓ∞))/F. When F contains μ_(2ℓ), adjoining all ℓ-power roots of unity already gives this extension.
- Cyclotomic Z_l-extension of a number field [definition]: The cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension K∞/K is the unique ℤ_ℓ-extension of K contained in the extension obtained by adjoining all roots of unity of ℓ-power order. If K contains μ_(2ℓ) and |μ_(ℓ∞)(K)|=ℓ^m, its degree-ℓ^n layer is K_n=K(μ_(ℓ^(m+n))).
- Dense subset of a metric space [definition]: A subset A of a metric space X is dense if every open ball centered in X with positive radius meets A.
- Determinant of a square matrix [definition]: For an n×n matrix A over a commutative ring, det A=∑_{σ∈Sₙ}sgn(σ)∏_{i=1}ⁿA_{i,σ(i)}, with the 0×0 determinant equal to one.
- Direct sum of abelian groups [definition]: For a family of abelian groups A_i, the subgroup of their Cartesian product consisting of tuples with finite support, with coordinatewise addition.
- Discrete valuation on a field [definition]: A surjective map v:K×→Z satisfying v(xy)=v(x)+v(y) and v(x+y)≥min(v(x),v(y)), extended by v(0)=+∞.
- Divisibility of integers [definition]: An integer a divides b if b=ac for some integer c.
- Divisible abelian group [definition]: An abelian group D is divisible if for every positive integer n the map x↦nx is surjective. For a prime ℓ, ℓ-divisibility means this only for n=ℓ.
- Exact sequence of modules [definition]: A sequence of homomorphisms of modules over one commutative ring is exact at a module if the image of the incoming map equals the kernel of the outgoing map; it is exact if this holds at every term.
- Field [definition]: A commutative ring in which 1≠0 and every nonzero element is invertible.
- Field embedding [definition]: An injective homomorphism of fields preserving the multiplicative identity.
- Field extension [definition]: An inclusion of one field into another.
- Field of p-adic numbers [definition]: For a prime p, ℚ_p is the completion of ℚ for the p-adic absolute value |x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}.
- Finite group algebra over a commutative ring [definition]: For a finite group G and commutative ring R, R[G] is the free R-module on symbols g∈G, with multiplication extended R-bilinearly from the group law. It need not be commutative if G is not abelian.
- Finite set [definition]: A set X is finite if it is bijective with {1,…,n} for some natural number n≥0; its cardinality is n.
- Finite-dimensional vector space [definition]: A vector space having a finite basis.
- Formal power series over a commutative ring [definition]: An expression ∑a_α X^α with coefficients in a commutative ring and multi-indices α in N^n, added coefficientwise and multiplied by the finite Cauchy sums defining each coefficient, without any assertion of analytic convergence.
- Free abelian group [definition]: An abelian group with a family of elements such that every group element is a unique finite integer linear combination of them; the number of basis elements is its rank.
- Function between sets [definition]: An assignment taking each element of a specified domain set to exactly one element of a specified codomain set.
- Galois field extension [definition]: An algebraic field extension that is both normal and separable.
- Galois group of a finite Galois extension [definition]: The group of field automorphisms of the extension that fix the base field pointwise.
- Galois group of an algebraic Galois extension [definition]: For an algebraic Galois extension L/K, Gal(L/K) is the group of K-automorphisms of L with the Krull topology, whose basic identity neighborhoods fix finite intermediate extensions pointwise.
- Group [definition]: A set with an associative binary operation, an identity element, and a two-sided inverse for each element.
- Group action on a set [definition]: A map G×X→X, written (g,x)↦gx, satisfying ex=x and (gh)x=g(hx).
- Group homomorphism [definition]: A map f:G→H satisfying f(xy)=f(x)f(y) for all x,y.
- Homomorphism of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive map f:M→N of modules over the same commutative ring satisfying f(rm)=rf(m).
- Ideal of a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup I of a commutative ring R such that ri belongs to I for every r in R and i in I.
- Idempotent element of an algebra [definition]: An element e satisfying e²=e.
- Integer [definition]: An integer is an element of the ordered ring ℤ, obtained by adjoining additive inverses to the natural numbers.
- Intersection of a family of sets [definition]: Inside a specified ambient set, the elements belonging to every member of the family.
- Invariant submodule of a group action [definition]: If a group G acts by R-linear automorphisms on an R-module M, its invariant submodule is M^G={m∈M:g·m=m for every g∈G}.
- Invertible element of an algebra [definition]: An element with a two-sided multiplicative inverse.
- Irreducible polynomial [definition]: A nonzero nonunit polynomial over a specified coefficient ring that cannot be written as a product of two nonunits. Over a field, both factors would have smaller positive degree.
- Kernel of a module homomorphism [definition]: For an R-module homomorphism f:M→N, its kernel is the submodule {x∈M:f(x)=0}.
- Krull topology on an infinite Galois group [definition]: On Gal(L/F), the subgroup fixing each finite intermediate extension E/F is an open neighbourhood of the identity; their cosets form a basis. For an absolute Galois group take L=F^sep.
- Kummer radical of an abelian pro-l extension [definition]: If F contains all ℓ-power roots of unity and L/F is abelian pro-ℓ, its Kummer radical consists of the classes ℓ^(−k)⊗x in (ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ)⊗F× for which F(x^(1/ℓ^k)) lies in L.
- L-primary character dual of an abelian pro-l group [definition]: For an abelian pro-ℓ group G, its ℓ-primary character dual is G∨=Hom_cont(G,ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ), where the target and the dual group have the discrete topology. For a discrete ℓ-primary torsion group A, write A∨=Hom(A,ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ) with the topology of pointwise convergence.
- L-primary completion of a local multiplicative group [definition]: For a nonarchimedean local field F and prime ℓ, the ℓ-primary completion of its multiplicative group is ℛ_F=lim←_r F×/(F×)^(ℓʳ), with the natural quotient transition maps and inverse-limit topology. It carries the continuous norm maps induced by finite local-field extensions.
- Least upper bound in a partially ordered set [definition]: For A⊆P, its least upper bound is an upper bound s satisfying s≤b for every upper bound b of A, when such an s exists.
- Linear combination of vectors [definition]: A finite sum a₁v₁+⋯+aₙvₙ of vectors multiplied by scalars from their vector space’s ground field; the empty sum is zero.
- Linear map between vector spaces [definition]: A map between vector spaces over the same field preserving addition and scalar multiplication.
- Linear span of a set of vectors [definition]: The subspace consisting of all finite linear combinations of a specified set of vectors.
- Linearly independent family of vectors [definition]: A family of vectors is linearly independent if every finite linear combination equalling zero has all coefficients zero.
- Local logarithmic unit group [definition]: For a nonarchimedean local field F, let Fᶜ/F be its cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension with finite layers F_n. Its logarithmic unit group is ⋂_(n≥0)N_(F_n/F)(ℛ_(F_n))⊆ℛ_F, where ℛ_F is the ℓ-primary completion of F×.
- Locally cyclotomic abelian pro-l extension [definition]: For a number field K containing μ_(2ℓ), an abelian pro-ℓ extension L/K is locally cyclotomic if, for every finite intermediate E/K and every finite place v of K, each completion E_w/K_v is contained in the local cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension K_vᶜ/K_v.
- Matrix over a ring [definition]: A rectangular array of elements of a ring; addition is entrywise and matrix multiplication uses row-by-column sums.
- Metric space [definition]: A set X with a function d:X×X→[0,∞) such that d(x,y)=0 exactly when x=y, d(x,y)=d(y,x), and d(x,z)≤d(x,y)+d(y,z).
- Minimal polynomial of an algebraic element [definition]: The unique monic polynomial of least positive degree over F vanishing at an algebraic element α; it generates the ideal of all polynomials over F vanishing at α.
- Module over a commutative ring [definition]: An abelian group M with an action of a commutative ring R satisfying r(m+n)=rm+rn, (r+s)m=rm+sm, (rs)m=r(sm), and 1m=m. Unlike a vector space, its scalar ring need not be a field.
- Multiplicative group of a field [definition]: For a field F, F× is the abelian group of its nonzero elements under multiplication.
- Multiplicity of a polynomial root [definition]: The multiplicity of a root a of a polynomial P is the largest m≥1 for which (x−a)^m divides P. A root of multiplicity1 is simple.
- Natural number [definition]: One of 0, 1, 2, …; natural numbers count finite collections.
- Normal algebraic field extension [definition]: An algebraic field extension L/k is normal if every irreducible polynomial in k[X] with a root in L splits into linear factors over L. Equivalently, the minimal polynomial over k of every element of L splits over L.
- Number field [definition]: A finite extension of ℚ.
- One-dimensional character with values in a ring [definition]: A one-dimensional R-valued character of a group G is a group homomorphism χ:G→R×, where R× is the multiplicative group of units of the commutative ring R.
- Ordered field [definition]: A field with a total order preserved by addition, in which the product of two positive elements is positive.
- Ordered pair [definition]: A pair (x,y) with distinguished first and second entries; (x,y)=(x′,y′) exactly when x=x′ and y=y′.
- Orthogonal idempotents in an algebra [definition]: Idempotents e and f satisfying ef=fe=0.
- P-adic local field [definition]: A p-adic local field is a finite extension K of ℚ_p, equipped with the uniquely extended p-adic topology and valuation.
- Partial order [definition]: A reflexive, antisymmetric and transitive binary relation, usually written ≤.
- Place of a number field [definition]: An equivalence class of nontrivial absolute values on a number field; its completion is denoted F_v. Archimedean and nonarchimedean places give its infinite and finite places.
- Polynomial over a field [definition]: A formal finite sum a₀+a₁x+⋯+aₙxⁿ with coefficients in a field, with the usual coefficientwise addition and multiplication.
- Polynomial ring over a commutative ring [definition]: The ring R[X₁,…,X_n] of formal finite sums of monomials with coefficients in R, with coefficientwise addition and the usual multiplication of monomials.
- Preimage of a subset under a function [definition]: For f:A→B and E⊆B, f⁻¹(E)={x∈A:f(x)∈E}.
- Prime number [definition]: An integer p>1 is prime if its only positive divisors are 1 and p.
- Prime-power torsion subgroup of an abelian group [definition]: For an abelian group A and a prime ℓ, A[ℓ^m]={a:ℓ^m a=0}, and A[ℓ∞]=⋃_(m≥0)A[ℓ^m].
- Pro-l Galois extension [definition]: An algebraic Galois extension L/K is pro-ℓ if its topological Galois group is a pro-ℓ group. It is abelian when that Galois group is abelian.
- Pro-l group [definition]: For a prime ℓ, a pro-ℓ group is a topological group isomorphic to an inverse limit of finite groups of ℓ-power order, the finite groups having the discrete topology.
- Product topology [definition]: On a Cartesian product of topological spaces, the topology with basic open sets restricting finitely many coordinates to open subsets and leaving all other coordinates unrestricted.
- Quotient module [definition]: For a submodule N of an R-module M, the cosets M/N carry addition and scalar multiplication induced from M.
- Quotient of an abelian group by a subgroup [definition]: For an abelian group G and subgroup H,the cosets G/H form an abelian group under (g+H)+(g′+H)=g+g′+H.
- Rational number [definition]: A real x is rational if x=a/b for integers a,b with b≠0; otherwise it is irrational.
- Real number [definition]: A real number is an element of a complete ordered field ℝ; completeness means every nonempty subset bounded above has a least upper bound.
- Ring of p-adic integers [definition]: For a prime p, ℤ_p is the subring {x∈ℚ_p:v_p(x)≥0}, with its p-adic topology.
- Root of unity [definition]: In a field, a root of unity is an element ζ with ζⁿ=1 for some positive integer n; its order is the least such n.
- Separable algebraic field extension [definition]: An algebraic extension L/F is separable if the minimal polynomial over F of every element of L has distinct roots in an algebraic closure.
- Separable closure of a field [definition]: A separable algebraic extension F^sep/F that has no further nontrivial finite separable algebraic extension, equivalently the separable elements in a chosen algebraic closure.
- Separable polynomial over a field [definition]: A polynomial having no repeated root in an algebraic closure of its coefficient field.
- Sequence in a set [definition]: A function from the natural numbers, or the positive natural numbers, to that set.
- Set [definition]: A collection of distinct elements, regarded as one object.
- Sign of a permutation [definition]: The sign of a permutation of {1,…,n} is (−1) to the number of inversions, where an inversion is a pair i<j with σ(i)>σ(j).
- Subfield generated by a subset [definition]: For S⊆L in a field extension L/F, F(S) is the smallest subfield of L containing F and S.
- Subgroup [definition]: A subset of a group containing the identity and closed under multiplication and inverses.
- Submodule of a module over a commutative ring [definition]: An additive subgroup of a module stable under multiplication by every scalar of its coefficient ring.
- Subset [definition]: A⊆B means every element of A is an element of B.
- Symmetric group of a finite set [definition]: The group of all bijections of a finite set to itself under composition.
- Tensor product of modules over a commutative ring [definition]: For R-modules M,N, the R-module M⊗_R N with a bilinear map (m,n)↦m⊗n through which every R-bilinear map M×N→P factors uniquely by an R-linear map.
- Topological group [definition]: A group with a topology for which multiplication G×G→G and inversion G→G are continuous.
- Topological ring [definition]: A ring with a topology for which addition, multiplication and additive inversion are continuous; the rings here are commutative with identity.
- Topological space [definition]: A set X with a family τ of subsets, called open sets, containing ∅ and X and closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections.
- Total order [definition]: A partial order in which any two elements are comparable.
- Union of a family of sets [definition]: The set of elements belonging to at least one member of the family.
- Valuation ring of a discretely valued field [definition]: For a discrete valuation v on K, the ring O_v={x:v(x)≥0}; its maximal ideal is m_v={x:v(x)>0}.
- Vector space [definition]: An abelian group with scalar multiplication by a field satisfying a(v+w)=av+aw, (a+b)v=av+bv, (ab)v=a(bv), and 1v=v.
- Vector subspace [definition]: A subset of a vector space containing zero and closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
- Z_l-extension of a number field [definition]: For a prime ℓ, a ℤ_ℓ-extension of a number field K is an algebraic Galois extension L/K whose topological Galois group is isomorphic to the additive group ℤ_ℓ.
- p-adic valuation of a rational number [definition]: For a prime p and nonzero rational x, v_p(x) is the unique integer r such that x=p^r a/b with integers a,b not divisible by p; put v_p(0)=+∞.
