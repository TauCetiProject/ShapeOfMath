LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Ternary spinor-genus representation error bound
   For a positive definite integral ternary quadratic lattice L and every ε>0, r_L(n)=r_spn(L)(n)+O_(L,ε)(n^(1/2−1/28+ε)) as positive integers n tend to infinity, with no congruence conditions on representing vectors.
   Source: explains, Theorem 3(i) and proof, p. 56
   Note: States and sketches the proof from Iwaniec, Schulze-Pillot and Siegel; originality not assessed.

2. Lower bound for a primitive ternary spinor-genus average
   For a positive definite integral ternary quadratic lattice L and every ε>0, r_spn(L)(n)≥c_(L,ε)n^(1/2−ε) for sufficiently large positive integers n primitively represented by some lattice in the spinor genus of L, with c_(L,ε)>0. The constant is not claimed effective.
   Source: explains, Lemma 5(ii), p. 55
   Note: Recalled from Schulze-Pillot 1984 (Crelle 352), Korollar 2.

3. Weighted spinor-genus representation average
   For a positive definite quadratic ℤ-lattice L, choose representatives M of the finitely many isometry classes in its proper spinor genus. Its weighted representation average is r_spn(L)(n)=(∑_M r_M(n)/|Aut(M)|)/(∑_M 1/|Aut(M)|), where Aut(M) consists of integral isometries preserving M.
   Source: explains, Theorem 2(ii), p. 54
   Note: Recalled definition (with congruence conditions).
