1. Perron splitting of a uniformly positive matrix cocycle
   Let θ be a homeomorphism of compact metric Ω and A:Ω→M_d(ℝ) continuous and nonnegative. Suppose some s≥1 makes A^(s)(ω)=A(θ^(s−1)ω)⋯A(ω) strictly positive for every ω. There are continuous positive unit vectors u(ω),v(ω) such that A(ω)u(ω) is a positive multiple of u(θω), A(ω)ker(v(ω)ᵀ)⊆ker(v(θω)ᵀ), and ℝᵈ=ℝu(ω)⊕ker(v(ω)ᵀ). Some C>0,0<α<1 give ‖A^(n)(ω)b‖≤Cαⁿ‖A^(n)(ω)u(ω)‖ for every unit b⊥v(ω), uniformly in ω and n≥0.
