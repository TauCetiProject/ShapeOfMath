1. Hennion's quasi-compactness theorem for Doeblin–Fortet operators
   Let T be a bounded operator on a complex Banach space (L,‖·‖) with spectral radius ρ(T), and let |·| be a norm on L such that T is compact from (L,‖·‖) to (L,|·|). If for every n≥1 there are R_n,r_n≥0 with ‖Tⁿf‖≤R_n|f|+r_n‖f‖ for all f∈L, and r=liminf_n r_n^{1/n}<ρ(T), then T is quasi-compact and its essential spectral radius is at most r. This strengthens the Ionescu Tulcea–Marinescu theorem.
