LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Linear reflected Brownian BSDE on a usual filtration
   Let p>1 and let a usual filtration carry a d-dimensional Brownian motion W. Let xi∈Lp, an adapted càdlàg lower obstacle S satisfy sup S^+∈Lp and S_T≤xi, and a progressively measurable driver h satisfy ∫₀ᵀ|h_s|ds∈Lp. Then the reflected equation Y_t=xi−∫_t^Th_sds−∫_t^TZ_sdW_s−(M_T−M_t)+K_T−K_t has a unique càdlàg solution, with M orthogonal to W, K predictable nondecreasing, ∫(Y_-−S_-)dK=0, and sup|Y|, K_T, (∫|Z|²)^(1/2), [M]_T^(1/2) in Lp.
   Source: explains, Theorem2.12, Lemma2.7 and Cor2.8; additive-driver specialization plus Brownian projection
   Note: Klimsiak gives the scalar general-martingale reflected equation. Brownian projection is a further standard decomposition, not an assumed Brownian representation property. H5 holds by an integrable dominating martingale.
