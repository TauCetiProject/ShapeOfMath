LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Ramification of X₁(N)→X₀(N) at prime level
   Let N≥5 be prime and k an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from N. The Galois covering X₁(N)→X₀(N) over k, with group (ℤ/Nℤ)×/{±1}, is unramified at the cusps and at every point with j≠0,1728; it is ramified exactly at the points of X₀(N) with j=1728 when N≡1 mod 4, with inertia cyclic of order 2, and with j=0 when N≡1 mod 3, with inertia cyclic of order 3 (wildly ramified of first type in characteristics 2 and 3).
   Source: explains, Chapter II §2, Table 1 and its verification, pp. 64-66
   Note: Located statement and verification; originality not assessed.

2. Rational CM points of X₀(N) for the class-number-one primes
   For N∈{11,19,43,67,163}, the modular curve X₀(N) has a ℚ-rational noncuspidal point fixed by the Atkin–Lehner involution w_N; it is the elliptic curve with complex multiplication by the ring of integers of ℚ(√−N), of class number one, with the cyclic N-isogeny given by √−N. For N=19,43,67,163 it is the only noncuspidal ℚ-rational point of X₀(N).
   Source: explains, Proposition (2.5) and TABLE (column v), pp. 39–41, 146
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.
