LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Debut theorem for a progressively measurable set
   On a complete right-continuous filtration, the first entrance time into a progressively measurable subset of [0,∞)×Ω is a stopping time, allowing infinity when the set is empty.
   Source: explains, §2, Theorem 2.1
   Note: Elementary proof of the debut theorem; not an origin claim.

2. Optional section theorem
   For an optional set E on a usual filtered probability space and every epsilon>0, there is a stopping time tau whose graph on {tau<∞} is contained in E and P(tau<∞)≥P(π(E))−epsilon, where π(E) is its projection on the sample space, measured in the completed probability space.
   Source: explains, §3, Theorem 3.1
   Note: Proof of the optional section theorem as a corollary of the debut argument; not an origin claim.
