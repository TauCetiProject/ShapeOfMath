1. Stationary solution for a strongly monotone additive SPDE
   Let V⊂H⊂V* be a separable reflexive Gelfand triple and p>2. If a time-independent hemicontinuous drift M satisfies 2⟨M(u)−M(v),u−v⟩≤−c‖u−v‖ᵖ_V, coercivity 2⟨M(v),v⟩≤C−c′‖v‖ᵖ_V, and ‖M(v)‖_{V*}^{p/(p−1)}≤C(1+‖v‖ᵖ_V), then trace-class additive Wiener noise admits a strictly stationary variational solution with continuous H paths and locally Lᵖ(V) paths, obtainable as the pullback limit from fixed initial states.
