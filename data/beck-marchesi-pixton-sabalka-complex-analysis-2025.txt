LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Riemann’s removable singularity theorem
   A holomorphic function on a punctured neighborhood of a complex point extends holomorphically across that point if it is bounded near the point.
   Source: explains, Proposition 9.1.6(a), pp. 139–140
   Note: Proves the stronger criterion lim(z−z₀)f(z)=0, which boundedness implies. Not an origin claim.

2. Identity theorem for holomorphic functions
   Two holomorphic functions on a connected complex domain that agree on a set with an accumulation point inside the domain agree everywhere.
   Source: explains, Theorems 8.2.1–8.2.2, Corollary 8.2.3, pp. 125–127
   Note: Identity principle via classification of zeros and connectedness. Learning reference, not an origin claim.
