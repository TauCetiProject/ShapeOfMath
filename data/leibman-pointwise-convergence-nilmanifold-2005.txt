1. Reduction of a uniform nilpotent subgroup to a lattice
   If L is a connected simply connected nilpotent Lie group and Λ is closed and cocompact, then Λ⁰ is a closed connected normal subgroup of L, L/Λ⁰ is connected simply connected nilpotent, and Λ/Λ⁰ is a discrete cocompact subgroup of L/Λ⁰. The resulting compact homogeneous space is naturally L/Λ.
   Source: explains, §2.8, p. 4
