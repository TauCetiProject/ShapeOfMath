LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Formal exponential stays in an algebraic subgroup
   If F has characteristic0, G⊂GL_n is a closed algebraic F-subgroup and X∈Lie(G)(F), the formal matrix curve exp(tX)∈GL_n(F[[t]]) satisfies every defining equation of G, hence is an F[[t]]-point of G. For a complete valued F, its convergent specializations also lie in G(F).
   Source: explains, IV.2, Theorem 2.1 and its proof, pp. 47–48
   Note: J_H⊂J_τ for τ∈𝔏(H) and exp(tτ)=ε∘exp(tτ') is an algebra homomorphism to K[[t]]; the matrix form exp(tX) follows from Δx_ij=∑_l x_il⊗x_lj.
