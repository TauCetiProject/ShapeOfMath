1. Lifting of higher-order regionally proximal pairs through factor maps
   If π:X→Y is a factor map between minimal systems and d≥1, then (π×π)(RP^[d](X))=RP^[d](Y).
   Source: introduces, Theorem 3.8(2), p. 10; §6
   Note: Statement inspected; proof in §6 not audited line by line.
   Origin evidence: Abstract: ‘the lifting of RP^[d] between two minimal systems is obtained’; §3.4: ‘the lifting property of RP^[d] between two minimal systems is obtained in the paper. This result is new even for minimal distal systems.’

2. Cube criterion for the regionally proximal relation of order d
   For a minimal system (X,T) and d≥1, (x,y)∈RP^[d] exactly when (x,y,y,…,y)∈Q^[d+1](X); RP^[d] is a closed invariant equivalence relation.
   Source: explains, Theorem 3.2, p. 8
   Note: HKM proved the distal case; priority for the general minimal case not separately reviewed.

3. Factor closure of inverse limits of nilsystems
   For d≥1, every topological factor of a minimal compact metric inverse limit of d-step nilsystems is again an inverse limit of minimal d-step nilsystems.
   Source: explains, Proposition 3.7 and Theorem 3.9, pp. 9–10
   Note: Selected proof route for the root; not an origin claim.
