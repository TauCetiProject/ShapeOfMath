1. Duhamel formula for an inhomogeneous semigroup equation
   If T_t is a strongly continuous semigroup with generator A and u is a classical solution of u′=Au+f with suitably integrable continuous forcing, then u(t)=T_tu(0)+∫₀ᵗT_{t−s}f(s)ds. The integral is a Bochner integral.
