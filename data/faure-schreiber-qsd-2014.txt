5. Compact-uniform one-step large-deviation lower bound
   For kernels P_ε on a metric space and a finite action ρ(x,y) on an open set of starting states, the one-step lower LDP bound is compact-uniform if, for every compact K in that open set and every nonempty open ball B, liminf_(ε↓0)inf_(x∈K)[εlog P_ε(x,B)+inf_(y∈B)ρ(x,y)]≥0, with log0=−∞.

6. Transitive compact invariant set
   A compact invariant set K of a continuous map F is transitive in the dense-orbit sense if some x∈K has {Fⁿ(x):n≥0} dense in K.

7. Morse-attractor concentration of small-noise quasi-stationary laws
   Let F:S→S be continuous with bounded image on closed Euclidean S, with closed absorbing S₀ and invariant S₊=S∖S₀. Let QSDs μ_ε have kernels P_ε and survivals λ_ε. Suppose a finite continuous action ρ on S₊×S vanishes exactly at y=F(x), is uniformly positive off each fixed tracking tube, and satisfies compact-uniform lower LDP bounds. Require β_δ(ε)=sup_xP_ε(x,{y:d(y,F(x))≥δ})≤e^(−c_δ/ε) eventually, and, for every c>0, some neighborhood V of S₀ satisfies inf_(x∈V∩S₊)P_ε(x,S₀)≥e^(−c/ε) eventually. If a finite Morse decomposition separates S₀ from S₊, its non-attractor Morse sets in S₊ are transitive, and μ_ε charges every neighborhood of some positive attractor, every weak QSD limit is F-invariant and supported on the positive Morse attractors; λ_ε≥1−e^(−c/ε) eventually for some c>0.
   Source: explains, Theorem2.7/Remark2.8/Proposition5.1

10. Uniform deviation bounds for bounded nonlinear Poisson kernels
   Let F:ℝ_+ᵈ→ℝ_+ᵈ be continuous and bounded, preserving the boundary and its complement, and let P_ε be its independent-coordinate scaled Poisson kernel.
