1. Stable horospherical subgroup of a group element
   For an element a of a topological group G, the stable horospherical subgroup is G⁻_a={g∈G: aⁿga⁻ⁿ→e as n→∞}; it is a subgroup normalized by a. The unstable horospherical subgroup is G⁺_a=G⁻_(a⁻¹).
   Source: explains, §7.3, pp. 32–33
   Note: Selected learning reference; not a priority claim.

2. Horospherical maximal entropy principle
   Let G be a finite product of groups of real or p-adic points of connected reductive characteristic-zero algebraic groups, Γ a lattice, and a∈G semisimple. Put U={u∈G:aⁿua⁻ⁿ→e}. For every probability μ on Γ∖G invariant under x↦xa⁻¹, h_μ(a)≤−log|det(Ad(a)|Lie U)|, with the product of normalized local absolute values; equality holds exactly when μ is U-invariant.
   Source: explains, Theorem 7.9, §§7.42, 7.55, pp. 34, 48–50
   Note: Exposition and proof under the notes’ standing algebraic local-group setting; not a priority claim.
