LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Normalization of a discrete valuation ring in a finite separable extension
   Let A be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field K and L/K finite separable. Its integral closure B in L is a finite A-module and a Dedekind domain. The maximal ideals of B above the maximal ideal of A correspond to extensions of the valuation to L, up to positive normalization; localizing B at each such ideal gives the extension valuation ring.
   Source: explains, Lecture 5 Theorem 5.24; Lecture 8 Theorem 8.20
   Note: Inspected proof exposition for a general Dedekind base, specialized to a discrete valuation ring; not a priority claim.

2. Local characterization of Dedekind domains
   A Noetherian integral domain that is not a field is a Dedekind domain exactly when its localization at every nonzero prime ideal is a discrete valuation ring. In particular every discrete valuation ring is a Dedekind domain, and the localization of a Dedekind domain at a nonzero prime 𝔭 is the valuation ring of the discrete valuation given by the exponent of 𝔭 in ideal factorizations.
   Source: explains, Lecture 2 Proposition 2.9
   Note: Exposition of the local characterization (convention allowing fields); not a priority claim.
