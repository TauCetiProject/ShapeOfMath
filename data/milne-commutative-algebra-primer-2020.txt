LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Hilbert basis theorem
   If R is a noetherian commutative ring, then R[x] is noetherian. In particular a polynomial ring in finitely many variables over a field is noetherian.
   Source: explains, §3, Theorem 3.7
   Note: Exposition and proof; not a priority claim.

2. Zariski’s lemma for a field finitely generated as an algebra
   If a field extension K/k is finitely generated as a k-algebra, then K/k is finite algebraic.
   Source: explains, Theorem 13.1, pp.59–60
   Note: Zariski’s lemma with induction proof using integrality; inspected for cone-085.
