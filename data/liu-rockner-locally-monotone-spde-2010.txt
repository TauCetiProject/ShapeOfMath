1. Well-posedness of monotone evolution equations in a Gelfand triple
   Let V⊂H⊂V* be a separable reflexive Gelfand triple and p>1. Suppose measurable A(t,v) is hemicontinuous, 2⟨A(t,u)−A(t,v),u−v⟩≤C‖u−v‖²_H, 2⟨A(t,v),v⟩≤C‖v‖²_H−c‖v‖ᵖ_V+f(t), and ‖A(t,v)‖_{V*}^{p/(p−1)}≤C‖v‖ᵖ_V+f(t), with c>0 and f≥0 integrable. For x∈H, u′=A(t,u),u(0)=x has a unique solution u∈C([0,T];H)∩Lᵖ(0,T;V),u′∈L^{p/(p−1)}(0,T;V*).
   Source: explains, Theorem 1.1 and §2 (pp.3–8), specialized to ρ≡0, β=0, B≡0
