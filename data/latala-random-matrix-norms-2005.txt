1. Latała fourth-moment bound for an independent-entry matrix
   For a real random matrixX with independent mean-zero entries and finite fourth moments, E‖X‖_(op)≤C[max_i(∑_jEX_ij²)^(1/2)+max_j(∑_iEX_ij²)^(1/2)+(∑_(i,j)EX_ij⁴)^(1/4)], whereC is universal.
