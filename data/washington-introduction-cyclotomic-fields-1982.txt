LOCATED CONCEPTS (10)

1. Kummer’s Bernoulli criterion for the relative class number of ℚ(ζ_p)
   For an odd prime p, p divides the relative class number h⁻(p) of ℚ(ζ_p) if and only if p divides the numerator of one of the Bernoulli numbers B₂,B₄,…,B_(p−3), written in lowest terms.
   Source: explains, §5.3 Theorem 5.16, p.61
   Note: p-adic L-function proof.

2. Von Staudt–Clausen theorem
   For every k≥1, B_(2k)+∑1/p, summed over the primes p with (p−1) dividing 2k, is an integer. Hence the reduced denominator of B_(2k) is the squarefree product of those primes.
   Source: explains, §5.2 Theorem 5.10 with proof, pp.55–56
   Note: Proof via Proposition 4.1.

3. Kubota–Leopoldt p-adic L-function of an even Dirichlet character
   For an odd prime p and an even Dirichlet character χ there is a unique p-adic meromorphic function L_p(s,χ) on a disc containing ℤ_p, analytic except for a simple pole at s=1 when χ is trivial, with L_p(1−n,χ)=−(1−χω^(−n)(p)p^(n−1))B_(n,χω^(−n))/n for every integer n≥1. Here ω is the Teichmüller character and B_(n,ψ) are generalized Bernoulli numbers; for χ=ω^k this gives L_p(1−k,ω^k)=−(1−p^(k−1))B_k/k.
   Source: explains, §5.2 Theorem 5.11 with proof, pp.56–58
   Note: Washington’s construction of L_p(s,χ).

4. Bernoulli polynomials
   The Bernoulli polynomials B_n(x)∈ℚ[x] are defined by te^(xt)/(e^t−1)=∑_(n≥0)B_n(x)tⁿ/n!. Equivalently B_n(x)=∑_(j=0)^n C(n,j)B_j x^(n−j), so B_n(0)=B_n.
   Source: explains, Ch.4, p.31
   Note: Definition and binomial expansion.

5. Generalized Bernoulli numbers of a Dirichlet character
   For a Dirichlet character χ of conductor f, the generalized Bernoulli numbers B_(n,χ) are defined by ∑_(a=1)^f χ(a)te^(at)/(e^(ft)−1)=∑_(n≥0)B_(n,χ)tⁿ/n!. For every multiple F of f one has B_(n,χ)=F^(n−1)∑_(a=1)^F χ(a)B_n(a/F) with Bernoulli polynomials B_n(x). For the trivial character B_(n,1)=B_n for n≥2.
   Source: explains, Ch.4, pp.30–31, Proposition 4.1
   Note: Definition and distribution relation.

6. Conductor of a Dirichlet character
   The conductor f_χ of a Dirichlet character χ modulo n is the least positive divisor f of n such that χ is induced from a character modulo f, i.e. χ(a)=χ(b) whenever a≡b (mod f) and a,b are prime to n; the inducing character modulo f is primitive. With the usual convention, a product χψ of Dirichlet characters denotes the primitive character associated with a↦χ(a)ψ(a).
   Source: explains, Ch.3, pp.19–20
   Note: Conductor and multiplication of characters.

7. p-adic powers of principal units of ℤ_p
   Let p be an odd prime and u∈1+pℤ_p. For s∈ℤ_p the binomial series u^s=∑_(j≥0)C(s,j)(u−1)^j converges, agrees with integer powers and satisfies u^(s+t)=u^s u^t. As a function of s it is a power series ∑a_n sⁿ with |a_n|_p≤p^(−n(1−1/(p−1))), convergent on a disc containing ℤ_p. For a∈ℤ_p^× one writes ⟨a⟩=a/ω(a)∈1+pℤ_p, with ω(a) the Teichmüller lift of a mod p.
   Source: explains, §5.1, pp.52–54
   Note: ⟨a⟩^s via exp/log and binomial series, Proposition 5.8.

8. Analyticity of p-adic binomial-coefficient series with geometric decay
   Let p be prime and 0<r<p^(−1/(p−1)). If f(X)=∑_(n≥0)a_n·C(X,n) with a_n∈ℂ_p and |a_n|≤M·rⁿ for some M, then f is given by a power series ∑b_nXⁿ with |b_n|≤M·R^(−n), where R=(r·p^(1/(p−1)))^(−1)>1; thus f is analytic on the disc |X|<R, which contains ℤ_p.
   Source: explains, §5.1 Proposition 5.8 with proof, pp.53–54

9. Relative class number of a prime cyclotomic field
   For an odd prime p, the relative class number h⁻(p) is the quotient h(ℚ(ζ_p))/h(ℚ(ζ_p+ζ_p⁻¹)) of the class numbers of ℚ(ζ_p) and of its maximal real subfield. Kummer showed that it is a positive integer, the first factor of the class number.
   Source: explains, Ch.4 §4.2 Theorem 4.10 and the following sentence, pp.38–39
   Note: Defines h⁻=h/h⁺ for CM fields, after proving h⁺ | h.

10. Maximal real subfield of a cyclotomic field
   For n≥3 and a primitive nth root of unity ζ_n∈ℂ, the maximal real subfield of the cyclotomic field ℚ(ζ_n) is ℚ(ζ_n)⁺=ℚ(ζ_n)∩ℝ. It equals ℚ(ζ_n+ζ_n⁻¹) and is the fixed field of complex conjugation restricted to ℚ(ζ_n).
   Source: explains, Ch.1 Remark after Proposition 1.5, p.4; Ch.4 §4.2 p.38
   Note: Fixed field of complex conjugation, ℚ(ζ_p+ζ_p⁻¹), called the maximal real subfield.
