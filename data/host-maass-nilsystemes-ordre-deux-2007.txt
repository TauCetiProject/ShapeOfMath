1. Inverse-limit realization of a fixed-step nilsequence
   For d≥1, a bounded sequence is a d-step nilsequence exactly when it has the form F(T^n x), n∈Z, for a continuous complex function F on a minimal compact metric system that is an inverse limit of minimal d-step nilsystems.
