1. Perron splitting of a uniformly positive matrix cocycle
   Let θ be a homeomorphism of compact metric Ω and A:Ω→M_d(ℝ) continuous and nonnegative. Suppose some s≥1 makes A^(s)(ω)=A(θ^(s−1)ω)⋯A(ω) strictly positive for every ω. There are continuous positive unit vectors u(ω),v(ω) such that A(ω)u(ω) is a positive multiple of u(θω), A(ω)ker(v(ω)ᵀ)⊆ker(v(θω)ᵀ), and ℝᵈ=ℝu(ω)⊕ker(v(ω)ᵀ). Some C>0,0<α<1 give ‖A^(n)(ω)b‖≤Cαⁿ‖A^(n)(ω)u(ω)‖ for every unit b⊥v(ω), uniformly in ω and n≥0.
   Source: explains, Propositions8.13,8.15pp1304–1306

2. Common growth exponent for a stationary uniformly positive matrix product
   Let (Ω,θ,Q) be an ergodic probability-preserving compact metric system with θ a homeomorphism. Let A:Ω→M_d(ℝ) be continuous and nonnegative, with one fixed-length product A^(s)(ω)=A(θ^(s−1)ω)⋯A(ω) strictly positive for every ω. There is a finite deterministic r such that n⁻¹log‖A^(n)(ω)‖→r and n⁻¹log‖A^(n)(ω)z‖→r for Q-almost every ω and every nonzero z∈ℝ_+ᵈ.
   Source: explains, Propositions8.16–8.18pp1307–1309
   Note: Full selected direction/growth-observable proof inspected; stochastic limit follows Birkhoff.

3. Skew product of a discrete random dynamical system
   For a discrete random dynamical system φ over a measure-preserving map θ on Ω, the skew product is Φ(ω,x)=(θω,φ(1,ω,x)); its iterates are Φⁿ(ω,x)=(θⁿω,φ(n,ω,x)). A probability on Ω×X invariant under Φ and having environmental marginal Q is an invariant joint law; in standard Borel spaces it is equivalently Q(dω)π_ω(dx) for an invariant random probability kernel.

4. Weighted invasion criterion for structured-population persistence
   For a structured stochastic population map over an ergodic compact metric environmental system (Ω,θ,Q) with θ a homeomorphism, assume a compact forward-invariant population set K and continuous Aᵢ(ω,x) with fixed primitive zero patterns. Put K₀={x:some xᵢ=0}, Φ(ω,x)=(θω,(Aᵢ(ω,x)xᵢ)ᵢ), and rᵢ(μ)=∫limsup_n n⁻¹log‖Aᵢ^(n)(ω,x)‖dμ for Φ-invariant laws with marginal Q. If some pᵢ>0 give ∑ᵢpᵢrᵢ(μ)>0 at every such ergodic law supported on Ω×K₀, empirical stochastic persistence holds from every x∈K∖K₀ for Q-almost every environmental path.

5. Growth criterion for one structured population
   Consider one structured stochastic population on a compact forward-invariant K containing0, over an ergodic compact metric environmental homeomorphism, with continuous nonnegative matrices of a fixed primitive zero pattern. Let r be the common top exponent of A(ω,0). If r>0, the population is stochastically persistent. If r<0 and A(ω,x)≤A(ω,0) entrywise for all ω,x, every positive population trajectory tends to0 for almost every environmental path.
