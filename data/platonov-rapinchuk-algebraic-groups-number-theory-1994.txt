LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Analytic manifold of smooth p-adic variety points
   If V is a smooth algebraic variety over Q_p, V(Q_p) has a natural Q_p-analytic manifold structure, with tangent spaces identified with the rational algebraic tangent spaces. Polynomial morphisms induce analytic maps.
   Source: explains, §3.1, Proposition 3.1 and Lemma 3.1, pp. 110–113
   Note: Analytic structure on simple K-points and comparison of tangent spaces.

2. Strong approximation for simply connected groups
   Let G be a simply connected absolutely almost simple group over a number field F and S a finite set of places such that G_S=∏_(v∈S)G(F_v) is noncompact. Then G(F)G_S is dense in G(A_F); equivalently G(F) is dense in the S-adelic group G(A_F^S).
   Source: explains, Theorem 7.12 with proof, §7.4, pp.427–432
   Note: Textbook proof via reduction to the Kneser–Tits conjecture over local fields; origin credited by PR to Kneser and Platonov, not to this book.
