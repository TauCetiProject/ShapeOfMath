1. Gluing of BV functions along the reduced boundary of a set of finite perimeter
   Let u,v∈BV(Ω;ℝᵐ) and E a set of finite perimeter in Ω, with one-sided traces u⁺, v⁻ on ∂*E∩Ω (relative to the inner normal). Then w=u1_E+v1_{Ω∖E}∈BV(Ω;ℝᵐ) iff ∫_{∂*E∩Ω}|u⁺−v⁻|dH^{n−1}<∞, and then Dw=Du⌞E^{(1)}+(u⁺−v⁻)⊗ν H^{n−1}⌞(∂*E∩Ω)+Dv⌞E^{(0)}, ν the inner normal.
   Source: explains, Theorem 3.84 with proof
   Note: Statement and proof of the gluing formula (3.81); priority not assessed.

2. De Giorgi structure theorem for sets of finite perimeter
   For a set E of finite perimeter in ℝⁿ, the reduced boundary ∂*E is countably (n−1)-rectifiable and |D1_E|=H^{n−1}⌞∂*E, so D1_E=−ν_E H^{n−1}⌞∂*E and the perimeter is H^{n−1}(∂*E); at each x∈∂*E the blow-ups (E−x)/r converge locally in measure to the half space {y:y·ν_E(x)<0}.
   Source: explains, Theorem 3.59
   Note: Exposition of De Giorgi’s theorem; not its origin.

3. Reduced boundary and measure-theoretic normal of a set of finite perimeter
   For E⊆ℝⁿ of finite perimeter, the reduced boundary ∂*E consists of the points x with |D1_E|(B_r(x))>0 for all r>0 at which lim_{r→0}D1_E(B_r(x))/|D1_E|(B_r(x)) exists and is a unit vector. This limit is the inner unit normal; its negative ν_E(x) is the measure-theoretic outer unit normal.
   Source: explains, Definition 3.54
   Note: Textbook definition.
