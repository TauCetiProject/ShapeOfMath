1. Real Stone–Weierstrass theorem
   A unital real subalgebra of the continuous real functions on a compact Hausdorff space is uniformly dense if it separates points.

2. Riesz–Markov representation on a compact space
   Every positive linear functional on the continuous real functions on a compact Hausdorff space is integration against a unique finite regular Borel measure.
   Source: explains, Theorem24;Lemma29/Corollary30;Theorem40

3. Signed Riesz–Markov representation on a compact Hausdorff space
   Every bounded real linear functional on C(K), K compact Hausdorff, is integration against a unique finite signed regular Borel measure μ; its operator norm is |μ|(K).
