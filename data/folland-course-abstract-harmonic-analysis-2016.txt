1. Fourier inversion theorem for locally compact abelian groups
   For a locally compact abelian group G with Haar measure there is a Haar measure on the dual group Ĝ, the dual measure, such that whenever f∈L¹(G) and f̂∈L¹(Ĝ), f(x)=∫_Ĝ f̂(ξ)ξ(x)dξ for almost every x, and for every x if f is continuous.
   Source: explains, Theorems 4.22 and 4.33, pp. 105-111
