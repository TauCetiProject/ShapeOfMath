LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Janossy densities of a point process
   For a point process ξ on a space with reference measure λ and a bounded region A, the k-th Janossy density j_{A,k} is the symmetric density on Aᵏ, with respect to λᵏ, of the positions of the points of ξ in A on the event that ξ(A)=k. Normalizations differ by a factor k!.
   Source: explains, §1, p.2
   Note: Informal definition of Janossy densities with reference to Daley–Vere-Jones; not a priority claim.
