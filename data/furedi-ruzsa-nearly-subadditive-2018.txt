1. Fekete’s subadditive lemma
   If a real sequence satisfies a_{m+n}≤a_m+a_n for all m,n≥1, then a_n/n converges to inf_{n≥1}a_n/n, which may be −∞.
   Source: explains, §1, (3)–(4) and the standard proof, p. 2
