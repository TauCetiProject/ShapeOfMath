LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Maximum principle for a scalar linear parabolic equation
   A classical supersolution of w_t−∑a_ij(t,x)∂_ijw−∑b_i(t,x)∂_iw−c(t,x)w≥0 on a bounded smooth spatial domain stays nonnegative if its parabolic-boundary values are nonnegative, the symmetric matrix a is positive semidefinite, and the continuous coefficients are bounded on the time interval. An exponential time factor removes any positive upper bound on c.
   Source: explains, Lecture 1, Theorems 1.1.1–1.1.2 and Corollary 1.1.3
