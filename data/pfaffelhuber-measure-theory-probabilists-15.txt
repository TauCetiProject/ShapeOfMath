1. Countable products of Polish spaces
   A countable product of Polish spaces, equipped with the product topology, is Polish; its Borel sigma-algebra is generated by coordinate projections.
   Source: explains, Lemma 5.5 and proof
   Note: Borel σ-algebra equals product σ-algebra; not a priority claim.

2. Product σ-algebra
   For measurable spaces (E_i,ℰ_i), i∈I, the product σ-algebra ⊗ℰ_i on the Cartesian product ∏E_i is the σ-algebra generated by the coordinate preimages π_i⁻¹(A), i∈I, A∈ℰ_i; it is the smallest σ-algebra making every coordinate projection measurable.
   Source: explains, Definition 5.3
   Note: Learning reference.
