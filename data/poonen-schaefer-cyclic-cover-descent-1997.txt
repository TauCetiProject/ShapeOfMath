1. Poonen–Schaefer descent for Jacobians of cyclic covers of the projective line
   Let p be a prime, k a perfect field of characteristic not p containing a primitive p-th root of unity ζ, X the curve y^p=f(x) with Jacobian J, φ=1−ζ, and L=k[T]/(f₀) for the radical f₀ of f. If X has a k-rational divisor class of degree 1, the (x−T) map is a homomorphism J(k)/φJ(k)→L^×/L^×p k^× whose kernel has order 1 or p, and is trivial when f has a factor over k of degree prime to p. Over a number field its image lies in the kernel of the weighted norm to k^×/k^×p and is unramified outside any set of places containing the archimedean places, those above p and those of bad reduction of J.
   Source: introduces, (8), Proposition 5.1, Theorem 11.3, Propositions 12.1–12.4
   Note: Checked in the introduction and §5.
   Origin evidence: Checked in the introduction (pp.1–3): Cassels defined the map for genus-2 y²=f(x), Schaefer [17],[18] treated hyperelliptic curves of odd degree and y^p=f(x) with distinct roots and deg f prime to p. This paper proves the descent for y^p=f(x) with p | deg f and repeated roots, with the kernel and image descriptions (§§5, 9–12) recorded in this concept. The general theorem in this form originates here; the earlier special cases are credited to Cassels and Schaefer.
