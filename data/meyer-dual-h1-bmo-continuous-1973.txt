LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Davis decomposition of a càdlàg local martingale
   For a real càdlàg local martingale M starting at0, write S_t=sup_(s≤t)|ΔM_s|. After localization with E S_∞<∞, M=K+L with K a finite-variation martingale, E TV(K)_∞≤4 E S_∞, and L a local martingale with |ΔL_t|≤4S_(t−). The decomposition is used locally when the expectations are not globally finite.
   Source: explains, Appendix, p. 145
   Note: Continuous-time construction of the Davis decomposition; the discrete-time decomposition is Davis 1970.
