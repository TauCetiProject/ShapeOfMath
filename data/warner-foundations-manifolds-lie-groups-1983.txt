1. Lie group homomorphisms with bijective differential are coverings
   If φ:H→G is a smooth homomorphism of connected real Lie groups whose differential Lie(H)→Lie(G) is a linear isomorphism, then φ is surjective, its kernel is a discrete normal subgroup, and φ is a covering map.
