LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Choi positivity criterion for a matrix-domain map
   A linear map φ:M_n(ℂ)→A into a C*-algebra is completely positive exactly when the matrix [φ(E_ij)]_(i,j=1)^n is positive in M_n(A).
   Source: explains, Prop. 1.5.12
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.

2. Completely positive matrix approximation implies exactness
   If a C*-algebra A admits completely positive contractions φ_i:A→M_(n_i)(ℂ) and ψ_i:M_(n_i)(ℂ)→A with ψ_iφ_i(a)→a in norm for every a∈A, then A is exact: tensoring any short exact sequence of C*-algebras with A by the minimal tensor product preserves exactness.
   Source: explains, §3.7, Cor. 3.7.4
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.
