1. Serre’s lifting lemma for closed subgroups of SL₂(ℤ_p)
   Let p≥5 be prime and G a closed subgroup of SL₂(ℤ_p) whose reduction modulo p is all of SL₂(𝔽_p). Then G=SL₂(ℤ_p).
   Source: explains, Ch. IV §3.4, Lemma 3

2. Serre’s ℓ-adic openness theorem for elliptic curves without complex multiplication
   Let E be an elliptic curve over a number field K without complex multiplication over K̄. For every prime ℓ the image of Gal(K̄/K) in Aut(T_ℓE)≅GL₂(ℤ_ℓ) is open; equivalently its Lie algebra is all of End(V_ℓE). In particular this image is not abelian and V_ℓE is irreducible under every open subgroup of the Galois group.
   Source: explains, Ch. IV §2.2 Theorem (IV-11)
