- Bilateral Laplace inversion on an integrable vertical line: Letμ be a Borel probability onℝ andc∈ℝ with∫e^(cx)dμ(x)<∞. IfF(t)=∫e^((c+it)x)dμ(x) belongs toL¹(ℝ), thenμ has the continuous densityf(x)=e^(−cx)(2π)⁻¹∫_(−∞)^∞e^(−itx)F(t)dt.
- Convolution of probability distributions on Euclidean space: The convolution μ*ν is the law of X+Y for independent random vectors with laws μ,ν; equivalently (μ*ν)(A)=∫∫1_A(x+y)μ(dx)ν(dy).
- Gaussian process: A real or vector-valued stochastic process whose every finite vector of coordinate evaluations has a multivariate normal law.
- Gibbs partition function on a measure space: For a measured configuration space (X,μ), an energy V and inverse temperature β>0, the Gibbs partition function is Z(β)=∫_X e^(−βV(x))dμ(x), when finite and positive; the associated free energy is F(β)=−β⁻¹log Z(β).
- Krasovsky’s separated-bulk GUE characteristic-polynomial moment asymptotic: Let H_N have GUE density proportional to e^(−2NTr(H_N²)), and p_N(x)=det(xI−H_N). For fixed distinct x₁,…,x_k∈(−1,1) and fixed α_j with Re α_j>−1/2, E∏_j|p_N(x_j)|^(2α_j) equals (1+O(log N/N))∏_j[C(α_j)(1−x_j²)^(α_j²/2)(N/2)^(α_j²)e^((2x_j²−1−2log2)α_jN)]∏_(i<j)(2|x_i−x_j|)^(−2α_iα_j), where C(α)=2^(2α²)G(α+1)²/G(2α+1).
- Legendre duplication formula for the gamma function: For Re z>0, Γ(2z)=2^(2z−1)π^(−1/2)Γ(z)Γ(z+1/2), and the identity extends meromorphically wherever both sides are defined.
- Mellin transform of a positive random variable: For a positive random variable X, its Mellin transform is M_X(s)=E[X^(s−1)] on the complex strip where the expectation is absolutely integrable, with X^(s−1)=exp((s−1)log X).
- Selberg integral evaluation: For n≥2, real a,b>0 and γ>−min(1/n,a/(n−1),b/(n−1)), the integral over [0,1]^n of ∏_i x_i^(a−1)(1−x_i)^(b−1)∏_(i<j)|x_i−x_j|^(2γ) equals ∏_(j=0)^(n−1) Γ(a+jγ)Γ(b+jγ)Γ(1+(j+1)γ)/[Γ(a+b+(n+j−1)γ)Γ(1+γ)].
- Shift relation for the self-dual beta-deformed Barnes function: For β>0 and Re x>0, G_β(x+β)=β^(1/2−βx)(2π)^((β−1)/2)Γ(βx)G_β(x), with G_β fixed by its convergent normalized integral.
- Standard Gumbel distribution: The standard Gumbel distribution has cumulative distribution function P(G≤x)=exp(−exp(−x)) for real x.
- Trotter tridiagonal representation of GUE eigenvalues: Let H_N have GUE density proportional to exp(−2NtrH_N²). Its eigenvalue multiset has the same law as that of(2√(2N))⁻¹T_N, where T_N is real symmetric tridiagonal, its diagonal entries are independentN(0,2), its adjacent off-diagonal entries are independentχ_(2j),j=1,…,N−1, and all these random variables are mutually independent.

1. Fyodorov–Simm conjecture for the GUE characteristic-polynomial maximum
   For GUE density proportional to e^(−2NTr(H²)), set M_N=max_(x∈[−1,1]){2log|det(xI−H)|−2Elog|det(xI−H)|}. The conjecture predicts 2log N−(3/2)loglog N−M_N converges in distribution to Y with bilateral Laplace transform E[e^(sY)]=C⁻¹(2π)^sΓ(s+1)Γ(s+3)G(s+7/2)²/[G(s+6)G(s+1)], where C makes the transform equal 1 at s=0.
   Source: introduces, Conjecture 1.1; §2.2, (2.30)–(2.31); §3
   Note: Conjectural, with heuristic freezing and empirically calibrated shift K=2π; numerical agreement does not make it a theorem.
   Origin evidence: The introduction calls Conjecture 1.1 the paper’s main result and distinguishes it from earlier CUE laws [22,23,25]. Section 2 derives the GUE-specific Barnes transform and explicitly says K=2π is conjectured from numerical data. Earlier ingredients are separately credited: Krasovsky [35], Selberg [20], Barnes continuation/freezing [25,39–42]. This claim concerns the new GUE maximum-law conjecture only.

2. Gaussian unitary ensemble
   The Gaussian unitary ensemble of order N is the probability distribution on complex Hermitian N×N matrices with density proportional to exp(−c Tr(H²)) with respect to Lebesgue measure on their independent real coordinates, where c>0 fixes the variance convention. Here c=2N, so the limiting spectral support is [−1,1].
   Source: relies_on, §1, (1.1)
   Note: The source assumes the standard ensemble, with a declared variance convention.

3. Krasovsky’s separated-bulk GUE characteristic-polynomial moment asymptotic
   Let H_N have GUE density proportional to e^(−2NTr(H_N²)), and p_N(x)=det(xI−H_N). For fixed distinct x₁,…,x_k∈(−1,1) and fixed α_j with Re α_j>−1/2, E∏_j|p_N(x_j)|^(2α_j) equals (1+O(log N/N))∏_j[C(α_j)(1−x_j²)^(α_j²/2)(N/2)^(α_j²)e^((2x_j²−1−2log2)α_jN)]∏_(i<j)(2|x_i−x_j|)^(−2α_iα_j), where C(α)=2^(2α²)G(α+1)²/G(2α+1).
   Source: relies_on, §1, (1.14)–(1.16); §2, (2.3)–(2.5)
   Note: The rigorous separated-point asymptotic is an imported input, credited to [35]; the near-collision cutoff remains heuristic here.

4. Selberg integral evaluation
   For n≥2, real a,b>0 and γ>−min(1/n,a/(n−1),b/(n−1)), the integral over [0,1]^n of ∏_i x_i^(a−1)(1−x_i)^(b−1)∏_(i<j)|x_i−x_j|^(2γ) equals ∏_(j=0)^(n−1) Γ(a+jγ)Γ(b+jγ)Γ(1+(j+1)γ)/[Γ(a+b+(n+j−1)γ)Γ(1+γ)].
   Source: relies_on, §2, (2.6)–(2.8)
   Note: Exact Selberg evaluation is used to compute integer moments.

5. Legendre duplication formula for the gamma function
   For Re z>0, Γ(2z)=2^(2z−1)π^(−1/2)Γ(z)Γ(z+1/2), and the identity extends meromorphically wherever both sides are defined.
   Source: relies_on, §2.1, (2.13)
   Note: Used to reorganize the moment recursion.

6. Barnes G-function
   The Barnes G-function is the canonical entire function given by G(z+1)=(2π)^(z/2)exp(−(z+z²(1+γ))/2)∏_(n≥1)[(1+z/n)^n exp(z²/(2n)−z)], where γ=lim_(n→∞)(∑_(k=1)^n1/k−log n). It satisfies G(1)=1 and G(z+1)=Γ(z)G(z); the recurrence alone does not specify its canonical normalization.
   Source: relies_on, §2.1; Conjecture 1.1
   Note: Barnes functions encode the analytic continuation and proposed transform.

7. Mellin transform of a positive random variable
   For a positive random variable X, its Mellin transform is M_X(s)=E[X^(s−1)] on the complex strip where the expectation is absolutely integrable, with X^(s−1)=exp((s−1)log X).
   Source: relies_on, §2.1, (2.9)–(2.23)
   Note: Complex moment continuation is phrased through Mellin transforms.

8. Bilateral Laplace transform of a real probability distribution
   For a real random variable Y, its bilateral Laplace transform is L_Y(s)=E[e^(sY)] on the complex strip where this expectation converges absolutely.
   Source: relies_on, §2.2; §3
   Note: The proposed extremal law is transformed and numerically inverted.

9. Gibbs partition function on a measure space
   For a measured configuration space (X,μ), an energy V and inverse temperature β>0, the Gibbs partition function is Z(β)=∫_X e^(−βV(x))dμ(x), when finite and positive; the associated free energy is F(β)=−β⁻¹log Z(β).
   Source: relies_on, §2, (2.1)–(2.2)
   Note: The logarithm is treated as a random energy landscape.

10. Gaussian process
   A real or vector-valued stochastic process whose every finite vector of coordinate evaluations has a multivariate normal law.
   Source: relies_on, §1, (1.8)–(1.11)
   Note: Logarithmic correlations motivate the freezing argument; singular limiting fields are not ordinary pointwise Gaussian processes.

11. Standard Gumbel distribution
   The standard Gumbel distribution has cumulative distribution function P(G≤x)=exp(−exp(−x)) for real x.
   Source: relies_on, Remark 1.2
   Note: The proposed Gumbel-plus-independent-shift decomposition is part of the conjectural law.

12. Convolution of probability distributions on Euclidean space
   The convolution μ*ν is the law of X+Y for independent random vectors with laws μ,ν; equivalently (μ*ν)(A)=∫∫1_A(x+y)μ(dx)ν(dy).
   Source: relies_on, Remark 1.2, (1.6)–(1.7)
   Note: Independent sums correspond to convolution of laws.

13. Shift relation for the self-dual beta-deformed Barnes function
   For β>0 and Re x>0, G_β(x+β)=β^(1/2−βx)(2π)^((β−1)/2)Γ(βx)G_β(x), with G_β fixed by its convergent normalized integral.
