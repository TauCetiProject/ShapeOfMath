1. Bloch–Kato comparison for abelian-variety Selmer groups
   For an abelian variety over a number field, the Bloch–Kato finite local conditions on its rational Tate realization agree with the rationalized local Kummer conditions. In particular decomposing a modular abelian Tate realization into newform critical realizations decomposes its rational Selmer group; finite lattice discrepancies do not affect finiteness of the primary Selmer group.
