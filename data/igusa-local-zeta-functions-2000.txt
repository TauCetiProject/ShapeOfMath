1. Change of variables for a p-adic analytic diffeomorphism
   For an analytic diffeomorphism f:U→V between open subsets of Q_p^d and a nonnegative Borel function h, ∫_Vh(y)dy=∫_Uh(f(x))|detDf(x)|_pdx, with product additive Haar measures; the same holds for integrable signed or complex h.
   Source: explains, §7.4, Proposition 7.4.1, pp. 111–112
   Note: Proof of the change-of-variables formula dy=|∂y/∂x|_K dx for K-bianalytic maps.

2. Integral implicit function theorem over a complete nonarchimedean field
   Let K be complete for a discrete nonarchimedean absolute value, with valuation ring O and uniformizer π. If F∈O[[x,y]]^m, F(0,0)=0 and det(∂F/∂y)(0,0) is a unit of O, the formal solution y=f(x) of F(x,f(x))=0 with f(0)=0 has coefficients in O. If every F_i is a special restricted power series (degree-d coefficients divisible by π^(d−1), no constant term), so is every f_i; f converges on O^n and gives all solutions in O^n×O^m. In particular such a map O^n→O^n with unit Jacobian at 0 has an inverse of the same form.
   Source: explains, §2.2, Theorem 2.2.1 and Corollary 2.2.1, pp. 23–24
   Note: Statement and proof; originality not assessed.
