LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Standard form of a von Neumann algebra
   A standard form (M,H,J,P) of a von Neumann algebra M on H consists of a conjugate-linear isometric involution J of H and a self-dual cone P⊆H with JMJ=M′, JcJ=c* for every c in the center of M, Jξ=ξ for ξ∈P, and aJaJ(P)⊆P for a∈M. Every von Neumann algebra has a standard form, unique up to unitary equivalence (Haagerup); H is then written L²(M), with commuting right action ξ·a=Ja*Jξ.
   Source: explains, Introduction, p. 271
   Note: Haagerup’s defining conditions 1–4 and uniqueness. Recorded as an explanation only; originality not assessed here.

2. Self-dual cone in a Hilbert space
   For a subset P of a complex Hilbert space H, the dual cone is P°={ξ∈H : ⟨ξ,η⟩≥0 for every η∈P}. P is a self-dual cone if P=P°; such a P is a closed convex cone.
   Source: explains, §1, p. 272
   Note: Definition of dual and self-dual cones. Not a priority claim.
