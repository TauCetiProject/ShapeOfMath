LOCATED CONCEPTS (5)

1. Norris’s lemma for continuous semimartingales
   Let Z_t=Z₀+∫₀ᵗA_sds+∫₀ᵗB_sdW_s on[0,1], with adapted A,B whose1/3-Hölder norms have every finite moment. There is r>0 such that for every p>0, P(‖Z‖∞≤ε and max(‖A‖∞,‖B‖∞)>εʳ)≤C_pεᵖ for0<ε≤1.
   Source: explains, Lemma 6.28 with proof, pp.63–64
   Note: Hairer’s version of Norris’s lemma, crediting Norris 1986; not a priority claim.

2. Exponential martingale inequality for Itô integrals
   If M_t=∫₀ᵗu_s dW_s for an adapted process u that is locally square integrable and W is a Wiener process, then for all x,y>0, P(sup_t M_t≥x and ∫₀^∞|u_s|²ds≤y)≤exp(−x²/(2y)).
   Source: explains, §4, derivation of (4.15), p.38
   Note: Derivation from the exponential supermartingale; not a priority claim.

3. Malliavin criterion for a smooth density
   If an ℝⁿ-valued Wiener functional F is Malliavin smooth and its Malliavin covariance Γ is invertible almost surely with E‖Γ⁻¹‖ᵖ<∞ for every p>1, then its law has a C∞ Lebesgue density.
   Source: explains, Theorem 6.7 with Lemmas 6.3, 6.8, pp. 53–55
   Note: Exposition and proof; not a priority claim.

4. Small-ball estimate for the reduced Hörmander covariance
   For bounded smooth Stratonovich fields satisfying the parabolic Hörmander condition, with flow Jacobian J and noise matrix V, C_t=∫₀ᵗJ_s⁻¹V(X_s)V(X_s)ᵀ(J_s⁻¹)ᵀds satisfies sup_{|η|=1}P(ηᵀC_tη<ε)≤C_{p,t,x}εᵖ for every p>0,t>0 and initial point x.
   Source: explains, Theorem 6.24 and proof, pp. 62–65
   Note: Exposition and proof; not a priority claim.

5. Malliavin smooth Wiener functional
   A Wiener functional is Malliavin smooth if it belongs to D^{k,p} for every integer k≥0 and every1<p<∞; here D^{k,p} is the completion of smooth cylindrical functionals in the Lᵖ norms of the functional and its first k Malliavin derivatives.
   Source: explains, §5.2, pp. 48–49
   Note: Definition of Malliavin smooth random variables; not a priority claim.
