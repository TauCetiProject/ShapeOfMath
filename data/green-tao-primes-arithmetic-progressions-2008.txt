LOCATED CONCEPTS (4)

1. Green–Tao theorem
   The prime numbers contain nonconstant arithmetic progressions of every finite length.
   Source: introduces, Abstract and Theorem 1.1, p.1–2
   Note: Main theorem of the paper.
   Origin evidence: The abstract states 'We prove that there are arbitrarily long arithmetic progressions of primes' and §1 describes the result as a classical conjecture not previously established; Theorem 1.1 is the first proof.

2. Green–Tao relative Szemerédi theorem
   For k≥3 and 0<δ≤1, if ν is a k-pseudorandom measure on ℤ/Nℤ and 0≤f≤ν with average at least δ, then the average of f(x)f(x+r)⋯f(x+(k−1)r) over x,r∈ℤ/Nℤ is at least c(k,δ)−o(1), where c(k,δ)>0 is the constant in the Varnavides form of Szemerédi's theorem.
   Source: introduces, Theorem 3.5, p.9
   Note: Transference principle.
   Origin evidence: The abstract calls the transference principle 'the main new ingredient of this paper', and §3 says Theorem 3.5 is 'one of the main theorems of the paper'.

3. k-pseudorandom measure on ℤ/Nℤ (Green–Tao)
   For fixed k≥3, a family of functions ν=ν_N:ℤ/Nℤ→[0,∞) with average 1+o(1) as N→∞ is k-pseudorandom if it satisfies Green–Tao's (k·2^(k−1),3k−4,k)-linear forms condition, that averages of products of ν along systems of pairwise non-parallel affine-linear forms are 1+o(1), and their 2^(k−1)-correlation condition, bounding correlations of shifts of ν by a weight with bounded moments.
   Source: introduces, Definitions 3.1–3.3, pp.7–9
   Note: Linear forms and correlation conditions.
   Origin evidence: Definitions 3.1–3.3 introduce the linear forms condition, correlation condition and k-pseudorandom measures as the paper's own hypotheses for the transference principle; no earlier source is credited for them.

4. Green–Tao pseudorandom majorant of the W-tricked primes
   With W the product of the primes up to a slowly growing w(N) and the W-tricked von Mangoldt function Λ̃(n)=(φ(W)/W)log(Wn+1) when Wn+1 is prime and 0 otherwise, for every k≥3 and every large prime N there is a k-pseudorandom measure ν on ℤ/Nℤ, built from Goldston–Yıldırım truncated divisor sums, with ν(n)≥k^(−1)2^(−k−5)Λ̃(n) for ε_kN≤n≤2ε_kN, where ε_k=1/(2^k(k+4)!).
   Source: introduces, Proposition 9.1, p.36
   Note: Uses Goldston–Yıldırım truncated divisor sums, credited to them, inside a new majorant.
   Origin evidence: Proposition 9.1 asserts k-pseudorandomness, a notion defined in this paper, for the majorant built in Definition 9.3 from the W-trick; the paper credits Goldston–Yıldırım only for the correlation estimates (Propositions 9.5–9.6).
