1. Geometric ergodicity from a half-line Lyapunov drift and positive transitions
   Let a nonexplosive càdlàg weak Feller strong Markov family on[0,∞), with usual filtration, have positive transition probabilities to every Lebesgue-positive Borel set at every positive time. Suppose a continuous V≥1, k>0, b<∞ and compact C satisfy that V(X_t)−V(X₀)−∫₀ᵗ[−kV(X_s)+b1_C(X_s)]ds is a local supermartingale from every initial state. Then there is a unique invariant probability pi with pi(V)<∞ and constants D,rho>0 such that ‖P_t(x,·)−pi‖V≤D V(x)e^(−rho t) for every x,t. No equality rho=k or special explicit prefactor is asserted.
