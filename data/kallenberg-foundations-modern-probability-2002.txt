1. Continuity theorem for Laplace transforms of nonnegative random variables
   If nonnegative random variables X_n satisfy E e^{−αX_n}→E e^{−αX} for every α>0, where X is a nonnegative random variable, then X_n converges to X in distribution.
   Source: explains, Theorem 5.3 (Laplace transform statement), p. 86
   Note: Textbook statement and proof route; not a priority claim.

2. Finite speed measure and mixing of a one-dimensional diffusion
   For a conservative irreducible regular diffusion on an interval with interior generator½f″+qf′ and instantaneous reflection at any regular included endpoint, the speed density is2exp(2∫q). If its total mass is finite, its normalization is the unique invariant probability measure and transition laws converge to it in total variation. Entrance or inaccessible endpoints contribute no boundary holding atom.
   Source: explains, Theorem 23.15 and Lemmas 23.16–23.19, pp. 465–468
   Note: ν-ergodicity of regular diffusions on natural scale with bounded speed measure. Not a priority claim.
