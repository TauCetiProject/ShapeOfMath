LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Trace theorem for W¹,ᵖ on bounded Lipschitz domains
   For a bounded Lipschitz domain U⊂ℝⁿ and 1≤p<∞ there is a bounded linear operator T:W¹,ᵖ(U)→Lᵖ(∂U;Hⁿ⁻¹) with Tf=f|∂U for every f∈W¹,ᵖ(U)∩C(Ū). Tf is the trace of f; boundary values f=g on part of ∂U mean Tf=Tg Hⁿ⁻¹-almost everywhere there.
   Source: explains, Theorem 4.6 and Definition 4.5, pp. 156–158
   Note: Exposition and proof of the trace theorem for bounded Lipschitz domains; not a priority claim.

2. Density of functions smooth up to the boundary in W¹,ᵖ of a Lipschitz domain
   If U⊂ℝⁿ is bounded with Lipschitz boundary and 1≤p<∞, every f∈W¹,ᵖ(U) is a W¹,ᵖ-limit of functions in W¹,ᵖ(U)∩C^∞(Ū); if also f∈C(Ū), the approximants can be chosen to converge uniformly on Ū.
   Source: explains, Theorem 4.3, pp. 151–153
   Note: Global approximation by functions smooth up to the boundary; not a priority claim.
