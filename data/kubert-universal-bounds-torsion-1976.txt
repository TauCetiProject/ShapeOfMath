LOCATED CONCEPTS (4)

1. Kubert reduction of rational torsion to large prime order
   If E/ℚ has no rational torsion point of prime order at least23, then E(ℚ)_tors is one of C_m for1≤m≤10 or m=12, or C₂×C_(2v) for1≤v≤4. This is the earlier small/composite-order reduction, before excluding large-prime torsion.
   Source: explains, Main result 1 (p. 195); Theorem IV.1.2 (p. 216)
   Note: Kubert's own theorem; recorded only as the inspected proof (introduction evidence not reviewed).

2. Rational points of the genus-one modular curves X₀(N)
   For N∈{11,14,15,17,19,20,21,24,27,32,36,49}, the modular curve X₀(N) has genus one and its finitely many ℚ-rational points are explicitly known (Ligozat). Consequently no elliptic curve over ℚ has a ℚ-rational point of order N for these N, and none has a ℚ-rational cyclic subgroup of order 20, 24, 32, 36 or 49.
   Source: explains, p. 216–217 (citing Ligozat)
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.

3. Nonexistence of rational cyclic subgroups of order 35 on elliptic curves over ℚ
   No elliptic curve over ℚ has a Galois-stable cyclic subgroup of order 35: every ℚ-rational point of X₀(35) is a cusp. In particular no elliptic curve over ℚ has a ℚ-rational point of order 35.
   Source: explains, Prop. IV.3.5, Cor. IV.3.6
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.

4. Nonexistence of rational points of order 18 or 25 on elliptic curves over ℚ
   No elliptic curve over ℚ has a ℚ-rational point of order 18 or of order 25: the ℚ-rational points of X₁(18) and X₁(25) are cusps (Kubert, via descent on their Jacobians).
   Source: explains, Theorem IV.5.19
   Note: Selected proof reference; not a priority claim.
