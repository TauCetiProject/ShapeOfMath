LOCATED CONCEPTS (3)

1. Neumann series in a unital Banach algebra
   If a lies in a unital Banach algebra and ||a||<1, then1−a is invertible and (1−a)^(−1)=Σ_(n≥0)a^n in norm.
   Source: explains, Proposition 2.1.20, p.13
   Note: Statement in the form ‖x−1‖<1 ⇒ x invertible with x⁻¹=∑(1−x)ⁿ, with proof; inspected for cone-085.

2. Gelfand–Mazur theorem
   Every nonzero complex unital Banach algebra in which every nonzero element is invertible consists precisely of the scalar multiples of its identity, so the scalar map C→A, λ↦λ1, is an algebra isomorphism. It is isometric when ||1||=1.
   Source: explains, Corollary 2.3.10, p.33
   Note: Gelfand–Mazur for Banach division algebras with short proof from Proposition 2.3.9; inspected for cone-085.

3. Norm criterion for positivity of a self-adjoint C*-algebra element
   Let a be a self-adjoint element of a unital C*-algebra and t≥‖a‖. Then the spectrum of a lies in [0,∞) exactly when ‖t1−a‖≤t; if this inequality holds for one t≥‖a‖ it holds for all.
   Source: explains, Proposition 4.1.6(iv) and proof, pp. 87–88
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.
