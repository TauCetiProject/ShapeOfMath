1. John ellipsoid theorem
   For every convex bodyK⊂ℝⁿ, an affine change of coordinates putsK between the unit Euclidean ball and then-fold dilate of that ball.
   Source: explains, Theorem 10.12.2, pp. 588–589
   Note: Selected proof exposition (following Ball); attributes the consequence to John. Not an origin claim.

2. John’s characterization of the maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid
   For a convex body K⊂ℝⁿ containing the closed unit ball B, B is the maximal-volume ellipsoid in K if and only if there are points u₁,…,u_m on the boundaries of both B and K and weights c_i>0 with ∑c_iu_i=0 and ∑c_i⟨x,u_i⟩²=|x|² for every x∈ℝⁿ.
   Source: explains, Theorem 10.12.1, pp. 587–588
   Note: Statement located; necessity credited to John (1948). Proof not given there.
