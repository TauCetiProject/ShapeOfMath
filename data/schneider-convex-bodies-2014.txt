LOCATED CONCEPTS (5)

1. John ellipsoid theorem
   For every convex bodyK⊂ℝⁿ, an affine change of coordinates putsK between the unit Euclidean ball and then-fold dilate of that ball.
   Source: explains, Theorem 10.12.2, pp. 588–589
   Note: Selected proof exposition (following Ball); attributes the consequence to John. Not an origin claim.

2. John’s characterization of the maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid
   For a convex body K⊂ℝⁿ containing the closed unit ball B, B is the maximal-volume ellipsoid in K if and only if there are points u₁,…,u_m on the boundaries of both B and K and weights c_i>0 with ∑c_iu_i=0 and ∑c_i⟨x,u_i⟩²=|x|² for every x∈ℝⁿ.
   Source: explains, Theorem 10.12.1, pp. 587–588
   Note: Statement located; necessity credited to John (1948). Proof not given there.

3. Approximation of convex bodies by strongly isomorphic simple polytopes
   For nonempty compact convex sets K₁,…,K_m⊂ℝⁿ and ε>0 there are simple polytopes P₁,…,P_m with Hausdorff distance d_H(K_i,P_i)<ε that are strongly isomorphic: for every unit vector u their faces with outer normal u all have the same dimension.
   Source: explains, Theorem 2.4.15 and proof, p. 111
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.

4. Simple polytope
   An n-dimensional convex polytope in ℝⁿ is simple if each of its vertices (0-dimensional faces) lies in exactly n facets ((n−1)-dimensional faces).
   Source: explains, §2.4, p. 109
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.

5. Strongly isomorphic convex polytopes
   Convex polytopes P,Q⊂ℝⁿ are strongly isomorphic if dim F(P,u)=dim F(Q,u) for every unit vector u, where F(P,u)=P∩{x:⟨x,u⟩=h(P,u)} is the face of P with outer normal u. Strongly isomorphic polytopes are combinatorially isomorphic.
   Source: explains, §2.4, p. 109
   Note: Selected proof or learning reference for cone task r2-053; not a priority claim.
