1. Legendre formula for the prime valuation of a factorial
   For a prime p and integer n≥0, v_p(n!)=∑_(j≥1)⌊n/p^j⌋; the sum has only finitely many nonzero terms.
   Source: explains, Example 3.11, (3.2), pp. 11–12
