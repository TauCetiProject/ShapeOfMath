LOCATED CONCEPTS (2)

1. Symplectic vector space
   A symplectic vector space (V,ω) over a field F is a vector space V with a bilinear form ω:V×V→F that is alternating, ω(v,v)=0 for every v (so ω(u,v)=−ω(v,u)), and nondegenerate.
   Source: explains, §1.2, Definition 1.3, p.4
   Note: Exposition over R (skew-symmetric = alternating there); not a priority claim.

2. Dimension bound for an isotropic symplectic subspace
   For a finite-dimensional symplectic vector space (V,ω), a subspace W satisfying ω|_(W×W)=0 has dimension at most half the dimension of V.
   Source: explains, Homework 1, problems 1 and 5 with hint, p.8
   Note: Exercise with hinted proof; not a priority claim.
