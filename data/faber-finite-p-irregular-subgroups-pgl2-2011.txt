LOCATED CONCEPTS (1)

1. Prime-to-p finite subgroups of PGL₂ over a prime field
   Let p be prime and H⊆PGL₂(𝔽_p) have order coprime to p. Then H is cyclic, dihedral, or isomorphic to A₄, S₄ or A₅. The alternatives need not all occur over a given prime field.
