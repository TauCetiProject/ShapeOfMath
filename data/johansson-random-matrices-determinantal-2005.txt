1. Andréief integration formula
   For a measure space (I,μ) and functions f_0,…,f_{N−1},φ_0,…,φ_{N−1} with every f_jφ_k integrable, ∫_{I^N}det[f_j(x_k)]det[φ_j(x_k)]dμ(x_1)⋯dμ(x_N)=N!det[∫_If_jφ_kdμ]_{j,k=0}^{N−1}.
   Source: explains, Proposition 2.10 and proof, pp. 9–10
   Note: Statement on a general measure space with a complete proof; not an origin claim (Andréief 1886).
