1. Hille–Yosida theorem for contraction semigroups
   A densely defined operator A on a Banach space generates a strongly continuous contraction semigroup exactly when it is closed, every λ>0 belongs to its resolvent, and ‖(λI−A)^{-n}‖≤λ^{-n} for every integer n≥1. Its resolvent is the Laplace transform∫₀∞e^{-λt}T_tdt.
   Source: explains, Theorem 1.9 and proof, pp. 5–6
   Note: Contraction-semigroup Hille–Yosida theorem with Yosida-approximation proof; exposition, not a priority claim.

2. Strongly continuous semigroup of bounded operators
   A family (T_t)_{t≥0} of bounded linear operators on a Banach space X with T₀=I, T_{s+t}=T_sT_t for all s,t≥0, and ‖T_tx−x‖→0 as t↓0 for every x∈X; also called a C₀-semigroup.
   Source: explains, Definition 1.1, p. 1
   Note: Definition; not a priority claim.

3. Generator of a strongly continuous semigroup
   For a strongly continuous semigroup (T_t) on a Banach space X, the generator is the linear operator Ax=lim_{t↓0}(T_tx−x)/t, defined on the subspace D(A) of those x∈X for which this norm limit exists.
   Source: explains, Definition after 1.2 and Theorem 1.3, pp. 1–2
   Note: Definition and density/closedness of the generator; not a priority claim.
