1. Etemadi's strong law for random arrays
   For an r-indexed pairwise independent identically distributed real array with E[abs(X)(log⁺abs(X))ʳ⁻¹]<∞, its rectangular averages converge almost surely to EX as every side length tends to infinity.
   Source: explains, Corollary 4.2 (1)⇒(2) via Theorem 4.1, pp. 155-157
   Note: Proves the d-dimensional pairwise independent law; not a priority claim (Etemadi 1981 Remark 1 states the extension).
